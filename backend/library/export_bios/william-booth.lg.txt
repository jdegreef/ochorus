William Booth yazaalibwa nga 10 Apuli 1829 mu Sneinton, ku lubalama lwa Nottingham, mutabani wa Samuel Booth ne Mary Moss. Eby'obusuubuzi bwa kitaawe bwe byagwa, William ng'aweza emyaka kkumi n'esatu yaweebwa okuyigirira ewa nannyini katale k'ebbanja, era eyo n'ayiga obwavu bwe bukola ku muntu. Mu 1844 yakyusibwa, n'amalirira nti Katonda afune ye yenna: “Katonda ajja kufuna byonna ebiri mu William Booth.”

Yatandika okubuulira mu bukubo obw'abaavu obwa Nottingham, oluvannyuma n'asengukira e London. Mu 1852 yafuuka omusumba ow'ekiseera kyonna, era nga 17 Juuni 1855 n'awasa Catherine Mumford, eyafuuka mukozi munne; baazaala abaana munaana. Mu 1865, ng'abuulira mu weema e Whitechapel mu buvanjuba bwa London, yazuula omulimu gw'obulamu bwe n'atandikawo Obuweereza obw'Ekikristaayo, obwafuuka Eggye ery'Obulokozi mu 1878, ye nga ye Jenerali waalyo.

Okusaba kwe kwali yingini y'omulimu gwonna, era yagamba abakungu be: “Kola nga buli kimu bwe kyesigamye ku mulimu gwo, era saba nga buli kimu bwe kyesigamye ku kusaba kwo.” Mu 1890 yafulumya In Darkest England and the Way Out, ekyateekawo omulimu omunene ogw'Eggye eri abaavu, era yawandiika n'ebitabo ebitono nga Purity of Heart. Wadde nga yayolekera okuziyizibwa n'okuvumibwa, n'okufa kwa Catherine mu 1890, yasigala anywevu.

Yafa nga 20 Agusito 1912 mu Hadley Wood, ng'aweza emyaka kinaana mu esatu, era abantu emitwalo ana baaliwo mu maziiko ge. Yaziikibwa ku lubalama lwa Catherine mu Malaalo ga Abney Park. Ku nkomerero y'obulamu bwe Eggye lyali likola mu mawanga amakumi ataano mu munaana, era likyalwanirira okukakasa kwe nti enjiri erina okukyusa emitima n'ebitundu byombi.
