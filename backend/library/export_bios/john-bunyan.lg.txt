John Bunyan yazaalibwa mu 1628 mu Harrowden okumpi ne Bedford, era n'akwata omulimu gwa kitaawe ogw'okuddaabiriza entamu. Mu 1644, ng'aweza emyaka kkumi na mukaaga, yayingizibwa mu ggye lya Palamenti. Bwe yakomawo yawasa, era mukyala we n'aleeta ebitabo bibiri kitaawe bye yamulekera. Okuwuliriza abakazi abaavu mu Bedford nga boogera ku kuzaalibwa okuggya kwamutwala eri omusumba John Gifford, era mu 1653 yeegatta ku kkuŋŋaaniro ery'e Bedford.

Okumala emyaka yabonyaabonyezebwa okutya nti yali akoze ekibi ekitasonyiyibwa, okutuusa olunaku lumu mu nnimiro ekigambo bwe kyamugwako nti obutuukirivu bwe buli mu ggulu. Yalaba nti obutuukirivu bwe yali Yesu Kristo yennyini, era enjegere ze ne zigwa ku magulu ge.

Obwakabaka bwe bwazzibwawo mu 1660, okubuulira ebweru wa Kkanisa ya Bungereza ne kufuuka musango, era mu November 1660 Bunyan yakwatibwa. Yamala emyaka kkumi n'ebiri mu kkomera ly'e Bedford kubanga teyakkiriza kulekera awo kubuulira: “singa nnali nfulumye mu kkomera leero, nnandibuulidde Enjiri nate enkya, ku bw'obuyambi bwa Katonda.” Eyo yawandiika Grace Abounding to the Chief of Sinners (1666), era yatandika The Pilgrim’s Progress, ekyafulumizibwa mu 1678 ne kibuna wonna.

Yateebwa mu 1672, n'aweereza ng'omusumba w'ekkuŋŋaaniro ery'e Bedford, abantu ne bamutuuma Bisoopu Bunyan. Mu August 1688 yagenda e Reading okutabaganya kitaawe w'omuvubuka n'omwana we, oluvannyuma n'atuuka e London ng'atobye amazzi. Yafa nga August 31, 1688, ng'alina emyaka amakumi ataano mu mwenda, n'aziikibwa mu Bunhill Fields. Omuddaabiriza w'entamu oyo yaloota ekirooto ekyatwalibwa mu nnimi nnyingi okusinga ekitabo kyonna okuggyako Bayibuli.
