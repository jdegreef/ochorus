Hannah Whitall Smith yazaalibwa nga Hannah Tatum Whitall nga 7 Febwali 1832 mu Philadelphia, mu maka g'Abakwakeri agaamanyibwa era agatya Katonda. Ng'aweza emyaka kkumi na mukaaga n'ekitundu yalengera obukulu bwa Katonda obw'ekitalo, naye okunoonya kwe kwali kuwanvu era kwa kulwana. Mu 1851 yafumbirwa Robert Pearsall Smith.

Mu 1865 yazuula ekyama kye yamala obulamu bwe ng'alangirira: nti okuwonyezebwa mu maanyi g'ekibi mulimu gwa Katonda okutuukiriza mu abo abeewaayo gy'ali. Yakifunza bw'ati: "Mu bufunze, nnandigambye nti omulimu gw'omuntu kwe kwesiga, n'omulimu gwa Katonda kwe kukola." Ekyo kyafuuka omutima gw'Ekyama ky'Omukristaayo eky'Obulamu Obw'essanyu (1875), akatabo akaatundibwa ebisukka mu bukadde bubiri mu bulamu bwe. Enkuŋŋaana z'ab'omu Smith mu Bungereza zaayamba okuzaala Olukuŋŋaana lwa Keswick mu 1875.

Yayita mu bigezo bingi. Obuweereza bwa Robert bwakoma mu 1875 wakati mu bulumirizi; ku baana be omusanvu bana baafa mu buvubuka, n'abasatu abaasigalawo ne baaggwaamu okukkiriza. Naye Hannah yayimirira ku kituufu ky'okwagala kwa Katonda so si ku kuwulira kwe. Yawandiika Obutaba n'Omululu bwa Katonda n'Engeri gye Nnakizuula (1903) ne Katonda ow'Okubudaabuda Kwonna (1906).

Mu myaka gye egy'oluvannyuma obulwadde bw'ennyingo bwamusibira mu kagaali k'abalema, naye okumanya nti Katonda amala kwafuula okufa "ekisuubizo eky'essanyu ku biseera eby'omu maaso". Yafa mu Bungereza nga 1 Maayi 1911 ng'aweza emyaka nsanvu mu mwenda. Ekirabo kye yaleka kwe kukkiriza kumu okunywevu: nti omulimu gw'omuntu kwe kwesiga, n'ogwa Katonda kwe kukola.
