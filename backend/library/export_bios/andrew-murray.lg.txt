Andrew Murray yazaalibwa nga 9 Maayi 1828 mu Graaff-Reinet, mu Koloni ya Cape, mu maka agaali gajjudde okusaba. Kitaawe, omuweereza Omuskoti, yasabanga wiiki ku wiiki nti Katonda afuke Omwoyo we ku Afirika ey'Obukiikaddyo. Ng'alina emyaka kkumi, ye ne mukulu we John baaweerezebwa e Scotland okusomera e Aberdeen, oluvannyuma ne basoma theeyologiya mu Yunivasite y'e Utrecht mu Budaaki. Eyo okukkiriza kwe we kwatuukira ku bukakafu obunywevu, n'awandiikira bazadde be nti: “Neekasudde ku Kristo.”

Bwe yawongebwa mu 1848, yaddayo eka n'aweereza e Bloemfontein, ng'atambula ku mbalaasa okutuuka mu maka ag'obwesulu. Mu 1860 e Worcester yalaba okuzuukuka, eky'okuddamu ky'okusaba kwa kitaawe okw'emyaka mingi. Okulabirira abakyusibwa abapya kwamusindika okuwandiika Abide in Christ. Oluvannyuma yaweereza e Cape Town, n'e Wellington okumala emyaka amakumi asatu mu etaano, gye yatandikira Essemeneri ya Huguenot mu 1874 era n'ayamba okukyusa ekkanisa ye okufuuka ekkanisa ey'obuminsani.

Yawandiika ebitabo, obujulizi n'okubuulira ebisukka mu 240, ku kusaba okusinga omutwe gwonna omulala, nga Humility ne Lord, Teach Us To Pray, era ne bivvuunulwa mu nnimi ezisukka mu kkumi n'ettaano. Teyalekebwa awatali bigezo: yafiirwa eddoboozi okumala emyaka ebiri, era akabenje mu 1893 kaamuleka n'omugongo ogwafumitibwa.

Yafiira e Wellington nga 18 Janwali 1917, mu mwaka gwe ogw'ekinaana mu mwenda. Ebitabo bye bikyayigiriza abakkiriza okusigala, okwetoowaza, n'okusaba, era wonna mu nsi omusomi aggula kimu ku byo n'ayiga nate okugamba nti, «Mukama waffe, tuyigirize okusaba».
