Reuben Archer Torrey yazaalibwa nga 28 Janwaali 1856 mu maka ag'obugagga e Hoboken, mu New Jersey. Nnyina yali mukazi wa kusaba, eyamala emyaka mingi ng'asabira obulokozi bw'omwana we. Naye ku Yale Torrey yawaayo obulamu bwe eri amazannyo n'okunywa, era yali amaliridde okuleka okukkiriza. Ekiro kimu mu 1875, ng'aweza emyaka kkumi na mwenda era ng'asazeewo okwetta, yeesanga ng'asaba, ng'ajjukira okusaba kwa nnyina. Yasuubiza Katonda okukola kyonna ky'amusaba, n'agolokoka nga muntu mulala.

Yamaliriza essomero ly'eby'obwakatonda mu 1878, n'awasa Clara Smith mu 1879, era n'asomera e Leipzig ne Erlangen. E Minneapolis mu 1888 yaleka omusaala gwe n'ateeka obulamu bw'amaka ge ku kusaba, ng'ayigidde mu byafaayo bya George Müller ekigambo nti “saba okutuusa ng’oyitiddemu”. Mu 1889 D. L. Moody yamutumya e Chicago okukulembera essomero eryafuuka Essomero lya Bayibuli erya Moody.

Mu 1898 yatandika okusaba ku Lwomukaaga akawungeezi, era bwe yasaba atumibwe okwetooloola ensi, okuyitibwa ne kuva e Australia. Okuva mu 1902 okutuuka mu 1905, ne Charles M. Alexander, yabuulira mu Australia, China, Japan, Buyindi ne mu Bizinga bya Bungereza. Oluvannyuma yafuuka omukulu asooka ow'Essomero lya Bayibuli erya Los Angeles. Yawandiika ebitabo ebisukka mu amakumi ana, nga How to Bring Men to Christ, era n'ayigiriza nti Omwoyo Omutukuvu Muntu w'okumanyibwa so si maanyi ag'okukozesebwa.

Yafiira mu Asheville, mu North Carolina, nga 26 Okitobba 1928, ng'alina emyaka nsanvu mu ebiri. Abaamumanya baamujjukira ng'omusajja ow'okusaba okwa bulijjo era okutasalako.
