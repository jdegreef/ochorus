Charles Haddon Spurgeon yazaalibwa nga 19 Juni 1834 mu Kelvedon, Essex. Kitaawe ne jjajjaawe baali babuulizi ba Kikongregasoni, era omulenzi yamala ebiseera bingi mu nnyumba ya jjajjaawe e Stambourne, ng'asoma ebitabo ebikadde ebya Kipuritaani. Ku Ssande ey'omuzira, nga 6 Jaanwali 1850, ng'aweza emyaka kkumi na etaano, yayingira mu kapeela katono e Colchester, omusajja omugonvu n'amugamba nti: “Omuvubuka, tunuulira Yesu Kristo. Tunuulira! Tunuulira! Tunuulira!” Mu kaseera ako Spurgeon yatunuulira, n'alokolebwa.

Teyayingira ttendekero, naye ng'aweza emyaka kkumi na mukaaga yali abuulira mu kkanisa z'ebyalo, era mu 1854 n'ayitibwa mu kapeela ka New Park Street mu London. Ekibiina kyakula nnyo, era okuva mu 1861 yabuulira mu Metropolitan Tabernacle eyagyamu abantu nga enkumi ttaano. Yawasa Susannah mu 1856. Yalwanagana n'okuggwaamu amaanyi mu mutima, naye ennaku ezo zaafuula okubuulira kwe okuwoomerera abaamenyese emitima. Yali akkiriza nti amaanyi ag'obuweereza bwe gaava mu kusaba kw'abantu be.

Yatandika Ttendekero ly'Abasumba mu 1856, n'ennyumba y'abaana ab'enfuuzi e Stockwell eyaggulwawo mu 1867 ng'eky'okuddamu ky'okusaba. Mu 1887 yalwanirira obuyinza bwa Bayibuli ng'Ekigambo kya Katonda, n'ava mu Baptist Union.

Yafiira e Menton nga 31 Jaanwali 1892, ng'aweza emyaka amakumi ataano mu musanvu. Yaleka ebibuulwa ebifulumiziddwa ebisukka mu enkumi ssatu mu bitaano, n'ebitabo nga Enkya n'Eggulo ne Eterekero lya Dawudi, ebikyasomebwa. Yayitibwa «Omulangira w'Ababuulizi», naye yabuulira Kristo, era yakikola ng'ali ku maviivi.
