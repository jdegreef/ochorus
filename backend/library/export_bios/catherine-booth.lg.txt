Catherine Booth yazaalibwa nga Catherine Mumford e Ashbourne, mu Derbyshire, nga 17 Januwali 1829, omuwala yekka mu maka aga Kimesodisiti aga John ne Sarah Mumford. Yasoma Bayibuli yonna emirundi munaana nga tannaweza myaka kkumi n'ebiri. Obulwadde bw'omugongo bwamusibira eka okumala emyaka esatu, naye n'abufuula essomero ery'emmeeme. Nga wa myaka nga kkumi n'etaano, oluvannyuma lwa wiiki mukaaga ez'okusaba, ennyiriri za Charles Wesley zaamuleetera obukakafu, n'adduka eri nnyina ng'aleekaana nti, "Maama, maama, ndi mwana wa Katonda!"

Mu 1852 yasisinkana William Booth, ne bafumbiriganwa mu 1855. Mu 1859 yawandiika akatabo Female Ministry ng'alwanirira eddembe ly'abakazi okwogerera Katonda, era ku Ssande ya Pentekoote mu 1860 e Gateshead, yayimirira n'atandika okubuulira yennyini. Yafuuka omu ku babuulizi abeewuunyisa okusinga ab'omulembe ogwo, ng'ajjuza ebizimbe ebisinga obunene mu Bungereza.

William bwe yatandika okubuulira abaavu mu buvanjuba bwa London mu 1865, omulimu ogwo gwavaamu Eggye ery'Obulokozi mu 1878. Catherine yayamba okukola enjigiriza yalyo n'awandiika ekitundu ekinene eky'okuyigiriza kwalyo, era abakazi baasobola okuweereza ng'ababuulizi okuva ku nnaku zaalyo ez'olubereberye. Ekyama kye kyali kusaba: yalwananga n'okwegayirira Katonda okumala essaawa nnyingi nga tannaba kwolekera kibiina.

Mu 1888 yategeezebwa nti alina kookolo w'amabeere, naye ekigambo kye kyali: "Olutalo luteekwa okugenda mu maaso." Yafa nga 4 Okitobba 1890 e Clacton-on-Sea, ng'alina emyaka nkaaga mu gumu. Obusika bwe bukyalabika mu mulimu gw'Eggye ery'Obulokozi wonna mu nsi, ne mu nzigi ze yaggulawo eri obukulembeze bw'abakazi.
