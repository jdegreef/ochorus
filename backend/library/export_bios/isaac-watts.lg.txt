Isaac Watts yazaalibwa nga 17 Julaayi 1674 e Southampton. Kitaawe, ow'ekibiina ky'Abatakkiriziganya n'Ekkanisa ya Bungereza, yasibibwa mu kkomera emirundi esatu olw'okukkiriza kwe. Omulenzi yali wa magezi mangi, ng'amanyi Olulatini, Oluyonaani n'Olwebbulaniya ng'akyali muto. Yagaana okugenda mu yunivasite kubanga kyandimwetaagisizza okwegatta ku Kkanisa ya Bungereza. Mu 1689, ng'aweza emyaka kkumi na etaano, yawandiika nti: “Nnayigirizibwa okwesiga Kristo, nsuubira.”

Ng'aweza emyaka nga amakumi abiri, ng'atakkaanya n'okuyimba okukakanyavu mu kkanisa, kitaawe n'amugamba awandiike ekisingako, era bw'atyo bwe yakola. Mu 1702 yafuuka omusumba w'ekibiina e London, era n'asigala nga musumba waakyo obulamu bwe bwonna. Mu 1707 yafulumya Hymns and Spiritual Songs, omwali When I Survey the Wondrous Cross, ne mu 1719 The Psalms of David Imitated, omwava Our God, Our Help in Ages Past ne Joy to the World. Ennyimba ze ne Zabbuli ze zibalibwa nga lusanvu mu ataano, era ayitibwa kitaawe w'ennyimba ez'Olungereza.

Mu 1712 omusujja ogw'amaanyi gwamunafuya, n'atasobola kubuulira okumala emyaka egisukka mu ena. Sir Thomas Abney ne mukyala we baamuyita mu maka gaabwe okumala wiiki emu, naye yabeera nabo emyaka amakumi asatu mu mukaaga. Eyo mwe yawandiikira ebitabo bye ebisinga, nga Divine Songs ey'abaana ne A Guide to Prayer.

Yafa nga 25 Novemba 1748, ng'aweza emyaka nsanvu mu ena, n'aziikibwa mu Bunhill Fields. Yali mutono, mulwadde, era yeesigama ku kisa ky'emikwano, naye okuva mu bunafu obwo mwavaamu ebigambo ebyayigiriza ekkanisa okutunuulira omusaalaba n'okwesiga Katonda. Teyabuulira bibiina binene, naye yabiwa eky'okuyimba.
