Albert Benjamin Simpson yazaalibwa nga 15 Desemba 1843 e Bayview, ku Kizinga kya Prince Edward, mu Kanada, mu maka ag'Abapresbiteri Abasukoti agatya Katonda. Ng'akyali muvubuka, ng'alumwa endowooza nti tannaba kumanya Katonda, yamenyeka mu mubiri ne mu mwoyo. Omusana gwajja okuyita mu kitabo kya Walter Marshall, Gospel Mystery of Sanctification, ekyamuyigiriza nti omwonoonyi ayinza okujja eri Kristo nga bw'ali. N'ajja, ng'alina emyaka nga kkumi na etaano.

Yasomera ku Ssomero lya Knox e Toronto, era mu 1865 n'ayitibwa mu Kkanisa lya Knox e Hamilton, oluvannyuma e Louisville, ne mu 1879 mu New York. Bwe yalaba abasenguse n'abaavu abaali ebweru w'enzigi z'ekkanisa, yava mu buweereza mu 1881 nga talina musaala, n'atandikawo Gospel Tabernacle gye buli muntu yanirizibwanga. Mu kyeya kya 1881 e Old Orchard Beach yatwala Mukama waffe Yesu ng'Omuwonya we, era obulwadde bwe obw'ebbanga ne buggwaawo.

Mu 1883 yaggulawo Missionary Training Institute, essomero ery'okusooka erya Bayibuli mu Amerika ey'Obukiikakkono, era ebibiina bye ne byegatta mu 1897 ne bifuuka Christian and Missionary Alliance, ekyafuuka amaanyi ag'obuminsani ku buli ssemazinga. Yawandiika ebitabo ebisukka mu kikumi, nga Days of Heaven Upon Earth ne The Fourfold Gospel, n'ennyimba ezikyayimbibwa. Obubaka bwe bwali bwangu: si mukisa, si kuwonya, wabula Kristo Yennyini.

Yafiira e Nyack, New York, nga 29 Okitobba 1919, era n'aziikibwa ku lusozi lw'essomero lye yatandikawo. Ebibiina bye bibala amakanisa mu nkumi, naye ekijjukizo kye ekisinga gwe musomi akomya okugoberera omukisa n'azuula Omugabi w'omukisa — Yennyini.
