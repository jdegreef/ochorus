Gareth Evans yazaalibwa mu 1938 mu Merthyr Tydfil, wakati mu biwonvu by'amanda ag'amayinja mu South Wales. Maama we yafa ng'alina emyaka kkumi n'esatu, era bwe yali ow'emyaka kkumi na mukaaga, mu lukuŋŋaana lw'abavubuka olw'Olwokutaano akawungeezi, ekibuuzo ky'omubuulizi omuvubuka — "Onookola ki ne Mukama Yesu Kristo?" — kyamuleetera okuwaayo obulamu bwe eri Kristo. Ekigambo ky'omukkiriza omukulu kyafuuka ekigambo ekimulungamya mu bulamu bwe bwonna: "Nnaatambulanga ne Yesu, si mu maaso ge wadde emabega we."

Yasoma fizikisi ku Yunivasite y'e Swansea, gye yasisinkana Anne; baafumbiriganwa mu 1961. Okumala emyaka abiri ekibiina ky'essomero kye kyali ekituuti kye — mu Wales, mu masomero g'amagye mu Germany ne Hong Kong, era oluvannyuma mu Toronto — era buli ssomero gye yasomesa yatandikawo n'ekibiina ky'Ekikristaayo.

Ku ntandikwa y'emyaka gya 1980 yaleka okusomesa n'afuuka omusumba mu Christian and Missionary Alliance, okusooka mu Kitchener, Ontario, n'oluvannyuma mu Victoria Alliance Church ku kizinga kya Vancouver, ye ne Anne gye bakyabeera. Okuva mu 1991 okutuuka mu 1994 baaweereza ng'abasumba ku lyato ly'okusaasira Anastasis mu Afirika ey'Obugwanjuba, era oluvannyuma ne wabaawo okuyitibwa okuyigiriza abasumba n'abayizi mu Australia, Brazil, Buyindi, Naijeriya n'awalala.

Mu lugendo olwo oluwanvu mwe mwava ebitabo bye — ebikkakkamu, ebyangu era eby'obusumba, ebyawandiikirwa ekkanisa eriko ebisago n'abakkiriza abeetaaga okujjukizibwa nti Katonda akutte enkya yaabwe. Gareth ne Anne balina abaana abawala basatu, abazzukulu munaana, n'abazzukulu b'abazzukulu kkumi na bana. Leero aweereza okusinga okuyita mu mukutu gwe, ejjinja limu oluvannyuma lw'eddala.
