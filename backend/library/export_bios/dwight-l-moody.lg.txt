Dwight Lyman Moody yazaalibwa nga 5 Febwali 1837 e Northfield, Massachusetts, nga ye w’omukaaga mu baana omwenda. Kitaawe yafa ng’alina emyaka ena n’aleka amaka mu bbanja. Bwe yali aweza emyaka kkumi na musanvu yagenda e Boston okukola mu dduuka ly’engatto ery’akojjaawe Samuel. Eyo, nga 21 Apuli 1855, omusomesa we ow’essomero lya Ssande, Edward Kimball, yamusanga emabega mu dduuka n’amubuulira okwagala kwa Kristo, era Moody n’awa Omulokozi omutima gwe.

Mu 1856 yasengukira e Chicago, mu 1858 n’atandika essomero lya Ssande, era mu 1860 n’aleka obusuubuzi okuwa Katonda obudde bwe bwonna. Mu 1871, oluvannyuma lw’Omuliro Omunene ogwa Chicago, okubeerawo kwa Katonda kwamujjira mu ngeri ey’amaanyi e New York, era okuva olwo ebikumi ne bakyuka. Ng’akutte ekigambo kya Henry Varley, yamalirira: “Olw’ekisa kya Katonda, nnaaba omuntu oyo.”

Mu 1873 yasaabala okugenda e Bungereza n’omuyimbi Ira Sankey, enkuŋŋaana ne zisaasaana okuva e York okutuuka e London, era yaddayo ewaka mu 1875 ng’omubuulizi asinga okumanyibwa. Yatandikawo Northfield Seminary mu 1879, Mount Hermon School mu 1881, Moody Bible Institute e Chicago mu 1886, era mu 1894 ekibiina ekyafulumya ebitabo eby’Ekikristaayo eby’omuwendo omutono. Buli nkya yakuumanga essaawa ez’olubereberye olw’Ekitabo Ekitukuvu n’okusaba.

Mu Novemba 1899 omutima gwe gwatandika okulemererwa e Kansas City, n’aleetebwa ewaka e Northfield. Ku makya g’omu 22 Desemba 1899 yagamba: “Guno gwe muwendo gwange; luno lwe lunaku lwange olw’okwambazibwa engule!” Yaziikibwa ku Round Top e Northfield, era emboozi ze ez’enjiri zikyakola omulimu gwazo buli we zisomerwa.
