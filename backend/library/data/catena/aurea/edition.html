Thomas Aquinas (1225–1274) compiled the Catena Aurea in Latin in the 1260s. The English translation in this book was published at Oxford by John Henry Parker between 1841 and 1845, in four volumes, one for each Gospel, under the editorship of John Henry Newman. Its words are given here as printed.

The printed volumes set each Father’s name at the head of his words in an abbreviated form, “CHRYS.”, “AUG.”, “THEOPHYL.”, with the place in his works in the margin. Here the names are given in full and the margin references are left out, so that the chain can be read without interruption.
