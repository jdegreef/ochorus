Charles Grandison Finney (1792–1875) yali munnamateeka eyafuuka omubuulizi w'enjiri, okubuulira kwe ne kuyamba okutambuza Okuzuukuka Okukulu Okw'okubiri mu bitundu eby'engulu ebya New York n'okusukka wo. Ng'akkirizza nti okuzuukuka si kyamagero eky'okulindirira wabula nkozesa ntuufu ey'eby'obuyambi Katonda by'awa, yatandikawo “enkola empya” eby'enkuŋŋaana ezaawangaala n'okusaba abantu butereevu, n'alaba ebibuga ebiramba nga bikyusibwa, era oluvannyuma n'afuuka pulezidenti wa Oberlin College. Ekitabo kye Lectures on Revivals of Religion kikola ku ngeri ekkanisa ey'olulimi Olungereza gy'etera okulowoozaako okuzuukuka okuva mu kiseera ekyo.
