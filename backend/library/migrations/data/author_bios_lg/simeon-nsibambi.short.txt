Simeon Nsibambi (1897–1978) yali mukungu w'ebyobulamu Omuganda eyayigirizibwa era omugagga mu Kampala, ng'enjala ye ey'omwoyo ye yamuleetera okusisinkana okw'amakulu mu Ssebutemba 1929 n'omusawo omuminsani Joe Church e Namirembe. Enkomerero y'ewiiki yaabwe ow'okusoma Bayibuli n'okusaba ku kujjula kw'Omwoyo yayamba okukoleeza Okuzuukuka kw'Afirika ey'Obuvanjuba (Abalokole). Nsibambi yaleka omulimu gwe ogwa gavumenti olw'obulamu obw'okusaba n'okubuulira buli muntu kinnoomu, n'afuuka, mu byafaayo bingi, kitaawe w'akakwate akaakyusa Obukristaayo bw'Afirika ey'Obuvanjuba, era akaazaala abakulembeze nga Festo Kivengere ne Janani Luwum.
