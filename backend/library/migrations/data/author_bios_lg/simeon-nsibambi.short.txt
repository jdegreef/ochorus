Simeon Nsibambi (1897–1978) yali musawo w'ebyobulamu Omuganda eyayigirizibwa era omugagga mu Kampala, ng'enjala ye ey'omwoyo ye yamuleetera okusisinkana okw'amakulu mu Sebutemba 1929 n'omusawo omuminsani Joe Church e Namirembe. Wikendi waabwe ow'okusoma Baibuli n'okusaba ku kujjula kw'Omwoyo yayamba okukoleeza Okuzuukuka kw'Afrika ya Buvanjuba (Abalokole). Nsibambi yaleka omulimu gwe ogwa gavumenti olw'obulamu obw'okusaba n'okubuulira buli muntu kinnoomu, n'afuuka, mu byafaayo bingi, kitaawe w'akakwate akaakyusa Obukristaayo bw'Afrika ya Buvanjuba, era akaazaala abakulembeze nga Festo Kivengere ne Janani Luwum.
