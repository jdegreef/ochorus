Andrew Murray yazaalibwa nga 9 Maayi 1828 mu Graaff-Reinet, ekibuga eky'oku nsalo ekyalimu enfuufu mu Cape Colony, mu maka agaali gamaze okujjula okusaba. Kitaawe, omuwabuzi Omusukoti eyatumibwa Ekkanisa lya Scotland, yalina empisa enywevu ey'okusomera ab'omu maka ge ebyafaayo eby'okuzuukuka, n'okwegayirira, buli wiiki nga bw'eddiŋŋaana, nti Katonda afuke Omwoyo we ku South Africa. Andrew yakulira ng'awulira okwegayirira okwo ku kyoto ky'amaka, era wadde eky'okuddamu kyali kya kulwawo okutuuka, kyali kya kujja nga kiyita mu mwana eyafukamira ku mabbali ga kitaawe oyo. Nnyina yakomyawo omusaayi gwe eri abanoonyi b'obubudamo Abahuguenoti Abafalansa n'Abaluteeri Abadaaki, bwe kityo mu misuwa gy'omulenzi oyo mwayitangamu okujjukira kw'abantu ababiri abaali babonyaabonyezebwa olw'okukkiriza kwabwe.

Omulenzi Eyaweerezebwa Emitala w'Ennyanja

Nga wa myaka kkumi, Andrew ne muganda we omukulu John baaweerezebwa emitala w'ennyanja okutuuka mu Scotland basomere mu Aberdeen, n'oluvannyuma mu Yunivasite ya Aberdeen, gye baafunira ebitiibwa byabwe eby'ebisomo mu 1845. Kyali kya ntiisa okuteeka abalenzi babiri abato mu meeri okugenda mu nsi endala n'obutabaddayo kubalaba nate okumala emyaka, era okwawukana okwo kwabanyigiriza. Mu Scotland baayingizibwa mu masemba g'okuzuukuka okw'obwannabbi okwali kunyeenya ensi mu kiseera ekyo, nga bassa omukka gwe gumu n'abasajja nga Robert Murray M'Cheyne ne Horatius Bonar. Okuva mu Aberdeen ab'oluganda ne beeyongerayo okutuuka mu Yunivasite ya Utrecht mu Netherlands okusoma eby'okutegeera Katonda, era eyo ne beegatta ku bibiina bya Réveil, ekitongole ky'okuzuukuka okw'Abaputotesitante ekyeteekawo okulwanyisa amagezi amakalu ag'omutima, era ekyassa ekitiibwa ku kukyuka, obutukuvu obw'omunda, n'eddiini ey'okulaba.

Wadde ng'okukulira kwe kwonna kwali kwa kutya Katonda, Murray teyeesigama ku ddiini ye ye. Kyali mu myaka gya Utrecht okukkiriza kwe ye lwe kwatuuka ku bukakafu obunywevu, era yawandiikira eka ku nkyukakyuka eyo n'obulyeyimu obw'omuntu eyamanya enjawulo wakati w'okukkiriza okusikiddwa n'okwewaayo okw'omuntu ye. Nga 9 Maayi 1848, ku lunaku lwe olw'amazaalibwa amakumi abiri, ye ne muganda we baayawulibwa Akakiiko ka Hague ak'Ekkanisa lya Reformed ery'Abadaaki, ne bakyusa amaaso gaabwe okuddayo mu Africa.

Obuweereza obw'Entebe y'Embalaasi

Omulimu gwe ogusooka gwali Bloemfontein, ekitundu ky'ekkanisa ekyali si kibuga wabula ekitundu ekinene. Bwe yateekebwawo mu 1849 nga yaakamala emyaka amakumi abiri mu gumu, yafuuka omuwabuzi asooka okubeera mu bukiikakkono bwa Mugga Orange, ng'avunaanyizibwa ku bibinja bya Voortrekker ebyali bisaasaanidde mu bbanga eddene ery'ewala erizeeyo enguudo. Yeebagala embalaasi okumala wiiki nnyingi nga tazze kukoma okutuuka ku byalo eby'obwannamunigina, ng'abatiza, ng'ayigiriza katekisimu, ng'awasa, ng'aziika, era ng'abuulira abantu abaali bayinza obutalaba muwabuzi nate okumala omwaka. Yali buweereza bw'entebe y'embalaasi n'olusenyi olugazi, era gwamuyigiriza amangu nti omulimu gwali gwa Katonda so si gugwe ye.

Mu 1856 yawasa Emma Rutherford mu Cape Town, obufumbo obw'okwagala okw'ekinnansi era okw'olubeerera obwabawa abaana kkumi na omu, mwenda ku bo ne bawangaala okutuuka mu bukulu. Oluvannyuma, mu 1860, ne kujja enkyukakyuka enkulu ey'obulamu bwe. Murray yakkiriza okuyitibwa okugenda Worcester, era eyo n'atambulira butereevu mu ky'okuddamu ky'okusaba kwa kitaawe okw'emyaka mingi.

Okuzuukuka okwa 1860

Okuzuukuka okwa 1860 tekwatandika ne Murray. Ku kyalo ekiriraanye Worcester, akabinja k'abakkiriza baali baakuŋŋaananga buli wiiki okumala emyezi, nga beegayirira olw'okufukibwa kw'Omwoyo; omukazi omu ku bo yeeraliikirira nnyo n'okuma yaali asaba ng'atakoma okumala wiiki. Omwoyo bwe yakka, yakka n'amaanyi agaawaanyisa empisa zonna ez'okusaba okwa Reformed ery'Abadaaki. Murray ye kennyini, ng'akulembera olukuŋŋaana olumu ng'olwo, yawa ebiragiro bye eby'obwegendereza n'akoowoola abantu basabe omu ku omu — naye mu kifo ky'ekyo olukuŋŋaana lwonna ne lubwatuka mu kaseera kamu mu kusaba, mu kukaaba, nga basaba okusaasirwa. Murray bwe yagezaako okukomyawo entegeka, omu ku balimi yamugamba lwatu ateekebwe emabega: tewali muntu ayinza kuzimba bbugwe wa kidiba okwetooloola ekyo Katonda kye yali akola. Kyali kiyigo ekyo omuwabuzi omuvubuka ow'entegeka era ow'obwegendereza kye yateeka mu mutima. Yayiga okuwewula okunywerera kwe ku nnuyi, era Katonda n'amukozesa okulunda okuzuukuka kwe yali tayinza kukola era kye yali tayinza kuvumirira kuzikiza.

Ebbanga eddene nga Andrew Murray tannatuuka Worcester, akabinja k'abakkiriza ku kyalo ekiriraanye baali baakuŋŋaananga buli wiiki okumala emyezi okwegayirira olw'okuzuukuka, era omukazi omu ku bo yasaba ng'atakoma okumala wiiki. Okusaba kwabwe kwaddibwamu mu kuzuukuka okwa 1860: Omwoyo yakka mu lukuŋŋaana olw'okusaba, ebibiina byonna ne bibwatuka byokka mu kusaba okusaasirwa, era enkyukakyuka eyo n'esaasaana okuva mu kyalo okutuuka ku byalo eby'obwannamunigina ebyetooloola — kwe kufukibwa kwennyini kitaawe wa Murray kwe yali asabira okumala emyaka mingi egy'okusinza okw'amaka.

Okuva mu kuzuukuka okwo ne muvaamu Murray omuwandiisi. Okulabirira kwe okw'obusumba eri amataba g'abakyufu abaggya, okweraliikirira kwe nti okwagala kwabwe okusooka kuleme okunnyogoga ne kufuuka mpisa bwereere, kwamuwaliriza okuwandiika Beera mu Kristo — okusooka mu Ludaaki nga Blijf in Jezus — era n'ekyo n'afuna omusuwa gwe yali agenda okukolerako obulamu bwe bwonna obwasigalawo. Okuva mu Worcester yasenguka okugenda mu Groote Kerk eya Cape Town mu 1864, ng'ayimirira awo okulwanyisa amayengo agaali geeyongera ag'obweddembe mu by'okutegeera Katonda, era mu 1871 n'agenda Wellington, gye yatereera okumala ekiseera ekisinga obuwanvu era ekisinga okubala ebibala eky'obuweereza bwe, emyaka amakumi asatu mu ttaano ku ppulupiti emu.

Wellington n'Okulaba Okugaziwa

Wellington yafuuka ekitandiiro eky'okulaba kwe okugazi. Ng'akakasizza nti ekkanisa lyetaaga okukyukira ebweru, yakola nnyo okutandikawo ebitongole ebyali bijja kumuwangaala: essomero ery'okutendeka abamisonaali, ne Sseminaali ya Huguenot ey'okusomesa abakazi, gye yaleetera abasomesa okuva mu America. Yayamba okutandikawo ebibiina by'okusaba n'ebibiina by'obumisonaali, era n'akulembera eddiini ye okuva ku kubeera ekitongole ekyetunuulira munda okutuuka ku kuba ekkanisa ery'obumisonaali. Ekkalaamu ye teyawummulako n'akatono; okutuuka ku nkomerero yali afulumizza ebitabo n'obutabo obusukka ebikumi bibiri, ku kusaba okusinga omutwe gwonna omulala, nga bikyusibwa mu lulimi oluvannyuma lw'olulala okutuusa okusinza kwe okuteefu okw'Oluafrikaans lwe kwafuuka kusomebwa ku buli ssemazinga.

Obwetoowaze, ekifo ky'okwesigama ku Katonda okutuukiridde, kiri, olw'obutonde bw'ebintu bwennyini, omulimu ogusooka era obulungi obusinga obw'ekitonde, era omusingi gwa buli bulungi.

— Andrew Murray, Humility

Okugezesebwa n'Emyaka egy'Akasirise

Okukkiriza kwe tekwalekebwa awatali kugezesebwa kwakwo, era teyeekweka nti si bwe kiri. Mu 1879 emimiro gye ne gimulemwa; yafiirwa eddoboozi lye n'ayingira mu ekyo kye yayita “emyaka gye egy'akasirise,” emyaka ebiri omubuulizi mwe yali tayinza kubuulira. Yanoonya abo abaayigiriza eby'okutegeera Katonda eby'okuwonya okw'obwakatonda, era mu 1882 yatambula okutuuka mu nnyumba ey'okukkiriza mu London, gye yava ng'akakasizza nti okuwonya kwabelera mu mulimu gwa Kristo ogw'okununula, era n'akomezebwawo ku ppulupiti ye. Wabula teyafuula okuwonya kwe muggo gw'okukuba abalwadde, wadde okugaana okujjanjabibwa okwa bulijjo. Oluvannyuma, mu 1893, akabenje k'eggaali mu Natal kaamuleka n'omugongo ogwali gwenkojjeddwa emirembe gyonna n'omubiri ogwakoona; mu myaka gye egy'enkomerero emirundi mingi yabuuliranga ng'atudde, omukadde eyeekunja ku bunafu bwe naye eyali tasirisibwa nabwo.

Kyali okuva mu kiseera ekimu ng'ekyo eky'obulumi Murray gye yalekera ekkanisa ekigambo kye eky'okubuulirira ekyayagalibwa okusinga. Mu 1895, ng'awummulira mu nnyumba ya mukwano gwe mu Wimbledon, ng'ali mu bulumi bw'omubiri era ng'afumitiddwa ekiro ekyayita ebbaluwa enkambwe eyalumba okuyigiriza kwe, yakwata ekkalaamu n'awandiika akawaandiiko si kwa kufulumizibwa wabula ku lw'omwoyo gwe ye.

Okusooka, Ye yannreeta wano; kya kwagala kwe nga ndi mu kifo kino eky'obuzibu: mu kituufu ekyo mwe nnaawummulira. Okuddako, anankuuma wano mu kwagala kwe, era ampe ekisa ky'okweyisa ng'omwana we. Oluvannyuma, ajja kufuula okugezesebwa omukisa, ng'anjigiriza amasomo g'ayagala mbe njiga, era ng'akolera mu nze ekisa ky'ayagala okumpa. Ekisembayo, mu kiseera kye ekirungi ayinza okunziggyayo nate — engeri n'ekiseera Ye by'amanyi.

— Andrew Murray, “Mu Kiseera ky'Obuzibu Gamba,” ekyawandiikibwa mu Wimbledon, 1895

Ekisenge eky'Omunda

Empisa eyali wansi wa byonna yali kusaba. Murray yayigiriza nti okusaba kiri mu kaseera kamu ekintu ekyangu ennyo omwana ky'ayinza okukola era omulimu ogusinga obw'awaggulu omuntu gw'ayinza okulinnya okutuukirira, era yakola kye yayigiriza, ng'akuuma “ekisenge eky'omunda” eky'okusaba okw'ekyama ng'ensulo buli kintu ekirala mwe kyava ne kikulukuta. Yagolokoka okusisinkana Katonda nga tannaba kusisinkana bantu, era n'abuulirira abalala nti okumanya engeri y'okwogera ne Katonda kintu kya maanyi okusinga okumanya engeri y'okwogera n'omuntu.

Okusaba kwe kwali empisa eyali omusingi gw'obulamu bwa Murray bwonna era omutwe gwe yaddiŋŋanako okusinga gwonna omulala mu byawandiika. Yanywerera nti ekifo eky'ekyama eky'okusaba — “ekisenge eky'omunda” — kwe kuggibwa amaanyi g'Omukristaayo, era nti wadde okusaba kwangu okumala eri omwana omunafu, mu kaseera ke kamu gwe mulimu omutukuvu ennyo omuntu yenna gw'ayinza okulinnya okutuukirira: obumu obw'amazima ne Katonda atalabika era asinga obutukuvu, obuyingirwamu mu kwewaayo okutuukiridde eri okwagala kwe era mu linnya lya Yesu.

Wadde nga mu ntandikwa yaakwo okusaba kwangu nnyo n'omwana omunafu ng'asobola okusaba, mu kaseera ke kamu gwe mulimu ogusinga obw'awaggulu era omutukuvu omuntu gw'ayinza okulinnya okutuukirira.

— Andrew Murray, Lord, Teach Us To Pray

Obusika

Andrew Murray yafa mu Wellington nga 18 Jjanwali 1917, ng'ali mu mwaka gwe ogw'ekyenda mu munaana mu mwenda, ng'awangaazizza okusukka ekitundu ky'ekyasa okusinga okuzuukuka okwamufuula nga bwe yali, era ng'awadde ekkanisa olusenyi lw'ebitabo ebikyayigiriza abakkiriza okubeera mu Kristo, okwetoowaza, n'okusaba. Teyasaba kujjukirwa nga omusajja omukulu; ebyawandiika bye ye byanyweza awatali kukoowa ekintu ekikontana — nti ekitiibwa ky'ekitonde bwe bwereere bwakyo, era nti mwoyo gwokka ogwaggwaamu obwakyo gwe guleka Katonda nga wa ddembe okuba byonna. Ebifaananyi ebibajje biyimiridde ku lulwe mu Wellington n'ebweru wa Groote Kerk mu Cape Town, naye ekijjukizo eky'amazima ennyo gwe muntu afukamidde, wonna mu nsi, aggulawo kimu ku bitabo bya Murray ebitono n'ayiga nate okugamba, “Mukama, tuyigirize okusaba.”
