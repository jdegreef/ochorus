Waggulu w'edduuka ery'essaawa mu kibuga ekikadde ekya Haarlem waaliwo ekisenge eky'obulimba, era emabega waakyo nga waliyo ekifo ekitono ennyo abantu abatono mwe basobola okuyimirira. Kyazimbibwa mu kisenge eky'okwebaka ekiri ku ddaala erya waggulu erya omukazi eyali mu myaka gy'amakumi ataano, atawasibwako, eyayimirizaawo obulamu bwe ng'addaabiriza essaawa, era eyamala obulamu bwe bwonna mu nnyumba y'emu enfunda. Olutalo bwe lwatuuka ku luggi lwe, Corrie ten Boom yafuula ennyumba eyo ekifo eky'obubudamo, n'afiirwa ekitundu ekisinga ekya famire ye olw'ekyo, n'awonawo mu kifo ekyategekebwa okumutta, era n'ava emitala okumala ekitundu ky'obulamu bwe ekyasigalawo ng'abuulira abantu ku kusonyiwa. Ye omu ku batono mu tterekero lino abajjukirwa si nnyo olw'ekyo kye yawandiika wabula olw'ekintu kimu kye yakola n'omukono gwe, emyaka mingi oluvannyuma lw'olutalo, mu kyeya ky'ekkanisa mu Munich.

Beje

Cornelia ten Boom yazaalibwa nga 15 April 1892 mu Amsterdam, n'akulira mu Haarlem, famire we baalina edduuka ery'essaawa n'amaka mu kizimbe kimu. Baakiyita Beje, ng'amanya baagaggya mu luguudo kwe kyali kizimbiddwa, Barteljorisstraat. Kitaawe, Casper, yali muddaabiriza wa ssaawa, eyatya Katonda era omuwombeefu, ow'enzikiriza ey'Ekidatchi Ekirongooseddwa (Dutch Reformed); mwannyina Betsie ye yali munne obulamu bwe bwonna. Baali maka ga bulijjo, agakkiriza, agatalina kya njawulo — ago agatereeta mawulire ga kyama okumala emyaka amakumi ataano, oluvannyuma, bwe gagezesebwa, ne galeeta buli kimu.

Corrie yayiga omulimu gwa kitaawe, era mu 1922, n'afuuka omukazi omusooka mu Netherlands okuweebwa layisinsi ey'okuddaabiriza essaawa. Mu kitundu ekisinga eky'obulamu bwe ogwo gwe mugaso gwonna — omukazi atali mufumbo, edduuka ery'amaka, ekkanisa, n'omulimu omuvuvu ogw'ebyuma ebitono. Yali kumpi n'emyaka amakumi ataano ekintu bwe kyabaawo ekyo ensi kaakano kye emumanyiira, ekintu ekisaana okujjukirwa buli muntu alowooza nti obulamu bwe obw'amazima tebunnatandika.

Ekifo eky'okwekweka

Germany yawamba Netherlands mu May 1940. Okuyigganyizibwa kw'Abayudaaya Abadatchi bwe kweyongera, ba ten Boom ne bayingizibwa mu mukutu ogw'ekyama, era ennyumba yaabwe — enkadde, eri wakati mu kibuga, ejjudde ensonda ez'enjawulo — n'efuuka ennyingo mu lujegere olwatambuza Abayudaaya n'abakozi ab'okuziyiza omulabe okutuuka mu bukuumi. Ekisenge eky'ekyama kyazimbibwa emabega w'ekisenge eky'obulimba mu kisenge kya Corrie yennyini eky'okwebaka ku ddaala erya waggulu. Eyeekaalu erabirira ennyumba eno leero egamba nti famire n'omukutu gwaabwe baayamba okununula abantu abaagerageranyizibwa okuba lunaana. Kigero kyokka, nga kiva ku abo abaaliwo; ekigendererwa si muwendo mutuufu wabula nti buli omu yali muntu, eyakwekebwa omu ku emu, nga obulamu bwa buli muntu mu nnyumba bwe bwali mu kabi.

Nga 28 February 1944 famire yaliibwako olukwe omukessi Omudatchi. Ennyumba n'egwaako amagye era abantu nga amakumi asatu ne bakwatibwa ku lunaku olwo, Corrie ne Betsie ne kitaabwe omukadde nga bali mu bo. Abaserikale tebaazuula kisenge kya kyama. Abantu omukaaga abaali beekwese emabega w'ekisenge ne basigala eyo, nga basirise, okumala ennaku, era oluvannyuma ne baggyibwayo ne batwalibwa mu bukuumi. Ekifo eky'okwekweka ne kinywera. Abo abakizimba tebaanywera.

Casper ten Boom, eyali mu myaka gy'amakumi munaana, yafa nga wayiseewo ennaku nga kkumi oluvannyuma lw'okukwatibwa. Corrie ne Betsie baayisibwa mu kkomera ery'e Scheveningen n'olusiisira olw'e Vught, oluvannyuma ne batwalibwa ebuvanjuba, mu Germany, mu lusiisira olw'abakazi olw'okusibirwamu olw'e Ravensbrück.

Ravensbrück, ne Betsie

Ekyaliwo e Ravensbrück kye kikulu mu bujulirwa bwa Corrie, era tewali kimu kikwata ku buvumu bwe ye. Kikwata ku mwannyina. Betsie ten Boom, omunafu era omulwadde, yasisinkana ekifo ekyo n'okukkiriza okwatabula buli muntu eyali okumwetooloola, nga ne Corrie mwalimu. Yakakasa nti Katonda teyali abuze mu nnyumba z'olusiisira, nti asobola okwebazibwa n'eyo, era nti baaleetebwa mu ggeyeena eno yennyini olw'ensonga — okuyiga ekintu lwe lulituuka lwe baliteekwa okukibuulira abantu.

Corrie ategeeza nti ennyumba yaabwe eyajjula abantu yali ejjudde obunyama, era nti Betsie yeebaza Katonda ne ku bwo. Oluvannyuma lwokka lwe baategeera ensonga: abakuumi tebaayagala kuyingira mu kisenge ekijjudde obunyama, bwe kityo bannnyinaze ababiri ne balekebwa bokka okusomera abakazi abalala Baibuli mu ddoboozi, awatali kuziyizibwa, mu kifo ekintu ekifaanana bwe kityo mwe kyali kitasoboka.

Betsie yanywerera ku kwebaza mu nnyumba z'olusiisira ku buli kimu, ne ku bunyama — ne Corrie, eyali tasobola kutegeera makulu gaakyo, n'awa okwebaza wadde bwe kityo. Abakuumi, kyazuulibwa, baali beewala ekisenge ekijjudde obunyama. Mu nsonda emu ey'olusiisira olwo abakuumi mwe bataayingira, bannnyinaze ababiri ne basomera abaali basaliddwa okufa Ekyawandiikibwa.

Corrie ate ategeeza ku kacupa akatono ak'eddagala erya vitamiini eddibaavu ke yasobola okuterekera, mwe yawanga ettondo buli lunaku Betsie n'oluvannyuma abakazi abalwadde abaali okubeetooloola nga beeyongera obungi — era ke, bwe yagamba, ne keeyongera okuwa amatondo okusukka ekigero we kaali kalidde okukalira, okutuusa lwe baatuukirwa endagala empya. Betsie yagifaananya n'ensuwa ey'amafuta eya nnamwandu ow'e Zerefaasi, mu nnaku za Eriya, eteyaggwaawo. Corrie ategeeza olugero ng'ekijjukizo kye ye; kya mu kunnyonnyola kwe ekifo ekyo, era kiweebwa wano nga bwe ye yakiwaayo.

Betsie yafiira e Ravensbrück nga 16 December 1944. Nga tannafa yali agambye Corrie, emirundi n'emirundi, nti kibagwanira okufuluma oluvannyuma ne babuulira abantu kye baayiga eyo — nti tewali kizikiza kya buziba nnyo Katonda gy'atabeera wansi n'okusingawo. Kye kigambo ekisinga okukwataganyizibwa ne bannnyinaze ababiri, era kyali kya Betsie:

Tewali bunnya bwa buziba nnyo Ye gy'atabeera wansi n'okusingawo.

— Betsie ten Boom, nga Corrie bwe yakitegeeza mu The Hiding Place

Nga wayiseewo ennaku, ku nkomerero ddala ya December 1944, Corrie n'ata. Ategeeza nti kyava ku nsobi ey'omuwandiisi, era nti abakazi ab'omu mulembe gwe baatwalibwa okuttibwa nga wayiseewo akaseera katono. Teyeewaana nti yali yeefunidde okuwona wadde okukitegeera bulungi. Yali ayingidde n'mwannyina ne famire; n'afuluma bwannamunigina, ng'atutte ekiragiro okuva eri omukazi eyafa.

Omutambuze wa Mukama

Corrie yakuuma ekiragiro. Okumala nga emyaka amakumi asatu mu esatu egyaddirira, okutuusa mu myaka gye egy'amakumi munaana, yatambula — amawanga agasukka nkaaga — ng'omwogezi eyeetooloola atalina kifo kya maka kigere, nga yeeyita omutambuze wa Mukama. Obubaka bwe si bwe bwali okusinga ku ntiisa gye yawonako. Bwali bukwata ku kusonyiwa, ku kutabaganya, ne ku kumpi kw'okwagala kwa Katonda okutakoowa mu bifo ebisingira ddala obubi abantu bye basobola okukola. Yali yeefunidde obuyinza okukyogera mu ngeri yokka obuyinza obwo mwe bufunibwa.

Awo, mu 1947, mu kkanisa mu Munich, obubaka ne bumuweebwa butereevu.

Yali yaakamala okwogera n'ekibiina eky'Abadaaki ku kusonyiwa kwa Katonda. Oluvannyuma omusajja n'ajja mu maaso, ng'amwenyumwenya, ng'agolodde omukono gwe — era n'amutegeera. Yali akuumi e Ravensbrück, omu ku basajja abaali ku luggi lw'ekifo kyennyini Betsie we yafiira era bannnyinaze we baateekebwa okuyita nga batambula, nga bali obwereere, wansi w'amaaso ge. Ye teyamutegeera. Yamugamba nti kati yali afuuse Omukristaayo, nti yamanya nga Katonda amusonyiye ku kye yakola eyo, era n'amubuuza obanga naye yandimusonyiye. Era n'ayimirira awo ng'agolodde omukono gwe.

Teyasobola kugukwata. N'ayimirira ng'akwatiddwa, era obusungu ne bumujjira — okujjukira Betsie, ekisenge ekiri, ne byonna. Yamanya enzikiriza ye kye yamwetaagisa, naye n'akizuula nti teyalina kwo. Bw'atyo n'asaba essaala yokka gye yasobola.

Ng'ayimiridde mu maaso g'akuumi eyabadde, ng'agolodde omukono gwe, nga ye tasobola kuyimusa gugwe, Corrie yasaba mu kasirise ekintu ekiri okumpi ne kino: Yesu, sisobola kumusonyiwa. Mpa okusonyiwa Kwo. Yali abuulidde ku kusonyiwa okwetooloola Bulaaya, naye mu kaseera ako kamu bwe kaamwetaagisibwa, teyalina kwa kwe kwennyini kwa kuwa.

Awo, ategeeza, n'akwata omukono gwe — nga tewali kuwulira, ng'ekikolwa eky'okwesalirawo so si eky'okuwulira — era bwe yakola bw'atyo, ekintu ne kiyita mu mukono gwe ne kiyingira mu mikono gyabwe egyakwatagana kye yamanya nti si kikye. Yakiyita omugga gw'okwagala, ogw'amaanyi nnyo n'okumpi okumumira. Yali ayagadde ekikolwa n'omutima omunnyogovu, era ebbugumu ne liva awalala. Kye kituufu kye yamalako obulamu bwe obwasigalawo ng'akinyweza mu bantu, era n'akyogera lwatu:

Okusonyiwa kikolwa kya kwesalirawo, era okwesalirawo kusobola okukola nga tekufaayo ku bbugumu ly'omutima.

— Corrie ten Boom, Tramp for the Lord

Okwagala kwe yasaba kwaweebwa mu kaseera ako lwe yagondera nga takulina. Kuno kwe kuddibwamu kwe yategeeza obulamu bwe bwonna: si nti Katonda yaggyawo obusungu bwe n'alyoka amuleka okusonyiwa, wabula nti yasooka kusonyiwa, mu kwesalirawo kwokka, n'okuwulira ne kumuddirira nga kuva gy'Ali. Essaala ey'omukazi eyali tasigazza kya kuwaayo yaddibwamu mu mukono gwe ye ogwagolola.

Ky'aleka

Mu 1967 Yad Vashem yakkiriza Corrie ten Boom ng'omu ku Batuukirivu mu Mawanga (Righteous Among the Nations), olw'omulimu gwa famire ogw'okukweka Abayudaaya. Mu 1971 yategeeza olugero lwonna mu The Hiding Place, olwawandiikibwa ne John ne Elizabeth Sherrill era olwalina ennyanjula eya Billy Graham; firimu n'eggoberera mu 1975. Ekitabo kyafuula omuddaabiriza wa ssaawa Omudatchi n'mwannyina eyafa okumanyibwa mu nsi yonna.

Yafiira mu Placentia, California, nga 15 April 1983 — ku lunaku lwe olw'amazaalibwa lwe yaweza emyaka kyenda mu emu, lwe lunaku lwe lumu lwe yazaalirwako. Mu buwangwa bwe waaliwo enzikiriza enkadde nti abantu abaweereddwa omukisa ogw'enjawulo bokka be bafiira ku lunaku lwabwe olw'amazaalibwa, era ka kibe nti kirina amakulu oba nedda, obulungi bwakyo tekyangu kubulaba: obulamu ne buggalibwa ku lunaku lwe bwaggukira, nga nkulungo enzijuvu erambiddwa okwetooloola emyaka kyenda mu emu.

Ky'aleka si nkola oba kitabo kya njigiriza. Kigambo kimu, ekyagezesebwa, ekyaggyibwa mu kifo ekyazimbibwa okukizikiriza: nti okusonyiwa si kuwulira kw'olindirira wabula kintu ky'okola, era nti bw'okikola n'omutima omunnyogovu n'emikono emyereere, okwagala okugijjuza kuweebwa. Betsie yasuubiza, ng'afa, nti balibeera n'ekintu eky'okubuulira kubanga baali baaliyo. Corrie yamala emyaka amakumi asatu mu esatu ng'akibuulira. Obukakafu bwe bwali mukono gwe ye, mu Munich, nga gukwata omukono gwe gwe gwali tegusobola kukwatako.
