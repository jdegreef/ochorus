Reuben Archer Torrey (1856–1928) yali muvaanjulizi w'enjiri Omunnamerika, omusumba, era omusomesa, ate nga yali mukwano gwa D. L. Moody omunywevu. Yaweereza ng'omukulu asooka ow'e Moody Bible Institute, oluvannyuma n'aba omukulu w'essomero lya Bible Institute of Los Angeles, nga akyali musumba wa Moody Church mu Chicago ne Church of the Open Door mu Los Angeles. Torrey yakulembera enkuŋŋaana z'okuzuukuka okw'eddiini ku masekkati ag'ensi agawera, era ebitabo bye ebingi ebikwata ku kusaba ne ku Mwoyo Omutukuvu byamufuula omu ku bayigiriza ba Baibuli abaasomebwa ennyo mu kiseera kye.
