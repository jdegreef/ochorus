Reuben Archer Torrey (1856–1928) yali Omwamerika omubuulizi, omusumba era omusomesa eyajja mu kukkiriza ku Yale ku kiro kye yali asazeewo okumalawo obulamu bwe. D. L. Moody yamufuula omukulembeze asooka ow’Essomero lya Baibuli erya Moody, era yali omusumba w’Ekkanisa ey’oku Chicago Avenue, oluvannyuma n’aweereza ng’omukulu asooka ow’Essomero lya Baibuli erya Los Angeles. Awamu ne Charles Alexander yabuulira eri obukadde n’obukadde mu Australia, mu Asia ne mu Bizinga bya Bungereza wakati wa 1902 ne 1905 — olugendo lwe yagamba nti lwava mu kusaba okw’oku Lwomukaaga akawungeezi. Yawandiika ebitabo ebisukka mu amakumi ana, okusinga ku kusaba ne ku Mwoyo Omutukuvu.
