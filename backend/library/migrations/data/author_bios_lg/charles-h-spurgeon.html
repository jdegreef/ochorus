Charles Haddon Spurgeon yayitibwa “Omulangira w’Ababuulizi,” era ku mulundi guno erinnya teryo kusukkirira. Okumala emyaka nga amakumi ana yayimirira mu maaso g’ekibiina ky’abantu enkumi ttaano n’omukaaga wakati wa London, nga tayimbye kaziba ka ddoboozi, n’abakwataliza kasirise n’okubuulira okwangu okwa Kristo eyakomererwa. Ebibuulwa bye byawandiikibwa mu nnukuta enkumpanya, ne bifulumizibwa buli wiiki, ne bitwalibwa mu maato okutuuka ku buli ssemazinga; okutuuka ku nkomerero y’obulamu bwe yali abuulidde oboolyawo abantu obukadde kkumi era yafulumya ebigambo ebisinga omuwandiisi omulala yenna Omukristaayo mu lulimi Olungereza. Naye omuntu eyali emabega w’ettutumu eryo emirundi mingi yali musumba abonaabona, asaba, alina omutima omuwombeefu, eyanywereza ku nsonga nti amaanyi amazima ag’obuweereza bwe tegaali ddoboozi lye yekka, wabula abantu abaali ku maviivi wansi w’ekituuti.

Omulenzi mu Muzira

Yazaalibwa nga 19 Juni 1834 mu Kelvedon, Essex, mu maka agaali gamaze okugagga mu buweereza bw’enjiri — kitaawe ne jjajjaawe bombi baali babuulizi ba Kikongregasoni, era ekitundu ekinene eky’obwana bwe kyayita mu nnyumba ya jjajjaawe mu Stambourne, wakati mu bitabo ebikadde ebya Kipuritaani ebyakola omutwe gwe okumala obulamu bwonna. Yakulira ng’amanyi enjigiriza z’ekisa mu mutima. Kye yali tannamanya ye Omulokozi yennyini. Okumala emyezi, ng’akyali muvubuka, yali wansi w’okukakasibwa okw’ekibi okunene, ng’ayaayaanira emirembe naye nga tafuna gyonna.

Enkyukakyuka yajja ku makya g’olwa Ssande omuzira lwe gwali, nga 6 Jjanwali 1850, bwe yali aweza emyaka kkumi n’etaano. Omuyaga gw’omuzira gwamuggya mu kkubo lye yali agenda ne gumuyingiza mu keerezia akatono aka Primitive Methodist mu Artillery Street, Colchester. Omubuulizi eyali ateekeddwa teyatuuka olw’omuzira, kale omuntu wa bulijjo atasoma — omuwesi w’engatto oba omutunzi olw’omulimu — yalinnya mu kituuti nga talina kya kwogera okuggyako ekyawandiikibwa kye ekimu: “Mutunuulire nze, mulokolebwe, mmwe enkomerero zonna ez’ensi” (Isaaya 45:22). Ng’akaza amaaso ge ku mulenzi oyo eyalabika ng’anakuwavu wansi w’olubalaza, yaleekaana, “Omuvubuka, tunuulira Yesu Kristo. Tunuulira! Tunuulira! Tunuulira!” Mu kaseera ako Spurgeon yatunula, n’alokolebwa.

Awo n’awo ekire ne kigenda, ekizikiza ne kiba kiveewo, era mu kaseera ako ne ndaba enjuba.

— Charles Spurgeon, ku kukyusibwa kwe

Yabatizibwa mu biseera eby’ekyeya ekyo mu mugga Lark, ng’afuuse, olw’okukakasibwa kwe kwennyini okw’essanyu, Omubatisiti. Enkyukakyuka yali ya mangu era etuukiridde: omulenzi eyali amaze ekiseera kiwanvu ng’alwana mu kizikiza kati teyayinza kusirika ku kitangaala.

Omulenzi Omubuulizi

Mu myezi mitono yali ng’ayigiriza, ng’agaba obupapula, era ng’abuulira mu byalo ebyetoolodde Cambridge. Ng’akyali mu myaka gy’obuvubuka bwe yafuuka omusumba w’akeerezia akatono mu Waterbeach, gye okubuulira kwe okw’omuliro amangu ago kwajjuza ekizimbe. Ebirabo bye tebyayinza kubeera nga bikwekeddwa kumala bbanga ddene. Mu 1854, ng’alina emyaka kkumi na mwenda gyokka, yayitibwa mu keerezia aka kyafaayo naye akaali kagenda kakendeera aka New Park Street Chapel mu London. Ekibiina ekyakuŋŋaana okuwuliriza omulenzi w’ekyalo atannagezesebwa kyali kitono; mu wiiki ntono tekyayinza kusobola bibiina. Baagaziya ekizimbe, oluvannyuma ne bapangisa Exeter Hall, oluvannyuma ne bapangisa Surrey Gardens Music Hall ennene, naye enkumi zaakyazzibwayo. London yali tennalaba kintu kifaanana ng’ekyo: omuvubuka ng’abuulira enjigiriza enkadde eya Kikalvini eri ebibiina ebijjudde, mu lulimi omuwala omuweereza gwe yayinza okugoberera, era omuyivu gw’ateyinza kunenya.

Omuliro n’Ekizikiza

Ettutumu lyaleeta ebigezo ebikaawu. Ku kawungeezi aka 19 Okitobba 1856, Spurgeon bwe yali akulembera abantu ng’obukumi bumu mu kusaba mu Surrey Gardens Music Hall, abaleeta akatabo abalina ebbeetuwa embi baaleekaana “Omuliro! Embalaza zigwa!” Tewaali muliro, naye mu kudduka okwolekera enzigi abantu musanvu baabetenteka okufa era abalala bangi baalumizibwa. Spurgeon, eyali n’emyaka amakumi abiri mu ebiri gyokka, yaggibwa mu kituuti “afiiridde okusinga okuba omulamu” era n’asaanawo mu nnaku ennene ennyo eyali kumpi okumaliriza obuweereza bwe. Mu bulamu bwe bwonna obwasigalawo yalwanagana n’okwennyamira okwaddiŋŋananga, era teyeefuula kikyamu.

Obwesigwa obwo bwafuuka kimu ku birabo bye ebikulu eri ekkanisa. Yayogera mu lwatu ku “kwennyamira okutalina nsonga” okwamugwangako, era yasanyusa enkumi z’abo abaabonaabona nga bannaabwe olw’okugaana okukweka okwe kwennyini. Enjigiriza ye ey’okubonaabona teyali ya kunyoomerera; yaweesebwa mu kyoto. Yajja okukkiriza nti okubonaabona kwe — akabi ako, olulimi, n’endwadde ya gaawuti eyaluma omubiri gwe oluvannyuma — bye byali ebintu ebyamusindika emirundi n’emirundi okunywerera ku Kristo, era ne bifuula okubuulira kwe okuwombeefu eri abamenyese.

Ekifo ky’Amaanyi

Mu 1861 ekibiina kyajja mu Metropolitan Tabernacle eyali akazimbiddwa buggya mu Newington, eyali n’ebifo eby’abantu ng’enkumi ttaano, Spurgeon gye yandibuulidde okumala emyaka amakumi asatu egyaddirira. Abagenyi baava mu nsi yonna okusoma enkola ze, nga basuubira okuzuula ekyama mu kwogera kwe okulungi. Spurgeon yamanya bulungi okusingawo. Yandibaserengudde mu kisenge ekya wansi nga ekivuga tekinnatandika era n’aggulawo oluggi ku bakumi b’abasajja abamu ab’ekkanisa lye, nga bali ku maviivi era nga beegayirira Katonda olw’okubuulira okwali kumpi okutandika.

“Oyagala okulaba ekifo ky’amaanyi eky’ekkanisa lino?” Spurgeon yandibuuzizza abagenyi — era n’abakulembera si ku kituuti wabula ku lukuŋŋaana lw’okusaba mu kisenge ekya wansi, abakumi gye baali beegayiririra abalala nga buli kusinza tekunnatandika. Yakiyita ekisenge ekyo ennyumba y’ekyoto ekya Tabernacle, era teyabuusabuusa nti kye kyali enkyukakyuka entuufu eya byonna bye yakola.

Yakkiriza nti okusaba mulimu gwennyini ogulina ebibala ebituufu, era yakubiriza abantu be okugukolerera n’amaanyi gaabwe gonna.

Okusaba kusika omuguwa wansi, n’ekide ekinene ne kivuga waggulu mu matu ga Katonda. Abamu tebasosonkoza kide n’akatono, kubanga basaba mu bunafu; abalala basika omuguwa emirundi egy’ekiseera n’ekiseera; naye awangula wamu n’eggulu ye omuntu akwata omuguwa n’obuvumu n’agusika awatali kukoma n’amaanyi ge gonna.

— Charles Spurgeon

Yali asibiddwa nnyo ku kusaba kw’ekibiina kye ne kimuleetera okubagamba mu lwatu nti teyandiyinza kweyongerayo awatali bo.

Katonda annyambe, bwe muva ku kunsabira! Muntegeeze olunaku olwo, era kinnetaagisa okulekera awo okubuulira.

— Charles Spurgeon

Okukkiriza Okuzimba

Obuweereza bwa Spurgeon tebwawugulwa ku kituuti kyokka. Mu 1856 yatandikawo Ekibiina ky’Abasumba okutendeka ababuulizi abaali babuze ssente ez’okusomesebwa okwa bulijjo, ng’ayigiriza yekka era ng’awagira abasajja bangi abaagenda okusimba ekkanisa. Yatandikawo Ekibiina kya Kolopoteji okutwala ebitabo eby’Ekikristaayo eri abaavu, yafulumya akatabo aka buli mwezi The Sword and the Trowel, era yeewaayo mu mirimu egy’ekisa kikumi.

Ennyumba y’Abaana Abatalina Bazadde eya Stockwell yazaalibwa okuva mu mwoyo gwe gumu ogw’okusaba n’okukkiriza. Ng’anakuwadde olw’embeera enzibu ey’abaana abatalina bakitaabwe, Spurgeon yali asabye Katonda amuganye okubayamba — era eky’okuddamu ne kijja mu ngeri gye yali tayinza kutegeka.

Nnamwandu ayitibwa Anne Hillyard, ng’akwatiddwa okukoowoola kwa Spurgeon, yamuwa £20,000 — obugagga — okuzimba ennyumba y’abaana abalenzi abatalina bazadde. Emitawaana gy’ensimbi bwe gyaleeta nti obusuubuzi bwe obw’eggaali y’omukka tebwayinza kusookera kufuulibwa ssente enkalu, Spurgeon yeeyongera okusaba, era ekirabo oluvannyuma lw’ekirabo ne bijja okuva eri abalala okutuusa ettaka lwe lyagulibwa n’ennyumba ne zizimbibwa. Ennyumba y’abaana yaggulwawo mu 1867, yalabirira abaana enkumi, era eyeeyongera n’omulimu gwayo n’okutuusa leero.

Teyandyawudde okubuulira kw’ekisa okuva ku kukukola. Okukkiriza kwe kumu okwajjuza Tabernacle kwazimba ekibiina, ennyumba y’abaana, n’ennyumba z’abakadde, era kwakikola, emirundi n’emirundi, ng’eky’okuddamu eri okusaba.

Okukka kw’Enjigiriza

Olutalo lwa Spurgeon olw’enkomerero olukulu lwali lw’amazima g’Ebyawandiikibwa byennyini. Mu 1887, ng’atiisiddwa okuggwaamu okwolekera okubuusabuusa ku Baibuli, okutangirira, n’amazima g’omusango wakati w’abasumba abamu Ababatisiti, yafuuwa akagombe ak’okulabula mu lukuŋŋaana lw’ebiwandiiko olwamanyibwa nga oluyombo lwa “Down-Grade” (Okukka kw’Enjigiriza). Mu kifo ky’okukkiriziganya, yeeggya mu Kibiina ky’Ababatisiti, ekyaddako okumunenya mu lwatu. Oluyombo lwamulyako emikwano era, bangi bwe baakkiriza, lwanguya okukka kwe. Naye yabala obuyinza bw’Ekigambo kya Katonda ng’obw’omuwendo okusinga omuwendo gwonna, era teyayinza kunyeenyezebwa.

Awaka

Omubiri gwe, ogwakoowa olwa gaawuti, sumagira, n’endwadde ya Bright, gwakendeera wansi w’omugugu gw’omulimu ogw’obulamu bwonna. Ng’alabiriddwa n’obwesigwa mukyala we omwagalwa Susannah — eyali yennyini mulwadde eyaddukanya ekitongole eky’okuwa ebitabo bye eri abasumba abaavu — yanoonya obuweerero mu mpewo enteefu eya Menton, mu Riviera ey’Obufaransa. Eyo, nga 31 Jjanwali 1892, ng’alina emyaka amakumi ataano mu musanvu, yafa. London yakungubaga nga ku lw’omulangira; enkumi zaayita ku mmeeza y’omubiri gwe, ne Baibuli ye eyaggulwa yagalamira ku gwo.

Yaleka emabega ebibuulwa ebiwera okusinga enkumi ssatu mu bikumi bitaano ebyafulumizibwa — ekyakyali ekitole ekisinga obunene eky’omulimu gw’omuwandiisi yenna Omukristaayo — wamu n’ebitabo ebyagalibwa nga Morning and Evening, The Treasury of David, All of Grace, ne Lectures to My Students. Oluvannyuma lw’ekyasa n’okusingawo bisomebwa n’omululu nga bwe byali edda, nga bivvuunulwa mu nnimi nnyingi, kubanga omuntu eyabiwandiika teyeebuulira yekka. Yabuulira Kristo, era yakikola ng’ali ku maviivi. Omulangira w’Ababuulizi yandyagadde kijjukirwe nti engule ye yali ya kwewola, era nti ekyama ky’ekifo ky’amaanyi kyali kya lwatu eri buli anaakwata omuguwa n’agusika.
