Watchman Nee, eyazaalibwa mu 1903 mu Foochow, mu Cayina, yali omuntu ow'amaanyi mu Bukristaayo obw'ekyasa 20 ne mu bukulembeze bw'ekkanisa y'Abacayina. Yakkiriza Obukristaayo ng'aweza emyaka 17, yawaayo obulamu bwe eri obuweereza, era n'awandiika ebitabo bingi eby'amaanyi nga "Obulamu Obwa Bulijjo obw'Omukristaayo." Nga ayigganyizibwa olw'okukkiriza kwe, yafiira mu 1972 ng'akyali mu kkomera ng'aweza emyaka 69. Omugabo gwe gukyeyongera okuyita mu kibiina kya Local Church mu nsi yonna. Enjigiriza ya Nee yassa essira ku bituufu eby'omwoyo eby'ekkanisa n'ekifo omukkiriza w'ali mu Kristo.
