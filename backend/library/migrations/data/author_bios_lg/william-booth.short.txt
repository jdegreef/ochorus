William Booth, eyazaalibwa mu 1829 mu Nottingham, mu Bungereza, yali omutandisi wa Eggye ery'Obulokozi. Ng'okusooka yali musumba Omumesodiisi, yawulira ng'ayitiddwa okuweereza abaavu ab'omu bibuga era n'atandika obuweereza bwe mu East End e London. Mu 1865, yatandikawo Christian Mission, oluvannyuma eyafuuka Eggye ery'Obulokozi mu 1878. Booth ne mukyala we Catherine baakulembera enkola empya ez'okubuulira n'okuyamba abantu, nga batandikawo amaka g'okusuza, ebifo eby'okuzza obuggya, n'ebifo eby'okugabira ekyokulya.
