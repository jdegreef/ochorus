Frederick Brotherton Meyer yazaalibwa mu London nga 8 Apuli 1847, mu maka ag'emirembe era agatya Katonda. Olugero lw'okukyusibwa kwe telwali lwa kwewuunyisa wadde olw'okuva mu bwavu; yakulira mu kitiibwa ky'abantu abagenda mu kkanisa, yasoma ku Brighton College, era n'afuna dipuloma ye mu Yunivasite ya London mu 1869 nga tannaba kutendekebwa mu buweereza bwa Ababaputiisiti ku Regent's Park College. Naye ekkubo eggolokofu ery'omuvubuka omuweereza eyakulira obulungi mangu ago lyandizibudde ne litandika ekintu ekigazi era eky'ekitalo okusinga bye yali asobola okuteekateeka. Mu myaka nkaaga egy'okubuulira, Meyer yandifuuse emu ku ddoboozi ery'Abavanjeri erisinga okwagalibwa mu kiseera kye, mukwano gwa D. L. Moody, empagi y'Olukuŋŋaana lwa Keswick, era omusajja empapula z'amawulire ze zandimutuumye, ekitundu mu kusaaga n'ekitundu mu kunyiikira, "Omulabirizi Omukulu ow'Amakanisa Ageetwala."

Omuweereza Omuvubuka n'Ekiseera eky'Enkyukakyuka

Obuweereza bwa Meyer obw'olubereberye bwali ku Pembroke Baptist Chapel mu Liverpool mu 1870, ne buddirira mu 1872 Priory Street Baptist Church mu York. Kyali mu York, nga akyali muvubuka, gye yasisinkana omuvanjeri Omunnamerika Dwight L. Moody, eyali tamanyiddwa nnyo mu Bungereza mu kiseera ekyo. Meyer yaggulirawo Moody enzigi mu makanisa n'abaweereza b'Abangereza, era bombi ne bafuuka mikwano gy'obulamu bwonna. Omukwano ogwo gwaleka akabonero mu Meyer; oluvannyuma yagamba nti obubuguma n'obwesimbu bwa Moody bwamulaga Obukristaayo obusinga obunene ku okwo okutya Katonda kwe okw'obwegendereza kwe kwali kunnyonnyoddwa okutuusa mu kiseera ekyo.

Naye essaawa ey'okusalawo mu bulamu bwe teyajja ng'eyita mu linnya erimanyiddwa naye ng'eyita mu muvubuka omuzannyi wa kuliketi. Mu Leicester, Meyer gye yali agenze okukulira Victoria Road Church n'oluvannyuma okuteekawo omulimu ogw'obwannannyini ku Melbourne Hall, yasisinkana Charles T. Studd n'abamisiyoneri abavubuka abaali bagenda ku China Inland Mission. Essanyu lyabwe eritaliiko bire lyayolesa ekintu Meyer kye yali abulako. Yabuuza engeri gye yandifunye kye baalina, ne Studd n'amugamba nti kyali kya kwewaayo eri Kristo si mu bujjuvu wabula mu bujjuvu obw'ekigambo — nga tekuli kye yeekuumira. Meyer yafukamira era, mu ngeri ye ey'okunnyonnyola erabika obulungi, n'akwasa Kristo empeta ey'ekyuma eyalina ebisumuluzo byonna eby'obulamu bwe, okuggyako ekisumuluzo kimu ekitono kye yeekuumira mu ngalo olw'ekyewuunya ekyekusifu eky'omutima gwe.

Bw'oba tonneesiga mu byonna, toneesiga n'akatono.

— F. B. Meyer

Teyasobola kugamba nti yali mwetegefu okuwaayo ekisumuluzo ekyo eky'enkomerero. Kye yasobola okugamba kyafuuka emu ku njatula ezisinga okujuliza mu kwewaayo kw'Abavanjeri — okusaba okw'amazima okw'omusajja eyali mwesimbu okusukka okwefuula okwewaayo kwe yali tannaba kuwulira.

Nga tasobola kuleka kisumuluzo ekyo kimu kye yali yeekuumidde, Meyer yasaba nti, "Siri mwetegefu, naye ndi mwetegefu okufuulibwa omwetegefu." Kyamulabikira nga Kristo yaggya ekisumuluzo mu ngalo ze n'agenda butereevu ku kyewuunya ekyaggalwa. Mu wiiki emu, Meyer yajulira, Mukama yali akyeyawulidde ddala n'akijjuza ekintu ekisinga obulungi ennyo — nga amayinja ag'obulimba gaawaanyisibwa n'ag'amazima.

Obuweereza n'Ebibala Byabwo

Okuva mu kwewaayo okwo kwe kwakulukuta obuweereza Meyer bwe ajjukirwa. Ku Melbourne Hall mu Leicester (1878–1888) ekkuŋŋaaniro lye lyakula ne lifuuka enkumi n'enkumi, era okuva mu 1888 yasenguka okudda mu London, ng'agabanya emyaka amakumi asatu egyaddirira wakati wa Regent's Park Chapel ne Christ Church, Lambeth. Yabuulira okwetooloola Bungereza ne Amerika, mu Afrika ey'Obumbi ne mu Buvanjuba obw'Obumbi, era n'afuuka omu ku bayigiriza abasinga okunoonyezebwa mu Lukuŋŋaana lwa Keswick, obulamu obw'"ekitiibwa" obw'okwewongera n'okwesiga Omwoyo we bwanywezebwa eri obukumi bw'enkumi.

Okutukuzibwa kwa Meyer tekwali kwa munda kwokka. Yalwana olutalo olutakoowa ku bibi eby'omu bantu eby'ekibuga kye — obutamiivu, obuzannyi bw'okusuula ensimbi, n'obusuubuzi bw'obwenzi. Wakati w'ekitundu ky'emyaka gya 1890 ne 1907, entalo ze zibalibwa okuggalawo ebkumi by'amayumba g'obwenzi n'amayumba g'omwenge, era n'azimba mu buweereza bwe ekibiina ky'okuyamba abasibe, amaka g'abaavu ennyo, n'omulimu wakati w'abaavu abasinga mu bitundu bya London. Abamuvuganya baamusekerera nga "Meyer Ayingirira, Alina Enneewulira Enkalubo," naye teyayimirira. Eri Meyer, obulagirizi n'okusaasira byali kimu: Katonda eyakulembera emmeeme okuyingira mu mirembe naye ye yakulembera omukono okuyingira mu bitundu by'abaavu.

Okutuuka kwe kwali kugazi mu ngeri ey'okwewuunya eri omusajja eyatandika ng'omuweereza Omubaputiisiti ow'obuntu obwa bulijjo mu bitundu by'ebyalo. Melbourne Hall, omulimu gwe yatandikawo mu Leicester, gwakula ne gufuuka gumu ku makuŋŋaaniro amanene ag'olubereberye ag'omu kiseera kino, obwammemba bwagwo nga bukula ne bufuuka enkumi n'enkumi abaasikirizibwa okubuulira kwe okw'olwatu era okw'okwagala. Olugendo lwe olw'omu Amerika, olwasooka okukolebwa nga ali ne Moody n'oluvannyuma yekka, lwatwala eddoboozi lye emitala wa Atlantiki emirundi n'emirundi. Ku lugendo olw'omu Afrika ey'Obumbi ne mukyala we mu 1908 yasisinkana Mohandas Gandhi n'amala ennaku eziwera nga bwogera naye, ng'ayolesa okusaasira okw'obwegendereza eri enzikiriza ya Gandhi ey'okuziyiza awatali maanyi — akabonero akatono naye akalimu amakulu ak'omusajja eyalina obwegendereza n'okusaasira ebyagaana okusibwa mu nnono ye ye.

Yali, okusinga byonna, omuwandiisi eyayigiriza Abakristaayo aba bulijjo okutambula ne Katonda. Yakola ebitabo ebisukka nsanvu, nga mw'otwalidde ebyafaayo eby'Ekyawandiikibwa eby'okwagala ebya Ibulayimu, Musa, Eriya, Peetero, ne Pawulo, n'ebitabo eby'okwewaayo ebisinga obulungi ebikyasomebwa. Mu The Secret of Guidance, ekitabo kye Ochorus ky'etwala, omutima gwe ogw'obuweereza guwulirika mu bujjuvu.

Amaanyi n'emirembe gyaffe biesigamira ku kumanya Katonda w'ayagala tubeere n'okubeerawo.

— F. B. Meyer

Yawandiikira abo abeeraliikirivu n'abalina emitima emizibu okusinga abo ab'obuvumu, ng'anyweza nti Katonda akkakkanira omwana asinga obuzito ayagala ddala okugondera. Amagezi ge ku kusaba tegaali nkola wabula mbeera ya kwesiga — nti ebituufu bijja okusooka, okukkiriza kuwummulira ku byo, n'enneewulira ne zigoberera mu kiseera kya Katonda ye.

Ebituufu bya Katonda, ebiteekeddwa ng'omusingi ogw'ejjinja eritakyuka. Okukkiriza kwaffe, nga kukwata ne kuwummuliramu. Enneewulira ez'essanyu, nga zijja mu kaseera kamu oba oluvannyuma lw'okuyitawo kw'ennaku n'emyezi, nga Katonda bw'ayagala.

— F. B. Meyer

Ebigezo Ebyayolekedwa n'Okukkiriza

Obulamu bwa Meyer obw'olwatu bwali buwanvu era bulimu ebibala, naye teyeefuula ng'atateganyizibwa. Yali mwesimbu, mu kuyigiriza kwe ne mu bbaluwa ze, ku ntalo ez'omunda omuweereza z'alwana nga tazibonabona lwatu. Olutalo lwe yajatula ennyo lwali obuggya. Yaweereza mu kibuga kimu n'ababuulizi ababibiina byabwe n'ettutumu byabwe byasukka bibye, era n'akkiriza nti omutima gwe gwandikwatiddwa obuggya olw'abo. Eddagala lye lyali lye limu lye yabuulira abalala: yatwala ekibi ekyo mu maviivi n'asaba mu bugenderevu olw'obuwanguzi bw'abavuganya be, ng'asaba Katonda nti abantu abasingako ne bookka bandikuŋŋaanidde okujja okubawuliriza, okutuusa obuggya lwe bwalekebwa awatali mmere n'okwagala ne kuzzibwawo. Bwali buwanguzi butono, obw'ekyama, naye obwayolesa emisingi gye gimu egy'ebisumuluzo ebyawaayo — nti ekkubo ery'okuva mu mutima ogusibiddwa kwe kukwasa Kristo n'obukyayi bw'omuntu.

Yasitula era, omugugu ogwa bulijjo ogw'omusajja eyawulira olw'okubonaabona kw'abalala. Mu kiseera ky'Olutalo Olusooka Olunene olw'Ensi Yonna yasomoka okugenda mu Bufalansa ne Omukweeka Hubert Peet okwerabira yekka engeri abaziyiza olutalo olw'omuntu ku bubwe abasibe gye baali bakolebwako, era n'awaayo eddoboozi lye olw'abo abatayagalibwa n'abagobebwa mu kiseera lwe kyandibadde kyangu okusigala nga musirise. Yamanya kye kyali okutategeerebwa, era n'addamu obutategeerebwa si na bukyayi wabula n'omulimu omungi.

Enkula yonna ey'obuweereza bwa Meyer yali, gy'ali, okuddamu okw'okwewaayo kwe yakola mu Leicester. Ng'awaddeyo ekisumuluzo eky'enkomerero, yasanga okunywera n'obukakanyavu bw'okutya Katonda kwe okw'olubereberye nga byawaanyisibwa emirembe emiteefu n'omugaso ogugaziwa gwe yali tannaba kwekolera. Emirundi mingi yagamba nti emaanu egwa awo wokka ekire we kiwummulira — nti Katonda agabirira omuweereza ali we yakulemberwa okubeera — era n'abala emyaka gye egyakulukuta ng'obukakafu nti ekisuubizo kyanywera.

Okufa n'Obusika

Meyer yabuulira n'atambula okumpi n'enkomerero. Mu Marisi 1929, ennaku entono nga olunaku lwe olw'amazaalibwa olw'omyaka kinaana mu ebiri terunnatuuka, yategeera nti okufa kwe kwali kumpi. Ebbaluwa gye yaweereza mukwano gwe eyawangaala okusinga ebibuulira bye bingi ennyo, era esomebwa mu ngeri y'emu ng'omusajja eyamala obulamu bwe emitala w'okwewaayo bwe yandisuubiddwa okuwandiika.

Naakawulira, nga kya kwewuunya nnyo gye ndi, nti nsigazza ennaku ntono okubeera omulamu. Kiyinza okuba nti eno nga tennakutuukako, nnaaba nyingidde mu lubiri. Toteganyizibwa kuwandiika. Tunaasisinkana ku makya.

— F. B. Meyer

Yafa nga 28 Marisi 1929. The Daily Telegraph yamutuuma "Omulabirizi Omukulu ow'Amakanisa Ageetwala," era okwetooloola ensi eyogera Olungereza yakungubagirwa ng'omuzimbi w'amatendekero eyanyweza wamu Obwavanjeri n'obw'omu bantu, ekya munda ennyo n'ekya lwatu olw'obugazi, mu bulamu bumu obutali bwa mbiro. Ebitabo bye byasigala nga bifulumizibwa; oluyimba lwe olw'okwewaayo ne lweyongera okuyimbibwa; era emirembe gy'abakkiriza abateefu abataamuwulira ng'abuulira baasanga, mu mpapula ze yaleka, mukwano gwabwe eyategeera entiisa yaabwe n'abalungamya mpola eri obulagirizi bwa Katonda.

Kye Meyer kye yaleka eri ekkanisa tekyali nkola wabula mbeera — omukono omuggule ogutakuuma kisumuluzo kyonna, okwatula okwesiga Katonda nga tekunnabeera kwetegefu, n'obukakafu obunywevu nti Oyo akulembera omutambuze era y'anaamuliisa mu kkubo. "Bwe tuba we Kitaffe ow'omu ggulu w'ayagala tubeere," yawandiika, "tuli bakakafu nti Ye anaatuwa ebyetaago." Yassa obulamu bwe ku ekyo, era nga buli bujulizi bwe bugamba, teyalemwa ssuubi.
