David Martyn Lloyd-Jones (1899–1981), eyamanyibwanga nga “Omusawo,” yali musawo muto wa magezi mu London — omuyambi w'omusawo wa Kabaka, Sir Thomas Horder — eyaleka obusawo n'agenda mu buweereza, ng'akkirizza nti endwadde y'omuntu esinga obunene ya mwoyo. Oluvannyuma lw'emyaka kkumi n'egumu ng'alunda abakozi e Sandfields mu South Wales ey'amakolero, yabuulira mu ngeri ey'okunnyonnyola mu Westminster Chapel, London, okumala emyaka amakumi asatu era n'afuuka omuntu wa maanyi mu kuzzibwawo kw'okubuulira kwa Reformed mu kyasa eky'amakumi abiri.
