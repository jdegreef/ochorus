John Calvin (1509–1564) yali omusomesa w’ebya Katonda asinga obukulu ow’omulembe ogwokubiri ogw’Okuzza Obuggya era omusumba w’e Geneva okumala emyaka egisukka amakumi abiri. Nga munnamateeka Omufaransa era humanist eyafuuka omuzzi w’obuggya oluvannyuma “lw’okukyuka okw’amangu,” yawandiika Institutes of the Christian Religion — ennyanjula esinga obujjuvu ey’okukkiriza okwa Reformed — era n’akola ku by’Obuporotesitanti okwetooloola Bulaaya ng’ayita mu kubuulira kwe, ennyonnyola ze ku kumpi Ebyawandiikibwa byonna, ne mu Academy of Geneva.
