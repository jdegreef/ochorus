Christmas Evans (1766–1838) ye yali omusinga ku babuulizi b'e Wales — omukozi wa nnimiro omwavu eyeeyigiriza yekka, eyaziba eriiso limu olw'okulumbibwa mu buvubuka bwe, eyamanyibwa nga “Bunyan wa Wales.” Okumala emyaka egisukka amakumi asatu yalabirira Ekkanisa za Ababatisita ezaali zisaasaanidde mu Anglesey ng'apeebwa £17 buli mwaka, ng'abuulira enkumi n'enkumi mu mbuulira ez'ebweru ennyumirivu era ezisikiriza. Obuweereza bwe bwazzibwamu obulamu oluvannyuma lw'endagaano eyaatiikirira eyakola ne Katonda wansi wa Cadair Idris.
