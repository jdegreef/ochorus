Richard Allen (1760–1831) yazaalibwa mu buddu mu Philadelphia, n'atundibwa ng'akyali muto eri omulimi ow'e Delaware, era n'akyukira Kristo ng'akyali muvubuka wansi w'okubuulira kw'Abamesodi. Mukama we bwe yakyuka naye n'amuleka okwegula eddembe lye, Allen n'afuuka omubuulizi omutambuze eyakola omulimu ogutali gwa bulijjo; okunyoomebwa okw'entebe ezaawuddwa mu St. George's Methodist Church kwamuleetera okutandikawo ekkanisa y'Abaddugavu eyeeyimirizaawo, Mother Bethel, mu Philadelphia. Mu 1816 yatandikawo era n'afukibwako amafuta ng'omulabirizi omukulu asooka ow'ekibiina African Methodist Episcopal Church, ekibiina ky'eddiini eky'Abaddugavu ekisooka okweyimirizaawo obulambalamba mu Amerika.
