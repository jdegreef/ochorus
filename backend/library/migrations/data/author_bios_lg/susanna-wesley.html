Susanna Wesley teyabuulira kubuulira kwonna, teyafulumya kitabo kyonna, era teyatandika kkanisa yonna. Yamala obulamu bwe bwonna munda mu bisenge by'ennyumba y'omusumba mu kifo ekitereevu ekya Lincolnshire, ng'azaala abaana n'abaziika, ng'abayigiriza ennukuta zaabwe n'okusaba kwabwe, ng'akwatagganya amaka wamu wakati mu bwavu, omuliro, n'okwawukana. Naye okuva mu ffumbiro eryo ery'abulijjo mwavaayo obulamu bubiri ku obwo obwaleeta ebibala ebisinga obunene mu byafaayo by'Ekikristaayo, era ekibiina kyonna ne kiyiga okumuyita nnyina waakyo. Luno lugero lwa kisa ekyakwekebwa mu mirimu gya buli lunaku, olw'okukkiriza okwasisinkana ennaku ey'amazima awatali kukankana, n'olw'okusaba okwalukibwa nnyo mu ssaawa okutuusa lwe kwafuuka enzimba yennyini ey'amaka.

Muwala w'Omuwakanyi w'eddiini

Yazaalibwa nga Susanna Annesley mu London ku 20 Jjanwari 1669, omwana asembayo mu baana nga amakumi abiri mu bataano aba Dokita Samuel Annesley, omusumba omukulu ow'Abawakanyi b'eddiini. Yakulira mu maka ag'okuyiga n'okukakasa okw'amaanyi, era bwe yatuuka ku myaka kkumi n'esatu yali, mu ngeri ey'okwewuunya, amaze okulowooza okutuuka wakati mu mpaka ez'ekiseera kye n'okwerondera yekka Ekkanisa y'e Bungereza mu kifo ky'Obuwakanyi bwa kitaawe. Empisa eyo ey'edda ey'okukkiriza okwakeberebwa era okwali kwokka teyamuvaako n'akatono. Mu 1688, ng'alina emyaka kkumi n'omwenda, yafumbirwa Samuel Wesley, omusumba ow'omululu era omuzibu, era oluvannyuma lw'ekiseera n'abeera naye mu nnyumba y'omusumba e Epworth, gye yali agenda okumaliriza ennaku ze ezaasigalawo.

Obufumbo bwabwe tebwali bwangu, era Susanna teyeefuula ng'ekirala n'akatono. Ssente zaali za bbula bulijjo; Samuel yasibibwa mu kkomera olw'ebbanja; obusungu bwe n'obubwe byandiyinza okukoonagana. Mu 1701 ab'ombi baagwa mu kuwakana okw'amaanyi Susanna, olw'omuntu we ow'omunda, bwe yagaana okwogera nti “Amiina” ku kusaba kwa bba olwa Kabaka William, gwe yali atalaba nga kabaka wa Bungereza ow'amazima. Samuel yalayira obutabeera naye okutuusa lw'anaakyusa, era okumala emyezi egiwerako yava mu maka ddala. Kwali kwawukana okw'obulumi era okwa lukale, era kulaga omukazi ataali wa kuwaayo muntu we gw'omunda newakubadde eri bba gwe yali asibiddwa okuwa ekitiibwa. Okwawukana kwakoma, era omwana waabwe asembayo, Charles, yazaalibwa oluvannyuma lw'okutabagana kwabwe; naye Susanna yasitula endowooza ze awatali kukutama okutuusa ku nkomerero y'obulamu bwe.

Ennyumba ey'abaana abangi, n'amalaalo amangi

Mu baana kkumi n'omwenda be yazaala, mwenda baafa nga bawere, nga mwe muli abalongo emirundi ebiri, era omu yattibwa mu bujjeboona omukyala omulabirizi eyali akooye. Munaana bokka be baawangaala okutuuka mu bukulu. Tekisoboka kusoma bulamu bwe awatali kukwatagana n'obuzito bwa baana abaaziikibwa, era Susanna teyakikweka mu madda g'okutya Katonda. Yakungubaga, era n'agenda mu maaso, era ne yeefuka ku abo abaasigalawo. Yamalirira okuyigiriza buli mwana yekka, era n'akikola n'empisa eyafuuka ey'ettutumu. Essomero lyatandika ku lunaku olw'okuzaalibwa okw'omwana olw'emyaka etaano; ennukuta zonna zaayigibwa mu lunaku lumu; buli nkya ne kawungeezi baabikkulanga ne baggalanga n'okusoma ennyimba za Zabbuli n'Ebyawandiikibwa. Yali akwatagana nti omulimu ogusooka ogw'omuzadde Omukristaayo kwe kuyigiriza omwana okugonda, era n'akiwandiika mu bweru.

Okusobola okukola ebirowoozo by'abaana, ekintu ekisooka ekiteekwa okukolebwa kwe kuwangula okwagala kwabwe n'okubaleeta mu mpisa ey'okugonda. Okumanyisa okutegeera mulimu gwa kiseera, era ku baana guteekwa okugenda mpolampola; naye okufuga okwagala kintu ekiteekwa okukolebwa mu mulundi gumu, era gye kisinga okwanguwa gye kisinga obulungi.

— Susanna Wesley, ebbaluwa eri John Wesley, 1732

Mu matu ag'ekiseera kino kino kiyinza okuwulikika ng'ekikakali, era kyali kya buzibu; naye ekigendererwa kye tekyali kufuga kwokka. Yayagala buli mwana abeere wa ddembe okuva mu buyinza obw'okwagala kw'omuntu ku bubwe waamu, ekyandireetera amagezi n'eddiini okumera. Era yagattirira obuzibu n'obwesigwa obw'ekitalo obw'obwegendereza. Mu maka amanene yateeka wabbali akawungeezi akaakakati buli sabbiiti okuba yekka ne buli mwana mu myalu, okwogera nabo ku myoyo gyabwe kinnoomu. Emyaka egiyita, mutabani we John eyali afuuse omukulu bwe yamusaba okuwandiika enkola ye, yakikola, era ebbaluwa gye yamuweereza mu 1732 yafuuka emu ku bawandiiko abasingayo okukuumibwa ku kulera Ekikristaayo. Emabega w'enkola waaliwo okukkiriza ku kiki omwana kye yali ddala.

Sisobola okuggyaako okutunuulira buli mwoyo gw'oleka wansi w'obulabirizi bwange nga ttalanta gye nkwasibbwa nga kya bwesige okuva eri Mukama omukulu.

— Susanna Wesley

Ekisiki ekyasikulwa okuva mu muliro

Endowooza eyo ey'okukwasibwa okutukuvu yakumibwa okusinga bwe yayakiriza olw'ekintu ekyajja okukola olugero lw'amaka ge. Mu Febwali 1709 ennyumba y'omusumba e Epworth yakwatibwa omuliro ekiro. Ab'omu maka baddukira mu kizikiza, era yasooka bwe baali bakuŋŋaanye ebweru ne bategeera nti omwana omu, John ow'emyaka etaano, yali akyakwatiddwa ku ddirisa ery'engulu. Baliraanwa baakola eddaala ery'abantu ne bamusikayo edda edda nga akasolya tekannagwa munda. Susanna yaddiza omwana we ng'omuntu azuukiziddwa okuva mu bafu, era teyakyerabira. John yennyini yandyogedde ku kuba “ekisiki ekyasikulwa okuva mu muliro” obulamu bwe bwonna obwasigala. Mu mpapula ze ez'ekyama nnyina yawandiika okumalirira ku mulenzi gwe yali kumpi okufiirwa.

Nteekateeka okwegendereza ennyo obulungi omwoyo gw'omwana ono, gwe Ggwe wateekateeka mu kisa ekingi, okusinga bwe nnali bulijjo, ndyoke nkole obunyiikivu bwange okuteeka mu birowoozo bye ensonga z'eddiini yo ey'amazima n'obulungi. Mukama, mpa ekisa okukikola n'obwesimbu n'amagezi, era owe omukisa okugezaako kwange n'obuwanguzi obulungi.

— Susanna Wesley, okufumiitiriza okw'ekyama

Wansi w'ekyambalo

Eri Susanna Wesley, okusaba tekwali kifo eky'okuddukira eky'ekiseera n'ekiseera; kwali mpisa eyalongoosa buli kintu ekirala. Wakati mu luyoogaano n'okunyigirizaganya kw'ennyumba ejjudde abaana, yateeka wabbali obudde buli lunaku okuba yekka ne Katonda, era n'akukuuma n'amaanyi. Bwe yali tasobola kuva mu kisenge, yasituzanga bwerere ekyambalo kye ekiwanvu ku mutwe gwe, era buli mwana mu nnyumba yamanya nti Maama bwe yali wansi w'ekyambalo kye yali ne Katonda era teyalina kutaataaganyizibwa okuggyako mu kwetaaga okusingayo obungi. Wansi w'eweema eyo ey'ekinnansi yasoma Bayibuli ye, yakebera omuntu we ow'omunda, era n'asabira ab'omu maka ge amannya.

Okusinziira ku bye yategeeza yennyini Susanna Wesley yateeka wabbali ng'essaawa bbiri eza buli lunaku olw'okusinza okw'ekyama, ng'agaba obudde wakati w'okusaba olw'omwoyo gwe yennyini n'okusaba olw'buli omu ku baana be kinnoomu. Olw'obutasobola kufuna kisenge kya kasiriikiriivu mu nnyumba ejjudde bwe etyo, yeekolera kimu ng'asitula ekyambalo kye ku mutwe gwe nga kabonero nti yali mu kusaba; abaana be baayiga okukiwa ekitiibwa mu kasiriikiriivu. Yabala okusaba ng'ekyokulwanyisa ekikulu eri okukemebwa, n'okufumiitiriza “awatali kugerageranya y'engeri esinga obulungi ey'okutukuza okwagala kwaffe, okunyweza okusalawo kwaffe, n'okwongera amaanyi ku kumalirira kwaffe okw'okutya Katonda.”

Okuddibwamu kw'okusaba okwo kwajja mu ngeri ze yasobola okulaba. Omwana eyasikulwa okuva mu nnimi z'omuliro yakula n'afuuka John Wesley, era oluvannyuma lwe Charles, era wakati waabwe baasitula enjiri ey'okuzaalibwa okuggya okubuna Bungereza n'ebifo bya Amerika, nga bakoleeza okuzuukuka okw'enjiri okukulu era nga batandika ekibiina ky'Obumesodisiti ekya leero kibalibwa mu bukadde mu makumi. Obubonero bw'engalo za Susanna buli wonna kw'ebbo: mu “nkola” yaayo, eyazaalibwa mu nnyumba ye y'omusumba eyalongoosebwa; mu kwagala kwayo ennyimba ne Zabbuli; mu kunyweza kwayo nti eddiini nsonga y'omutima ogufuulibwa omuggya.

Okununulibwa kwa John ow'emyaka etaano okuva mu nnyumba y'omusumba eyayaka mu 1709 kwali, eri nnyina, obulabirizi bwa Katonda obw'omulembe, era yakuddamu si mu kusanyuka kwokka wabula mu kwewaayo, ng'amalirira okuba “omwegendereza ennyo obulungi omwoyo gw'omwana ono” Katonda gwe yali ateekateeka “mu kisa ekingi bwe kityo.” Okusaba kwaddibwamu okusukka ekintu kyonna kye yandisabye: omulenzi gwe yawonga ku ddirisa yafuuka omubuulizi obuweereza bwe, ne muganda we Charles, bwe bwazuukusa eggwanga era ne buzaala ekibiina eky'ensi yonna. Nnyina eyali tasobola kubuulira, ku nkomerero, yabuulira, ng'ayita mu batabani be.

Omubuulizi mu ffumbiro

Waliwo endabika endala emu okuva e Epworth eyeekwatagana n'olugero luno. Mu kiseera ekiwanvu bba lwe yali tayo, Susanna yatandika okukuba okusinza okwa kawungeezi ku Ssande mu ffumbiro ery'ennyumba y'omusumba olw'abaana be yennyini, ng'asoma okubuulira n'okuyimba Zabbuli. Abaweereza baasaba okujja, oluvannyuma baliraanwa, okutuusa ekibiina ekitono bwe kyazimba okutuuka ku myoyo bibiri, ebisingako ku byo ebyajjuza ekkanisa y'ekitundu. Ekigambo bwe kyatuuka ku Samuel n'awakanya nti tekyali kya kusaanira eri omukazi, yaddamu nti teyandisobodde mu muntu we ow'omunda okuleka obulungi obwali bwa lwatu bwe butyo, era nti singa yali ayagala ddala kikomekkerezebwe kimulagira lwatu era addemu mu maaso ga Katonda. Teyakikola. Mu mulembe ogutaawa bakazi kifo kya kubuuliriza kyonna, Susanna Wesley yali, awatali kusala magezi, afuuse omubuulizi owa ngeri emu eri kyalo kye.

Yimba oluyimba lw'okutendereza

Emyaka gye egy'enkomerero gyali egy'obusiru okusinga era, ku nkomerero, egyakoleddwa akabonero k'emirembe gye yali anoonyezza ekiseera ekiwanvu. Yafa ku 23 Julaayi 1742, ng'alina emyaka nsanvu mu esatu, ng'alina abaana be abaakula abawerako abakuŋŋaanye ku ndagala ye. Nga akamwa ke tekannalemwa yabawa ekiragiro eky'enkomerero ekyakuŋŋaanya obulamu bwe bwonna mu sentensi emu.

Baana, amangu nga mmaze okusumululwa, muyimbe oluyimba lw'okutendereza eri Katonda.

— Susanna Wesley, ebigambo bye eby'enkomerero

Era bwe yagenda ne bakikola. John Wesley, eyabuulira ku kuziikibwa kwe e Bunhill Fields eri kye yayita ekimu ku bikuŋŋaaniro eby'ekitiibwa okusinga bye yali alabyeko, yawandiika akaseera ako mu Jaana ye: “awatali kulwana kwonna, oba akabonero, oba okusinda, omwoyo ne guteebwa mu ddembe.” Yagalamizibwa okuwummula wakati w'Abawakanyi n'abatukuvu b'ekifo ekyo ekyatutumuka eky'okuziika, ekifo ekisaanira omuwala w'Omuwakanyi w'eddiini eyayita n'ayingira mu Kkanisa era n'akiwa ebimuli bibiri ku byo ebyamasamasa okusinga.

Susanna Wesley teyaleka jjinja lyonna eddene ery'okukolebwa kwe yennyini. Yaleka ekintu ekitali kya bulijjo: ennyumba eyalongoosebwa bwe etyo olw'okwagala n'okusaba okutuusa Katonda n'azimba okuzuukuka okuva mu yo. Ajjukiza buli mukkiriza ow'abulijjo, era naddala buli muzadde akooye, nti obwakabaka bwa Katonda emirundi mingi tebujja nga buyita mu bituuti n'ebibiina by'abantu wabula nga buyita mu bwesigwa mu bisenge ebitono, nga buyita mu maama wansi w'ekyambalo kye ng'ayimusa abaana be amannya mu maaso g'entebe ey'obwakabaka ey'ekisa. Batabani be baakyusa ensi; naye ye yabayigiriza okusaba, era n'abayigiriza okuyimba, era ekyo kyali kimala.
