Billy Graham (1918–2018) yali omubuulizi w’enjiri Omumerika, omusumba wa Southern Baptist, eyabuulira enjiri mu maaso gaabwe — okusinziira ku kubalirira kw’ekibiina kye — abantu nga obukadde 215 mu nsi ezisukka 185, ng’amaliriza buli lukuŋŋaana olunene n’oluyimba “Just As I Am.” Yatandikawo Billy Graham Evangelistic Association, n’ateekawo mu 1948 obukuumi bw’obwesimbu obwa Modesto obwakuuma obuweereza bwe nga tebuliimu kavuyo.
