Martin Luther (1483–1546) yali omufulente Omugirimaani, eyazza obuggya enjigiriza ey'okuweebwa obutuukirivu olw'okukkiriza kwokka, okwo ne kukoleeza Okuzza Obuggya kw'Abaputotesitanti. Entiisa mu kibuyaga eky'enkuba n'emutwala mu nnyumba y'abafulente, ate olunyiriri olwali mu Baruumi ne lumutuusa mu mirembe; yateeka ensonga ze ekyenda mu ttaano mu 1517, yawakanya paapa ne kabaka omukulu ku Lukiiko lwa Worms, yavvuunula Endagaano Empya mu Lujerumaani e Wartburg, era n'awa Ekkanisa ennyimba n'ekatekisimu zaayo. Ebigambo bye ebisembayo bye yawandiika byali nti, “Tuli basabiriza. Kino kya mazima.”
