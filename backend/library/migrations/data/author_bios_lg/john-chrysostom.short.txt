John Chrysostom (c. 347–407) yali omubuulizi asinga bonna mu Kkanisa ey'edda, gwe baayita omumwa ogwa zaabu emirembe egyaddirira olw'ebibuulirwa ebyajjuza amakanisa mu Antioch ne Constantinople. Yali muwozi eyaleka embuga z'amateeka, n'abuulira olunyiriri ku lunyiriri okuyita mu bitabo bya Bayibuli byonna era n'awakanya awatali kusonyiwa obugagga n'okwemalira, ekyamuggyako ekifo kye eky'obulabirizi. Yafiira mu 407 ng'ali mu lugendo olw'empaka olw'okugenda mu buwaŋŋanguse, ng'alina ku mimwa gye okutendereza okwali ekigambo kye eky'obulamu: Katonda atenderezebwe olwa buli kintu.
