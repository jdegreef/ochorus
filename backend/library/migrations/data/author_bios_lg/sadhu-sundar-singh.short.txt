Omubuulizi w’enjiri Omuyindi eyazaalibwa mu maka g’Abasiiki, eyasisinkana Kristo mu kwolesebwa mu 1904 n’afuuka sadhu Omukristaayo atambula n’ebigere ebyereere, ng’abuulira okubuna Buyindi, okutuuka mu Tibet, n’okwetooloola ensi, okutuusa lwe yabula ku lugendo lwe olw’enkomerero mu nsozi za Himalaya mu 1929.
