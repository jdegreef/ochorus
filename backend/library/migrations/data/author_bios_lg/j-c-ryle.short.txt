John Charles Ryle (1816–1900) yali Omulabirizi omusooka ow’e Liverpool era omwevanjulizi omukulu ow’ekkanisa ey’omulembe gwa Vikitoliya — omuvubuka omugagga eyazikirizibwa okugwa kw’ebbanka y’amaka ge, n’akyusibwa okuyingira mu buweereza, era eyamanyikira obupapula obw’Enjiri obw’olwatu obwasaasaanyizibwa mu bukadde.
