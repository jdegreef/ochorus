John Newton (1725–1807) yali omuduumizi w’emmeeri ez’abaddu eyafuuka, oluvannyuma lw’okukyuka okwa mpolampola okwatandika mu muyaga gwa Atlantic ogw’omu 1748, omusumba Omwangirikaana e Olney, n’awandiika “Amazing Grace,” era — mu bukadde bwe — eddoboozi erikulembera okulwanyisa obusuubuzi bw’abaddu bwe yali akoze, ng’abuulirira William Wilberforce.
