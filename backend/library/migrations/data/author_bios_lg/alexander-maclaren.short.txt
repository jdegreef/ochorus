Alexander Maclaren (1826–1910) yali omubuulizi Omuskoti ow’Ababatisiti eyaweereza Union Chapel, Manchester, okumala emyaka amakumi ana mu etaano — “omulangira w’ababuulizi abannyonnyozi b’Ebyawandiikibwa,” eyasoma buli lunaku obulamu bwe bwonna Ebyawandiikibwa eby’Oluyonaani n’Olwebbulaniya, ne biriisa okubuulira kwe okwakuŋŋaanyizibwa mu kitabo kye Expositions of Holy Scripture.
