Amy Beatrice Carmichael yazaalibwa nga 16 Ddesemba 1867 mu kabuga akatono ak'oku lubalama lw'ennyanja aka Millisle, mu Disitulikiti ya Down, mu bukiikakkono bwa Ireland, nga ye mubereberye mu baana omusanvu ab'omu maka amasinza Katonda ag'Ekipuratanti. Kitaawe, David, yali museevu; amaka gaamanya obulungi n'obwavu obw'amazima, oluvannyuma lw'okufa kwe okw'amangu Amy bwe yali akyali muto. Yakulira ng'asaba essaala ez'obwana n'obuvumu bw'omwana. Esinga okumanyibwa mu zo era ekyajjukirwa n'okutuusa kaakano: ng'akyali muwala muto yasaba Katonda, buli kiro, akyuse amaaso ge amaddugavu gafuuke ga bbululu, era buli nkya yadduka okulaba mu ndabirwamu oba yali akikoze. Yali tannakikola. Wabula emyaka mingi oluvannyuma, ng'asiiga olususu lwe olweru olw'Ekiyirisi n'akaawa akaddugavu asobole okuyingira mu masabo g'Abahindu awatali kutegeerwa mu bukiikaddyo bwa India, we yandimannya lwaki amaaso amaddugavu gaali kirabo ekisinga obulungi.

Okukkiriza kwe kweyongera obuziba mu buvubuka bwe okutuuka ku kintu eky'omuwendo omungi okusinga okwegomba kw'omwana. Mu nguudo za Belfast, amaka we gaali gasenguse, yatandika okukuŋŋaanya abawala abaavu abasinga ab'omu makolero g'engoye — abaayitibwanga «shawlies», olw'okuba baali baavu nnyo okugula enkuufiira, ne beebikka ebiwero ku mitwe gyabwe — okuyigirizibwa ku Ssande. Omulimu ne gukula okutuusa lwe gwetaaga ekizimbe kyagwo, era mu 1887 «Welcome Hall» eyasooka n'ezimbibwa okugutereka. Ng'ayita mu Lukuŋŋaana lwa Keswick, yayingira mu mukutu gw'okuyigiriza okw'obutukuvu okw'ekiseera kya Victoria okwabumba obulamu bwe bwonna: okuyitibwa okwewaayo kyonna, okuteeka buli kimu awatali kusigazaawo miguu gya Kristo.

Okuyitibwa Ebuvanjuba

Okuyitibwa okugenda mu mulimu gw'obwamisonaali, bwe kwajja, kwali kutegeerekeka era, eri abaali bamwetoolodde, kwa ntiisa. Ng'omulwadde ow'olubeerera — yali bonaabona olw'obulumi bw'obusimu obwamugalamizanga wansi wiiki nnyingi — Amy yasooka okwewaayo eri Misoni ey'Omunda mu Cchina, naye obulamu bwe ne businga okugutuukiriza. Mu kifo ekyo yasaabala mu nnyanja okugenda e Japani mu 1893, ng'omu ku bamisonaali abasooka abaatumibwa wansi w'ebbendera ya Keswick, ng'ayiga olulimi era ng'ayambala engoye ez'Ekijapani. Endwadde n'esalako olugendo olwo oluvannyuma lw'emyezi nga kkumi n'etaano. Ne kuddirira ekiseera ekimpi mu Ceylon. Oluvannyuma, mu Novemba 1895, yatuuka mu bukiikaddyo bwa India — era eyo, okuggyako okutuuka kwe, teyaddayo nate. Yandiweereza emyaka amakumi ataano mu etaano awatali kuwummulako n'omulundi gumu okudda ewaabwe.

India yamenya n'ezzaawo obuweereza bwe. Ng'atambulatambula mu byalo by'Abatamiiri, ng'abuulira ng'ali ku ggaali erisikibwa ente, yatandika okumanya obubi obwakwekebwa: abaana abato, abamu nga bawere, «abaawongebwa» ab'amaka gaabwe eri amasabo, abawala gye baateekerwateekerwa okwenda okw'obusinza. Enkyukakyuka esookerwako yajja nga 7 Marisi 1901, omuwala ow'emyaka musanvu ayitibwa Preena, eyali adduse okuva mu ssabo gye baali bamukwatidde, bwe yatuuka ewa Amy. Emikono gy'omwana gyali gikyalina obubonero we baamwokera abakazi b'essabo n'ebyuma ebyokya olw'okugezaako okusooka okudduka. Preena yalinnya ku bulago bwa Amy, era ekkubo lya Amy ne litereezebwa. Teyasobola kuggyawo ekyo kye yali amanyi kaakano.

Amaka g'e Dohnavur

Okuva mu kusisinkana okwo ne kukula Ekibiina ky'e Dohnavur, ekyakuŋŋaanira okwetooloola ekyalo Dohnavur okumpi n'enkomerero ey'obukiikaddyo bwa India. Amy teyakifuga ng'ekitongole; yanywereza ddala kibeere maka, era abaana — abaanunulibwa kinnoomu, emirundi mingi mu kabi era ku nkomerero y'amateeka — bamuyite si «madaamu», wabula Amma, maama. Yayambala saali, n'atwala emmere n'empisa za India, n'awa abaana amannya g'Ekihindi, era n'agaana okufuula omulimu ekyokulabirako. Yanywerera ku nkalakkalira ey'ebyensimbi gye yayiga okuva ku Hudson Taylor: obwetaavu bwandibuuliddwa Katonda mu ssaala, si kusaba baweereza; tewandibaddewo kusaba wadde okukuŋŋaanya. Abaana ne beeyongera obungi — abawala kkumi na musanvu mu 1904, kikumi mu asatu mu 1913 — era mu 1918 Ekibiina ne kiggulawo emiryango gyakyo eri abalenzi nabo. Amasomero g'abaana abato, amasomero, amakolero, n'oluvannyuma eddwaliro ne bizimbibwa mu kifo ekyo, byonna nga biwanirirwa, Amy bwe yanywereza, essaala eziddibwamu.

Essaala ye yali empagi olunaku lwa Amy lwonna kwe lwetooloolanga; yagiyita ensibuko awatali yo buli kimu ekirala kyandigudde. Okuva mu 1912 yali yeetisse omugugu ogw'enjawulo olw'abalenzi b'amasabo, ng'asaba Katonda oba amuggyeeko omugugu oba amulage ky'okukola — n'oluvannyuma n'alindirira emyaka okufuna eky'okuddamu. Mu Janwali 1918 yokka, omulenzi bwe yaleetebwa awatali kusuubira mu kifo ky'abaana abato, ekkubo we lyagguka, era omulimu gw'abalenzi gwe yali asabidde okumala emyaka mukaaga ne gutandika ku nkomerero.

Olw'okuba teyasaba muntu yenna okuggyako Katonda olw'ensimbi, okuweebwa kwennyini ne kufuuka ekiwandiiko ekigenda mu maaso eky'essaala eziddibwamu. Mu myaka egy'okulwana egya Ssematalo Owasooka, omuwendo gwa ppawundi bwe gwagwa n'emiwendo ne gyaka waggulu, Ekibiina mu ngeri emu tekyabulwako. Obujulizi bwa Amy yennyini okuva mu myaka egyo bwali bwangu era nga tebusagaasagana: tebaabulwako kintu kirungi na kimu. Ebyalo ebyabeetoolodde, yagamba, byalaba mu kusigala nga Dohnavur bw'eyo obukakafu obw'olwatu obw'okuweebwa kwa Katonda.

Ebigezo, n'Engeri Okukkiriza gye Kwabisisinkana

Teri kintu na kimu mu mulimu ekyali kyangu oba eky'obukuumi. Amy yawawaabirwa okuba nga yabba abaana, n'asikibwa mu bibuuzo by'amateeka, n'awakanyizibwa abo abasuubula abaana be yali akomya. Yaziika abaana be yali alwaniridde okulokola. Era yawandiika ku byonna awatali kubikaba. Ekitabo kye eky'omu 1903 Things as They Are kyeraliikiriza abasomi baabwe Abaŋŋereza n'ekifaananyi kyakyo ekitaliimu kwewaana ku mulimu gw'obwamisonaali okutuusa abamu ne bamuwawaabira okugeya — yali agaanye ebifaananyi eby'okusanyusa abawagizi bye baali basuubira. Teyeefuula ku mulundi gumu nti omuwendo gwali mutono.

Okugezesebwa kwe yennyini okusinga obuziba kwajja ku nkomerero. Mu 1931 yasaba essaala ey'akabi — nti Katonda akole kyonna mu ye ekyandimusaanyizza okumuweereza — era nga wayiseewo akaseera n'agwa okugwa okubi, n'amenyeka okugulu n'ateguka ekkenda, ekiwundu ekyamuleka, awamu n'obusongezo, ng'agalamidde wansi ku kitanda ekiseera ekisinga obungi eky'emyaka amakumi abiri egy'enkomerero egy'obulamu bwe. Okuva mu kisenge ekyo, ng'alina obulumi obutasalako, yawandiika ebitabo bye ebisukka mu amakumi asatu ebisinga obungi, n'afuka amagezi eri Ekibiina kye yali tasobola nate kutambulira wakati waakyo, era n'akola ekitabo ekitono eky'okusinza If, olukalala lw'emisittale egiwenjula egipima buli musomi okwolekagana n'okwagala okwalagibwa ku Kalvali.

Bwe siba na kusaasira ku muweereza munnange nga Mukama wange bwe yansaasira, kale simanyi kintu na kimu ku kwagala okwa Kalvali.

— Amy Carmichael

Emisittale egyo tegyawandiikibwa nga giva mu bbanga ery'okuwummula. Gyava mu mukazi eyali amaze emyaka amakumi asatu ng'afuka okusaasira ku baasuulibwa, era eyagiwandiika ku nkomerero ng'ali ku kitanda eky'obulumi, ng'akebera emmeeme ye yennyini n'obukambwe nga obw'omusomi yenna. Ekyaddibwamu kidda emirundi n'emirundi, buli nkyukakyuka nga eyita okulemererwa kw'okwagala okulala okusirikira:

Bwe nsobola mu bwangu okwogera ku bukyamu n'ebibi by'omuntu yenna; bwe nsobola okwogera mu ngeri ey'obutafaayo wadde ku busobyu bw'omwana, kale simanyi kintu na kimu ku kwagala okwa Kalvali.

— Amy Carmichael

Okufa n'Obusika

Amy Carmichael yafiira e Dohnavur nga 18 Janwali 1951, ng'alina emyaka kinaana mu esatu, ng'asabye nti waleme kuteekebwawo jjinja ku ntaana ye. Abaana ne bamuziika n'ekyesaawo ky'ebinyonyi waggulu w'ekifo era n'ekigambo kimu ng'ekijjukizo — Amma. Ekibiina kye yatandikawo kyawangaala oluvannyuma lwe era kikyagenda mu maaso n'omulimu gwakyo mu Tamil Nadu n'okutuusa leero, nga kirabirira abaana ku ttaka lye yasabira okubaawo.

Yaleka ennyiriri y'ebitabo ebyogera ku India ekitono okusinga bwe byogera ku kwewaayo: Gold Cord, olugero lw'Ekibiina; If; n'ebitabo bingi eby'ennyimba n'ebbaluwa abasomi ab'emirembe n'emirembe bye baalaba nga bikwata ku mmeeme era nga birongoofu. Yabala obulamu bw'obwamisonaali mu bwangu ng'akakisa ak'okufa, era yayigiriza nti Omukristaayo asaba okugoberera Kristo okumpi tasaanidde kwewuunya kutekebwako akabonero olw'okugoberera okwo. Empisa ye mu buli kigezo yali okusirika n'okulindirira Katonda nga tannaba kukola.

Kale lindirira mu maaso ga Mukama. Lindirira mu kusirika, era mu kusirika okwo obukakafu bulikujjira.

— Amy Carmichael

Guno gwe gwali omukutu gw'obulamu bwe bwonna: essaala ey'obuvumu ey'omwana Katonda gye yamanya obulungi obutagiddamu nga bwe yagisaba; omukazi omuvubuka omulwadde eyagaana okusigala ewaabwe; maama eri abaana enkumi n'enkumi ensi be yali esudde; era, ku nkomerero, omulwadde eyeeyongera, okuva ku kitanda kye, okupima buli kusituka kw'omutima okwolekagana n'okwagala okw'Omusaalaba. Teyasaba kugoberera okutaliimu bikovu, era teyakuweebwa. Yandigambye nti bwe bwali obusaasizi obw'amazima ennyo obw'obulamu bwe.
