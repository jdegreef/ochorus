Thomas à Kempis (nga 1380–1471) yali omumonaki Omuholandi eyazaalibwa mu Bugirimaani, eyamala emyaka ensanvu mu kkanisa y’abamonaki emu ebweru wa Zwolle, ng’akoppolola ebiwandiiko, ng’akuuma ebyafaayo by’ekibiina kye, era ng’ayigiriza abayizi baakyo. Yabumbibwa e Deventer mu Ab’Oluganda ab’Obulamu obwa Bulijjo, yawandiikira abavubuka abo obutabo buna obw’okubuulirira obwafuuka The Imitation of Christ — oluvannyuma lwa Baibuli, ekitabo Ekikristaayo ekyasingira ddala okusomebwa. Ekyamala ebbanga liwanvu nga kigenda awatali linnya lya mwandiisi era nga kiwakanyizibwa, kaakano kimanyiddwa nti kye kikye. Obulamu bwe bwali obwekwese, obutaayanguyizibwa, era okwekweka okwo kwe ttaka ekitabo mwe kyakulira.
