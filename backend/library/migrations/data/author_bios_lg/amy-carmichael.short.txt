Amy Beatrice Carmichael, eyazaalibwa mu 1867 e Millisle, Ireland, yali omuminsani Omupolotesitanti mu Buyindi. Yaweereza emyaka 55 awatali kuwummulako, ng'atandikawo Ekibiina ky'e Dohnavur okununula abaana okuva mu businzi bw'obwenzi obw'omu masabo. Ng'amanyiddwa olw'okwewaayo kwe okw'amaanyi n'ebiwandiiko bye ku ssaala, yawandiika ebitabo 35 era n'asigala mu Buyindi okutuusa okufa kwe mu 1951. Omulimu gwe gugenda mu maaso okuyita mu buweereza bwe yatandikawo. Obulamu bwa Carmichael bwalaga obuwulize obw'amaanyi eri okuyitibwa kwa Kristo n'okwemanyiiza obuwangwa bw'abantu be yaweerezanga.
