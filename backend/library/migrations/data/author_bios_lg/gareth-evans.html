Gareth Evans yazaalibwa mu 1938 mu Merthyr Tydfil, wakati mu biwonvu bya amanda mu Wales ey’Obukiikaddyo, era mu Wales mwe ebintu ebibiri ebikulu ebyanywerera mu bulamu bwe we byasimbira emirandira: okukkiriza kwe, ne Anne, omuwala gwe yandiwasizza. Yatuuka ku kuyitibwa kwe mu ngeri etaali ya bulijjo. Okutendekebwa kwe okusooka tekwali mu ssemeneri wabula mu ssaayansi entuufu — yasoma Fiziika mu Yunivasite ya Wales, ng’afuna ekitiibwa ne Diguli ey’Okusomesa — era mu 1961, ng’alina diguli mu mukono, ye ne Anne baafumbiriganwa.

Okumala emyaka mingi ekituuti kye kyali kibiina ky’essomero. Gareth yeyafunira ku by’obulamu ng’omusomesa wa fiziika mu ssomero ery’omu ddaala, okuyitibwa kwe yeetikka wamu naye bwe, mu 1975, ye ne Anne bwe baasengukira mu Kanada n’addamu okusomesa mu nsi empya. Empisa z’omu laboratory — obutuufu, obugumiikiriza, okugaana okuwummulira mu bibuuzo ebitali bikakafu — tezaamuvaako, era oluvannyuma zaawa okusomesa kwe okwa Bayibuli obulungamu bwakwo obw’enjawulo: empisa ya ssaayansi ey’okubuuza ekiwandiiko ekyo kye kigamba ddala, n’ekyo kye kitegeeza ddala.

Okukyukira eri obuweereza obw’ekiseera kyonna kwajja mu 1979, bwe yayitibwa okulunda ekkanisa ya Christian and Missionary Alliance mu Kitchener, Ontario. Emyaka ena oluvannyuma okuyitibwa okulala kwatwala ab’Evans ebugwanjuba mu Victoria, British Columbia, Gareth gye yalundira emyaka munaana era ye ne Anne gye baafuulira awaka waabwe okuva olwo.

Abamu bandituumye Abaebbulaniya 11 nti “Abazira ab’Okukkiriza.” Nze sirabamu bazira n’omu.

— Gareth Evans, He Holds My Tomorrows

Sentensi eyo emu eggulawo eddirisa eri endowooza ye yonna. Eri Gareth ebyokulabirako ebikulu eby’okukkiriza si basajja abanene mu by’omwoyo aba kwewuunya nga tuli wala mu bukuume wabula abantu aba bulijjo, abalina obulema, abakkiriza bukkiriza Katonda ku kigambo kye — ekitegeeza nti okukkiriza kwabwe kwe kukkiriza omusomi kw’asobola ddala okwenyigiramu. Ye ddoboozi ly’addamu emirundi n’emirala: okukkiriza kwa mirimu, okukkiriza kwa banafu nga bwe kuli kw’ab’amaanyi, era kutambulizibwa dddaala emu ey’obuwulize mu kiseera.

Mu 1990 okukkaanya okwo kwamutwala ye ne Anne ku nnyanja. Gareth yaweereza nga chaplain ku Mercy Ship M/V Anastasis, eryato ery’eddwaliro eryali likolera wakati wa Bulaaya ne Afirika ey’Obugwanjuba, ng’alabirira abakozi b’eryato era ng’akwata buli mukisa okwogera — mu makanisa, mu bikuŋŋaano by’abasumba, ne mu nkuŋŋaana z’ababaka mu kkubo. Baava ku lyato mu 1994, naye tebaava ku bulamu obw’okutambula eryato lwe lyali libaggulidewo. Mu myaka egyaddirira, Gareth atambudde nnyo, ng’asomesa mu masomero ag’okutendeka abayigirizwa ne mu nkuŋŋaana okwetooloola ensi yonna.

Okuva mu kusomesa okwo mwe gwava ebitabo bye. Okuwandiika kwe tekulina kwanguyiriza, kwangu, era kwa busumba, nga kugendereza ky’ayita ekkanisa erumizibwa — abakkiriza abakooye olw’okubonaabona oba okwesuubira okwafiirwa abetaaga okujjukizibwa nti Katonda akwata enkya zaabwe. Ebitabo nga He Holds My Tomorrows, The Key in My Hand, Stepping Stones, Soar Like the Eagle, ne Feasting at the Table bikulembera omusomi mpolampola okuyita mu Byawandiikibwa, nga bigatta okunnyonnyola okw’obwegendereza n’okuzzaamu amaanyi okw’omusajja alunze ebibiina eby’amazima okuyita mu buzibu obw’amazima. Nga takyasibiddwa ku kituuti kimu, kaakano aweereza okusinga okuyita ku mukutu gwe, ku lwo okusomesa kwe kwenkanankana kusobole okutuuka ku abo abasaasaanye n’abo abeeyawudde.

Okuyita mu buli kusengula — okuva mu biwonvu bya Wales okutuuka ku kibiina ky’essomero ery’omu Kanada, okuva ku kituuti ky’ekkanisa okutuuka ku lyato ery’ekisa n’ebweru ku kkubo ery’enkuŋŋaana — Gareth Evans agobelidde olukalala lw’okuyitibwa n’obwesige bwe bumu obuteefu obwawa ekitabo kye ekimanyiddwa ennyo erinnya lyakyo: nti enkya ze zaali dda mu mikono egy’obukuumi. Ye ne Anne balina bawala basatu, n’ekibinja ekikula eky’abazzukulu n’abazzukulu b’abazzukulu — ekibala ekiteefu eky’obulamu obuwanvu obw’okukkiriza okwa bulijjo, okw’obwesigwa kw’amaze emyaka gye ng’akubuulira abalala.
