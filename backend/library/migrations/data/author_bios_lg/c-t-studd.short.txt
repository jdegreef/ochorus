C. T. Studd (1860–1931) yali muzannyi wa cricket owa Bungereza, eyagaba obugagga oluvannyuma lw’okukyuka kwe ne yeegatta ku “Cambridge Seven” ng’omuminsani — ng’aweereza e China, e Buyindi, era ku nkomerero mu Kongo, gye yatandikira ekyafuuka WEC. Omusajja ow’obunyiikivu obw’amaanyi, obutakkiriza kukkakkana, era oluusi obuzibu, yawangaala ng’agoberera okukkiriza nti tewali ssaddaaka esobola kuba nnene nnyo ku lwa Kristo.
