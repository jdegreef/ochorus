Sundar Singh yazaalibwa nga 3 Ssebutemba 1889, e Rampur, mu ssaza ly’Abasiiki erya Patiala mu Punjab, nga ye mwana omulenzi asembayo obuto mu maka agaali ag’obugagga era ag’ekitiibwa. Yakulira mu bulamu obw’okwesiima, era obulamu bwe bwonna yageranyanga obugagga obwo obugonvu naye obutaliimu mirembe n’ekkubo ekizibu naye ery’essanyu ery’omubuulizi atambulatambula. Bazadde be baali Basiiki, naye amaka gaabwe gaali maggule n’eri abayigiriza b’eddiini y’Abahindu, era omulenzi n’akulira mu njigiriza zombi. Abaawandiika ku bulamu bwe baagambibwa nti bwe yaweza emyaka omusanvu, yali amanyi kumutwe ekitundu ekisinga obunene ekya Bhagavad Gita. Oluvannyuma yagambanga nti teyali Musiiki, wabula yali muntu anoonya.

Eyasinga okumukwatako mu buto bwe ye nnyina. Yamutwalanga eri abasajja abatukuvu ne mu masabo, n’amuteerawo ekyokulabirako kya sadhu — eyeefiiriza ensi okunoonya Katonda — n’amugamba nti lumu kimugwanidde okunoonya Emirembe egyo gyokka egimatiza. Emyaka mingi oluvannyuma, omusumba bwe yamuwa ekirowoozo okusomako mu ttendekero ly’eby’eddiini, yamuddamu nti yali yasoma dda mu ttendekero erisinga obulungi mu nsi yonna: ku maviivi ga nnyina. Nnyina yafa ng’alina emyaka kkumi n’ena, era okufa kwe kwamuleka nga yennyamidde nnyo nga talina amubudaabuda. Yeeyongera okunoonya — mu biwandiiko eby’eddiini, mu mikolo gy’eddiini, ne mu kuyigirizibwa yoga sadhu omu — naye n’atafuna kintu na kimu ekyandikkakkanyizza omutima gwe.

Bayibuli mu muliro

Mu ssomero ly’obuminsani erya Presbyterian mu kyalo kye we yasookera okusanga Endagaano Empya, era n’agikyawa. Yamulabikira ng’evvoola okukkiriza kwa bajjajjaabe n’amalala g’omusaayi gwe ogw’Abasiiki. Yakasuukiriranga ababuulizi Abakristaayo amayinja era n’akubiriza abalala okukola bwe batyo. Awo, mu bigambo bye ye, ne wajja ekikolwa kye yeenenyanga obulamu bwe bwonna: “Mu maaso ga kitange nnasalaasala Bayibuli n’ebitabo ebirala eby’Ekikristaayo, ne mbiyiwako amafuta g’ettaala, ne mbyokya.” Yali mwesigwa mu buli mukolo gw’eddiini ye, naye era yali talina mirembe — nga talina mirembe n’akatono, okutuusa lwe yasalawo okufa.

Ennaku ssatu ng’amaze okwokya Bayibuli, mu Ddesemba 1904, omuvubuka ow’emyaka kkumi n’etaano yazuukuka ku ssaawa kkumi ez’ekiro, n’anaaba amazzi agannyogoga, n’asaba: “Ai Katonda, oba nga waliwo Katonda, ndaga ekkubo ettuufu, bw’otookikole nnaatta.” Singa tewajja kuddibwamu, yali agenderera okugalamiza omutwe gwe ku luguudo lw’eggaali y’omukka nga eggaali ey’essaawa kkumi n’emu ey’oku makya eyitawo. N’asaba, n’asaba, n’asaba nate okumala ekitundu ky’essaawa ekirala.

Ku ssaawa kkumi n’ekitundu, nga bwe yakinyumyanga, ekisenge ne kijjula ekitangaala ekinene, era mu kitangaala ekyo n’alaba ekifaananyi kya Yesu Kristo — “Kyalina endabika ey’ekitiibwa n’okwagala bwe kityo” — n’awulira eddoboozi mu Luhindustani: “Olimala bbanga ki ng’onjigganya? Nzize okukulokola; obadde osaba okumanya ekkubo ettuufu. Lwaki tolikwata?” Yali ye wuuyo gwe yali avvoola. “Awo ekirowoozo ne kinzijira nti, ‘Yesu Kristo tafudde, wabula mulamu, era ateekwa okuba nga ye Yennyini.’ Bwe ntyo ne ngwa ku bigere bye ne nfuna Emirembe gino egy’ekitalo gye ssaasobola kufuna walala wonna.”

Okwolesebwa kwaggwaawo, naye yagamba nti emirembe tegyaggwaawo n’akatono. Yagenda butereevu eri kitaawe n’amutegeeza nti yali afuuse Omukristaayo. Kitaawe n’amugamba addeyo yeebake — kubanga ku lunaku olwasooka eggulo lyokka yali ayokezza Bayibuli. Omulenzi n’addamu nti: “Kale, kaakano nzudde nti Yesu Kristo mulamu, era mmaliridde okumugoberera. Leero ndi muyigirizwa we era ŋŋenda kumuweereza.”

Agobebwa, n’ekiro ekisooka mu ggulu

Ab’omu maka ge baagezaako buli kimu okumuzza: okumusuubiza obugagga n’ebifo eby’ekitiibwa, okumujjukiza ensonyi ze yandiretedde erinnya ly’amaka, okumwegayirira kwa Raja omu eyali mukwano gwabwe ng’asinziira ku malala g’ekika kye, n’emyezi egy’okumunyoomesa. Bwe watabaawo na kimu ku ebyo ekyamunyeenya, ne bamwegaana ne bamugoba — era, ng’abaasooka okuwandiika ku bulamu bwe bwe baakiwandiika, nga bamuwadde emmere eyali etabuddwamu obutwa, kubanga kyali kirungi afe okusinga okubaswaza. Ekiro kye ekisooka ng’omuntu agobeddwa yakimala mu mpewo ennyogovu wansi w’omuti.

Ekiro ekyo nkijjukira ng’ekiro kyange ekisooka mu ggulu.

— Sadhu Sundar Singh

“Wakati mu bugagga n’okwesiima saasobola kufuna mirembe mu mutima gwange,” bwe yakyogerako. “Okubeerawo kw’Omulokozi kwafuula okubonaabona emirembe.” Ku lunaku lwe olw’amazaalibwa ag’emyaka kkumi n’omukaaga, nga 3 Ssebutemba 1905, yabatizibwa mu Kkanisa ya Bungereza e Simla.

Sadhu Omukristaayo

Ng’akyali Mukristaayo omuto yakola okusalawo okwaluŋŋamya byonna ebyaddirira. Yayambala ekyambalo ekya kyenvu eky’omusajja omutukuvu Omuhindu n’atandika okutambula ku makubo nga sadhu — naye sadhu wa Yesu Kristo. Yalina ekyambalo kimu, bulangiti emu, n’Endagaano Empya. Teyakwatanga ku ssente, yalyanga ekyo kye baamuwanga, era yasulanga mu mpuku, wansi w’emiti, oba mu nnyumba za buli muntu eyakkirizanga okumusuza. Ekyambalo kyamuggulirawo enzigi ezaali ziggaddwawo eri omuminsani omugwira; naye era kyategeezanga nti ekitiibwa kyasobolanga okufuuka obusungu obw’amaanyi mangu ddala, ekyalo bwe kyategeeranga omusajja ono omutukuvu gwe yabuuliranga. Yatambula mu Punjab, Kashmir, Baluchistan ne Afghanistan, era n’afuula Kotgarh, ekyalo mu nsozi za Himalaya ekiri nga mayiro amakumi ataano okuva e Simla, ekifo kye eky’okutandikirako.

Eyo yakolerako okumala ekiseera wamu ne Samuel Stokes, Omumerika omuvubuka eyali agabye obugagga bwe bwonna asobole okubeera mu Buyindi ng’omusajja w’eddiini omwavu; bombi baalabirira abagenge e Sabathu era ne baweereza mu nkambi y’abalwadde ba kawumpuli okumpi ne Lahore. Stokes, bwe yali amujjanjaba ng’alwadde omusujja, yamuwulira ng’akuba kaama mpola nti: “Nga kiwooma okubonaabona ku lulwe!” Yasomera mu St. John’s Divinity College e Lahore era n’aweebwa olukusa okubuulira, naye n’addiza Omulabirizi Lefroy olukusa olwo mu kifo ky’okutikkirwa obusumba, asobole okubuulira mu ddembe mu Bakristaayo ab’amannya gonna. Sundar teyatandikawo kibiina kya ddiini, teyabatizanga abaakyuka olw’okubuulira kwe — yabasindikanga mu makanisa ag’omu kitundu — era n’agaana okufuuka guru wa muntu yenna. “Nnyinza ntya okukufuula omuyigirizwa wange,” bwe yagamba omuntu omu eyali ayagala okumugoberera, “nga nange kennyini ndi muyigirizwa buyigirizwa?”

Ebyawandiikibwa ku myaka egyo bijjudde okuyisibwa obubi. Omujulirwa omu, eyali mmemba w’ekibiina kya Arya Samaj, yawandiikira olupapula lw’amawulire Olukristaayo olwa buli wiiki ng’annyonnyola engeri gye yalabamu sadhu omuvubuka ng’atuula ku muti ogwatemebwa mu kyalo eky’oku lusozi, ne yeesiimuula mu maaso, n’atandika okuyimba ku Kristo, okutuusa omusajja omu lwe yamukuba n’amusuula wansi. Sundar n’asiba omukono gwe ogwali guvaamu omusaayi n’ekitambaala kye eky’oku mutwe, ne yeeyongera okuyimba, ng’asabira abo abaali bamukubye. Omusajja eyamukuba oluvannyuma yasaba okubatizibwa, era n’abatizibwa.

Tibet, n’ebyafaayo ebyabuusibwabuusibwa

Okuva mu 1908, ensi ya Tibet eyali eggaddwawo yafuuka ennimiro gye yeerondera. Bantu batono abaali batutteyo enjiri, era obunnyogovu n’akabi byamusikiriza mu kifo ky’okumuziyiza. Okumala emyaka mingi, emyezi egy’ebbugumu yagimalanga ng’agezaako okusomoka ensalo, ate ebiseera eby’obutiti ng’abuulira mu Buyindi.

Mu mwaka gwe ogw’amakumi abiri mu esatu, wadde mikwano gye baali bamuwabudde obutakikola, yagezaako okusiiba ennaku amakumi ana mu kibira ekiri wakati wa Hardwar ne Dehra Dun, ng’abala ennaku n’entuumu y’amayinja okutuusa lwe yanafuwa ennyo n’atasobola na kugasuula wabbali. Abatemi b’enku baamuzuula ne bamusitula mu bulangiti ye ne bamuggyayo. Oluvannyuma yagamba nti okusiiba okwo kwamalawo okubuusabuusa kwe yalina: omubiri gwe bwe gwagendanga gunafuwa, emirembe gye ne gyeyongera okunywera, n’amalirira nti egyo gaali mirembe egyaweebwa okuva mu ggulu, so si egyava mu ye yennyini. Yakkiriza era nti yasunguwalanga mangu bwe yabanga akooye ng’abuuzibwa ebibuuzo bingi — “bw’onoowandiika ekitabo kyo, oteekwa okuwandiika ne ku bunafu bwange.”

Mu myaka egyo mwe muva ebyafaayo ebyamufuula omututumufu — era ebyasinga okuwakanyizibwa. Yanyumya nti yakwatibwa mu kabuga akayitibwa Rasar, n’asuulibwa mu luzzi olukalu wakati w’amagumba g’abaali battiddwa edda, era ku kiro eky’okusatu n’aggibwamu n’omuguwa omununuzi gwe yali tayinza kulaba, ekisaanikira ky’oluzzi ne kiddamu okusibibwa ku kkufulu emabega we; yagamba nti lama omukulu yasanga ekisumuluzo nga kikyali ku lukoba lwe ye yennyini. Yayogera ku kibiina ekinene eky’ekyama eky’aba sannyasi Abakristaayo, ne ku musajja omukadde ennyo eyeewala abantu, “Maharishi wa Kailash,” eyabeeranga mu mpuku waggulu ennyo mu nsozi za Himalaya. Bino Sundar ye yabinyumya; tewali muntu mulala yabirabako. Mu myaka gya 1920, omuyivu Omujesuwiti H. Hosten n’omusumba Omuswiisi Oskar Pfister baafulumya ebiwandiiko ebyamulumba okumala ebbanga, nga bagamba nti ebyafaayo ebyo tebisobola kukakasibwa oba nti si bya mazima, era abamu ne bamulumiriza obulimba. Abalala, nga mu bo mwe muli omuyivu Omugirimaani Friedrich Heiler, baanoonyereza mu Buyindi ne bawolereza obwesimbu bwe. Ensonga eyo tegonjoolwanga n’akatono, era abasomi basobola okukwata ebyafaayo bino n’obwegendereza. Ekyeyoleka bulungi y’engeri Sundar yennyini gye yabitunuuliramu: “Abantu bagulumizizza nnyo ekintu kino mu bulamu bwange,” bwe yayogera ku musajja oyo omukadde. “Omulimu gwange si kubuulira Rishi, wabula kubuulira Kristo.” Heiler yawandiika nti yagumiikiriza okulumbibwa okwo n’obukkakkamu, era yayogeranga ku balabe be mu kwagala kwokka.

Okutuuka ku nkomerero z’ensi

Mu 1918, okukyalira Madras kwafuula omubuulizi oyo atambulatambula eyali tamanyiddwa muntu amanyiddwa mu nsi yonna. Abantu enkumi n’enkumi bajja okumuwuliriza mu bukiikaddyo bwa Buyindi ne mu Ceylon, oluvannyuma mu Burma, Malaya, China ne Japan. Mu 1920 yasaabala ku nnyanja n’agenda e Bungereza, n’oluvannyuma n’agenda mu Amerika ne Australia; mu 1922 yatambula mu Switzerland, Bugirimaani, Sweden, Norway, Denmark ne Holland. Yabuulira mu kkanisa ya Luther e Wittenberg ne mu Kkanisa Ennene ey’e Uppsala, Ssaabalabirizi Söderblom nga y’amuvvuunulira. Kitaawe, eyali omulabe we asinga obukambwe edda, yali afuuse Omukristaayo naye era yayamba okusasula ssente z’olugendo lwe. Kitaawe bwe yasaba mutabani we amubatize, Sundar yamusindika eri abaweereza ba kkanisa ab’abulijjo: omulimu gwe, bwe yagamba, gwali si kubatiza wabula kubuulira njiri.

Kristo yekka bwe bulokozi. Oyo abeera mu ye afa eri ekibi n’ayingira mu bulamu obutaggwaawo.

— Sadhu Sundar Singh, e Basel, 1922

Mu Ceylon, maama omu yamwegayirira assengako emikono ku mutabani we eyali agenda okufa. “Mu mikono gino temuli maanyi,” bwe yamugamba, “wabula mu mikono gya Kristo egyafumitibwa gyokka.” Ku nkomerero yagenda mu ddwaliro, n’asaba, n’ateeka omukono gwe ku mutwe gw’omulenzi; ennaku ssatu oluvannyuma n’alaba omulenzi ng’atudde ne nnyina mu lumu ku nkuŋŋaana ze. Naye abantu bwe baakakaatika okumutwala ng’omukozi w’ebyamagero, yalekera awo okusabira abalwadde mu ngeri eyo, baleme okumutunuulira ye mu kifo kya Kristo.

Ettutumu lyamutiisa okusinga okuyigganyizibwa bwe kwamutiisa. Ebibiina by’abantu byamuyitanga mutukuvu; eyali mukyala wa kabaka wa Russia edda yamusaba amuwe omukisa, n’agaana, ng’agamba nti emikono gye gyali gyayuza Ebyawandiikibwa Ebitukuvu. Yayagalanga okugamba nti Yesu bwe yayingira mu Yerusaalemi ng’atudde ku ndogoyi, endogoyi ye yatambulira ku byambalo, era kyandibadde busirusiru endogoyi okulowooza nti ekitiibwa kyali kyayo. Ensi ez’ebugwanjuba nazo zaamumalamu amaanyi: yasanga bangi mu nsi ez’Ekikristaayo nga babeera ng’abataliiko Kristo, era n’akyogera lwatu — awatali kunenya Kristo.

Ku Bigere bya Mukama

Mu myaka gya 1920, wakati w’engendo ze, yawandiika mu Luurudu ebitabo ebitono ebiddiriŋŋana, ebyavvuunulwa mangu mu Lungereza: Ku Bigere bya Mukama, Ekituufu n’Eddiini, Okunoonya Ekituufu, Okufumiitiriza ku Nsonga ez’Enjawulo ez’Obulamu obw’Omwoyo, era ku nkomerero Nga Tuli ne Kristo n’Awatali Kristo, kye yassaako omukono e Sabathu mu Agusito 1928. Nga bijjudde engero eziva mu nsozi n’emigga bye yamanya, biddiŋŋana emirundi n’emirundi ku kusaba, omusaalaba, n’okubeerawo kwa Kristo omulamu. Mu Ku Bigere bya Mukama, ekyawandiikibwa ng’emboozi wakati w’omuyigirizwa ne Mukama we, anyweza nti okusaba “si ngeri ya kusabiriza” wabula “kufuba kukwata ku Katonda Yennyini.” (Ekitabo ekirala, Okwolesebwa kw’Ensi ey’Omwoyo, kinnyonnyola okwolesebwa okw’ekitalo okw’obulamu obw’oluvannyuma, nga mw’otwalidde n’emboozi n’abaafa; n’abo abaamwagala baakisoma n’obwegendereza, era si wa bujulirwa bwe we businga amaanyi.)

Obukristaayo ye Kristo Yennyini.

— Sadhu Sundar Singh, Okunoonya Ekituufu

Yali yagamba dda nti yasuubira okufiira mu Tibet, era nti yandikyanirizza. “Kale temulowooza nti ‘Afudde,’” bwe yagamba abantu b’omu Switzerland abaali bamuwuliriza, “wabula mugambe nti: ‘Ayingidde mu Ggulu ne mu Bulamu Obutaggwaawo; ali ne Kristo mu bulamu obutuukiridde.’” Mu Apuli 1929, wadde mikwano gye baamuwabula obutagenda, yasitula nate okugenda e Tibet. Yasembayo okulabibwa e Kalka, wansi wa Simla. Teyakomawo, tewali kabonero ke kaali kazuuliddwa, era oluvannyuma lw’ekiseera gavumenti n’emutwala ng’afudde.

Oboolyawo ekyo kisaanira omusajja ataakkiriza muntu yenna kuzimba ku ye. Teyayagala kibiina, teyayagala bayigirizwa be ku bubwe, teyayagala kumanyibwa lwa byamagero. Yayagala kimu kyokka: okulaga, ng’ayita ku ye yennyini, Oyo eyasisinkana omulenzi eyali aggwaamu essuubi ku makya ennyo n’amuwa emirembe egitaamuvangako n’akatono — emirembe gye yasanga, nga bwe yakyogeranga, wansi w’omuti ku kiro ekisooka mu ggulu.
