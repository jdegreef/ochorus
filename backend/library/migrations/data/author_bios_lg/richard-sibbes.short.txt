Richard Sibbes (1577–1635) yali omubuulizi Omupuritaani Omungereza eyamanyibwa mu mulembe gwe nga “Omusawo ow’Omu Ggulu”. Mu kifo eky’okuba nti okubuulira kwa Bapuritaani okungi kwanyigiriza nnyo omuntu ku mutima, Sibbes yali amanyiddwa olw’obukkakkamu eri abantu abaali bamaze okuggwaamu essuubi ku bo bennyini; ekitabo kye ekisinga okumanyibwa kiggya omutwe gwakyo mu kisuubizo kya Isaaya nti olumuli olubetentefu terulimenyebwa. Yabuulira ku Gray’s Inn mu London era yali omukulu wa St Catharine’s Hall, e Cambridge, era ebbugumu lye lyakola ku bawandiisi abajja oluvannyuma lwe, nga Richard Baxter mw’ali.
