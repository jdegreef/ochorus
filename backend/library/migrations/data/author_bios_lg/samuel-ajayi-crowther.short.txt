Samuel Ajayi Crowther (nga 1809–1891) yali mulenzi Omuyoruba eyava e Osogun, gwe baawamba mu lulumba lw'abaddu mu 1821, n'awonyezebwa amaato g'abaddu Royal Navy n'assibwa e Freetown, Sierra Leone, gye yabatizibwa mu 1825. Yasomesebwa Church Missionary Society, n'afuuka Omwafrika asooka okwawulibwa mu Kkanisa ya Anglican (omusumba, 1843), ate mu 1864 n'afuuka omulabirizi omukulu Omwafrika asooka. Yavvuunula Baibuli yonna ne Book of Common Prayer mu Luyoruba, era n'agumira n'obwetoowaze okunyoomebwa kw'omulimu gwe okwaleetebwa abaminsani Abazungu abato mu myaka gye egy'enkomerero. Yafiira e Lagos ku 31 Ddesemba 1891.
