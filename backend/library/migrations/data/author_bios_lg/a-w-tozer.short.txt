A. W. Tozer (1897–1963) yali omusumba Omunnamerika eyeeyigiriza ow'ekibiina kya Christian and Missionary Alliance, ng'ebitabo bye, okusinga byonna The Pursuit of God ne The Knowledge of the Holy, awamu n'obulamu bwe obw'okusaba ng'avunamye mu kusinza, byamufuula eddoboozi erya bunnabbi eriyita ekkanisa okudda eri okunoonya Katonda yennyini. Yali musumba okumala emyaka egisukka amakumi ana, okusinga okwatuukirira mu Southside Alliance Church mu Chicago, era yalongoosa akatabo k'ekibiina kye ky'eddiini okutuusa lwe yafa.
