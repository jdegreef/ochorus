Hannah Whitall Smith yamala obulamu bwe bwonna ng'akakasa ekintu kimu ekyangu: nti Katonda mulungi, era nti Katonda amala. Yakiwandiika mu bitabo bye eby'ennaku eby'emyaka nsanvu, n'akiwakanira mu bitundu bibiri eby'ensi, era n'akinywezaako mu nnaku ezandiggumizza omutima ogutali gwa kisa. Eri abasomi obukumi n'obukumi asigala eddoboozi eddangavvu ery'Omukwakeri eriri emabega w'Ekyama ky'Omukristaayo eky'Obulamu Obw'essanyu, omukazi eyayigiriza nti omulimu omuzito ogw'okusitula omugugu ogw'okutukuzibwa gwa Katonda, era nti ogwaffe gwokka kwe kumwesiga n'okuleka.

Yazaalibwa nga ayitibwa Hannah Tatum Whitall nga February 7, 1832 mu Philadelphia, mu maka g'Abakwakeri agaamanyibwa era agatya Katonda. Kitaawe, John Mickle Whitall, ne nnyina, Mary Tatum Whitall, baamukuza nga Munnaffe "ow'obuzaale" mu nkola ya Gurney ey'enjiri, era okukuza kuno kwali okutuukiridde ddala. "Buli kigambo, endowooza, n'ekikolwa eky'obulamu bwaffe byali binnyikiziddwa mu Bukwakeri," yajjukira. "Tewali kadde na kamu lwe twawona." Teyakikyayira; bwe yatunuulira emabega ng'alina emyaka nsanvu, yeebaza buli lunaku olw'"obuyinza obw'obutuukirivu era obugulumiza obwenkana awo." Okusinziira ku bujulizi bwe ye, obwana bwe bwali bwa ssanyu obwaka — yabuyita "embeera emu ey'ekitalo ey'omusana n'ebimuli" — naye ate, mu ngeri esikiriza, ne bw'eyali omuwala ow'emyaka kkumi na musanvu yeebuuza mu ddoboozi mu kitabo kye eky'ennaku lwaki tewali kubonaabona kwajja "okunziggya mu ssanyu ery'obulamu buno."

Okunoonya Okuwanvu

Okuzuukuka kwa Smith tekwajja lwa kutya kibi wabula lwa kwewuunya. Bwe yali ng'alina emyaka nga kkumi na mukaaga n'ekitundu, mu kusoma kwe yalaba akatangaavu ak'ekitiibwa kya Katonda ekingi ennyo, era "awo n'awo, okunoonya kwange ne kutandika." Kwali, ng'okunnyonnyola kwe ye okw'obwesimbu, "okunoonya okuzibe amaaso, okutamanya." Nga yakuzibwa okunoonya kyokka "ekitangaala ekiri munda," yakyukira munda ye awatali kukoowa n'atalaba mwo okuggyako okulwana. Ekitabo kye eky'ennaku eky'eddiini eky'olubereberye, yaatula, "kye kijjukizo kimu ekiwanvu eky'okulwana n'okubonaabona, awatali n'akatangaala kamu ak'ekitangaala." Obwesimbu buno bwe butone bwe: teyakkiriza n'akatono okuwundira embeera ye ye.

Ng'akyali omuwala omuto yawandiika mu kitabo kye eky'ennaku ku kintu kimu ky'ateyasobola kwekolera ye ye: "Simanyi ngeri ya kusaba. Woowe, nnaakola ntya? Nnaagenda wa? … Kigambibwa nti, 'Musabe, muliweebwa.' … Naye siyinza kufuuka mutuukirivu ddala okutuusa lwe nneenenya, ate siyinza kwenenya." Kyali kikaaba okuva ku nkomerero y'amaanyi ge ye — okusaba okw'amazima okw'omuntu azudde nti tasobola kwerokola ye ye.

Mu 1851, ng'alina emyaka kkumi na mwenda, yafumbirwa Robert Pearsall Smith, ow'omu maka amalala agaamanyibwa ag'Abakwakeri. Obufumbo bwagaziya ensi ye era, mu kisa, ne bumuggya mu "kwekebera munda okw'obulwadde" okwali kufudde eddiini ekintu eky'okubonaabona. Abafumbo baabeera okumpi ne Philadelphia ne bagenda mu New Jersey, era mu myaka gya 1850 bombi bajja mu kukkiriza okw'enjiri okw'ebbugumu okusingawo. Naye enkyukakyuka enkulu yali ekyali ejja.

Ekyama Ekyazuulibwa

"Kyali mu 1865," yawandiika, "ekiseera eky'okuna, era nga kye kisinga okusikiriza, eky'olugendo lwange olw'omwoyo lwe lwakya ku mwoyo gwange." Yali Mukristaayo okumala emyaka mwenda egy'essanyu, naye yasigala, okusinziira ku kubala kwe ye, "buli kiseera nga neekooya ku nze nnyini." Yamanya nti Katonda mwesigwa naye n'asanga obulamu bwe obw'omunda "bujjudde okugwa — si kibi kya kungulu nnyo, wabula ebibi eby'omunda: obunnyogovu, okufa okw'omwoyo, obutaba na kwagala kwa Kikristo." Ekyo kye yazuula, era ky'anaamala obulamu bwe obusigadde ng'akirangirira, kwe kununulibwa okuva mu maanyi g'ekibi — si kibonerezo kyakyo kyokka — kwali mulimu gwa Katonda okutuukiriza mu abo abeewaayo gy'ali. Yakendeeza ensonga yonna mu bigambo bina: "Si nze, wabula Kristo."

Mu bufunze, nnandigambye nti omulimu gw'omuntu kwe kwesiga ate omulimu gwa Katonda kwe kukola.

— Hannah Whitall Smith

Kino ne kifuuka omutima gw'Ekyama ky'Omukristaayo eky'Obulamu Obw'essanyu, ekyafulumizibwa mu 1875. Yakiyita "ekyama," yannyonnyola, olw'okuba kyali kimaze ebbanga ddene nga kimukwekeddwa — "si kyama olw'okuba Katonda yakikweka, wabula olw'okuba nze nnyini nnali sinnaba kukizuula." Ekifaananyi kyakyo ekimanyiddwa ennyo gy'egaali n'omugugu: omutambuze akkiriza okwebagazibwa naye n'asigala ng'asitula omugugu omuzito ku bibegabega bye ye, "kubanga saayinza kulowooza kukuleka ng'ositula omugugu gwange nammwe." Obulamu obw'Ekikristaayo, yayigiriza, butegeeza okuteeka wansi obuzito bwonna ku nkomerero.

Omugugu omusinga obunene gwe tulina okusitula mu bulamu gwe nze nnyini. Ekintu ekisinga obuzibu kye tulina okufuga kwe nze nnyini.

— Hannah Whitall Smith

Akatabo kano akatono kaakwata ku mutima mu nsi yonna ey'Abaprotestanti aboogera Olungereza; kaatunda ebitabo ebisukka obukadde bubiri mu bulamu bwe era tekaakoma kufulumizibwa. Mu 1873 ne 1874 ba Smith baasomoka okugenda mu Bungereza, ng'okusoma kwa Hannah okwa Bayibuli okwali okuteefu era okw'amagezi kwamuleetera okwagalibwa okungi — yayitibwa "malayika wa kkanisa" — era enkuŋŋaana zaabwe, nga mw'otwalidde n'enkuŋŋaana ez'amaanyi e Broadlands ezaali ziteekeddwateekeddwa Mukama ne Nnyabo Mount Temple, zaayamba okuzuukusa mu 1875 Olukuŋŋaana lwa Keswick. Okumala ekiseera ba Smith baali abantu abaamanyibwa ennyo mu ntambula ey'amadiini ag'enjawulo ey'Obulamu Obw'Ekitiibwa (Higher Life).

Ebigezo Ebyayogerwako mu Bwesimbu

Awo ne wajja okugwa. Mu 1875 obuweereza bwa Robert Pearsall Smith obw'olukale bwakoma amangu wakati mu bibamuvunaana eby'empisa embi, era entambula eyali ebasitudde ne yeeggya ku ndaba ey'ensonyi eyo. Mu myaka egyaddirira Robert yava mu kukkiriza kwe baali bagabana n'ajja mu butakkiriza Katonda, era Hannah yasitula ennaku ey'ekyama ey'obufumbo obutaddayo kufuna ssanyu lyabwo ery'olubereberye. Era yali maama eyafiirwa emirundi mingi: ku baana be omusanvu, ana baafa nga bakyali bato, era n'abaziika mu kiseera ky'emyaka gye egy'obukulu obw'omu makkati. Ennaku zaayongera oluvannyuma, abaana abasatu abaawonawo okutuusa mu bukulu — Mary, Logan, ne Alys — bwe baava ku kukkiriza okw'Ekikristaayo kwe yali awaddeyo buli kimu okusigiriza. Muwala we Alys yafumbirwa omufirosoofa Bertrand Russell, oboolyawo eyasinga okumanyibwa eyatakkiririza mu myaka egyo.

Smith yayaniriza ennaku zino si na kwegaana wabula na theolojia y'emu gye yali abuulira. Yagaana okuzimba okukkiriza ku nneewulira. "Oyinza okwewulira bulungi oba oyinza okwewulira bubi," yawandiika, "naye newankubadde nneewulira ennungi wadde embi tezikwatako ku kintu ekituufu... Katonda bw'akwagala, tekikola nsonga, okusinziira ku mazima, oba weewulira nti akwagala oba toweewulira." Yayagalanga okujuliza Luther, eyali, Setaani bwe yamubuuza oba yeewulira ye ye nga mwana wa Katonda, n'addamu nti: "Nedda, siwulira n'akatono, naye mmanyi. Dda ennyuma wange, Setaani." Enjawulo eyo — wakati w'amazima ag'okwagala kwa Katonda n'okwewulira kwe okukyukakyuka okw'okwagala okwo — bwe businziiro bwe yayimirira ku ttaka bwe lyayuuguuma.

Ekibala ky'okunoonya kwe okuwanvu era okw'obulumi kyali emirembe egyanywera era egyaddibwamu. "Bwe nnamala okuyiga nti amazima ag'eddiini gasinga nnyo obukulu ku nneewulira zange ku mazima ago," yawandiika, "ne kyenva ndeka okutunuulira enneewulira zange, ne nnoonya kwokka okuzuula amazima, ne nfuuka bulijjo omusanyufu mu bulamu bwange obw'eddiini." Omuwala eyali akaabidde nti tamanyi ngeri ya kusaba yatuuka, ng'ayita mu kwewaayo, mu kuwummula kw'ateyaddayo kufiirwa oluvannyuma — ekyo "ekyama ky'obulamu obw'essanyu" kye yamala emyaka mingi egyasigala ng'akikaka ku balala.

Katonda ow'Okubudaabuda Kwonna

Okuva mu myaka gino ne muvaamu okukkiriza kwe okukuze: nti Katonda gwe yali, ng'abangi, alowoozezaako nga "omu ku bitonde ebisinga okwefaako, ebyefaayo byokka mu nsi yonna" yali mu mazima "bye bikontanya ddala." Ebyafaayo bye eby'omwoyo, Obutefaako bwa Katonda ne Engeri Gye Nnabuzuula (1903), bilondoola obulamu bwe ng'olukalala lw'"ebiseera" eby'eddiini, era obuzito bwabyo bwonna businzibwa mu sentensi gye yasanga ku lupapula olusooka olw'e Bayibuli ye: "Omulembe guno guzudde buto obutefaako bwa Katonda." Mu Katonda ow'Okubudaabuda Kwonna (1906) yanyweza okulaba kw'emu okw'obukwatampola okwa Kitaffe, ng'okwagala kwe, yakkiriza, kiteekwa okusukka n'okwa maama. Awamu n'okuwandiika kwe yeewaayo mu nkyukakyuka: yayamba okutandikawo Ekibiina ky'Abakazi Abakristaayo Abaziyiza Otamiiro (Woman's Christian Temperance Union) mu 1874, yaweereza ng'omukulembeze w'Ekitongole kyakyo eky'Okubuulira Enjiri, yawagira eddembe ly'abakazi ery'okuba n'eddoboozi ery'eddiini mu lujjudde, era n'ayogera ku lw'omulimu gw'eddembe ery'okulonda.

Mu myaka gye egy'enkomerero endwadde y'amagumba yamusiba mu ntebe ey'amanvuulu ne mu bulumi obungi, naye endowooza ye yeeyongera kutangaala. Obukadde, yawandiika, tebuteekwa kuba "kiseera kya nnaku era kya kukoowa"; nga tulina "Katonda waffe omulungi, atefaako, emabega wa byonna, obukadde bufuuka ekifo eky'okuwummuliramu ekisanyusa." Okumanya "nti Katonda gy'ali, era nti Katonda amala," yagamba, kwafuula "okufa okuba ekisuubizo ekiwoomu eky'ebiseera eby'omu maaso."

Nga ndi mu bukuumi mu kumanya nti Katonda gy'ali, era nti amala, nsanga obukadde nga bwa kusanyusa mu kaseera kano, n'okufa nga kisuubizo ekiwoomu eky'ebiseera eby'omu maaso.

— Hannah Whitall Smith, Obutefaako bwa Katonda

Hannah Whitall Smith yafiira mu Bungereza nga May 1, 1911, ng'alina emyaka nsanvu mu mwenda. Yali ayogeddeko ku nnaku ze mu bwesimbu — obufumbo obwafumitibwa, amalaalo ana amatono, abaana abaava ku kukkiriza kwe — era teyawangulwa na n'emu ku zo. Omugabo gwe si nkola wabula okukkiriza kumu, okunywevu, okwayisibwa nga kuyita mu bitabo ebikyasomebwa n'oluvannyuma lw'ekyasa: nti omugugu gw'enkyukakyuka yaffe ffe tegwali gwaffe kusitula, nti omulimu gw'omuntu kwe kwesiga kwokka ate omulimu gwa Katonda kwe kukola, era nti Katonda akola omulimu mulungi awatali kukoowa, awatali kwefaako.
