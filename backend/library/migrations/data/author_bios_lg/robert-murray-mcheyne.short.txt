Robert Murray M’Cheyne (1813–1843) yali omuweereza wa St Peter’s, Dundee, ajjukirwa olw’okunoonya obutukuvu bwe ye n’obusumba obw’ekisa. Yakola akalanda k’okusoma Bayibuli akakyakozesebwa, yeegatta ku lugendo olw’okunoonyereza ku Bayudaaya olwa 1839, era n’afa olwa typhus ng’aweza emyaka 29, ng’akungubagirwa ekibuga kyonna.
