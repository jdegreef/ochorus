Catherine Booth ajjukirwa mu mannya abiri. Erisooka lye lwe yaweebwa bazadde be e Ashbourne, mu Derbyshire, ku lunaku olw'17 Jjanwali 1829: Catherine Mumford, omuwala omukwatirivu era ayagala ennyo ebitabo eyandisomye Bayibuli ye emirundi munaana nga tannaweza myaka kkumi na ebiri. Ery'okubiri lye lyaweebwa ekibiina emyaka mingi oluvannyuma, abantu obukadde n'obukadde mu mawanga mangi bwe baamuyita mu linnya limu lyokka, nti Maama w'Eggye. Wakati w'amannya ago abiri waliwo obulamu obumu ku bwe bwaba n'ebivaamu ebisingira ddala obukulu mu Bukristaayo bw'omu kyasa eky'ekkumi n'omwenda, era, wakati waabwo, omukazi eyanywerera nti Katonda akyayogera, akyalokola, era akyayita omuntu yenna gw'ayagala okubuulira enjiri ye.

Obwana obw'omuntima omulungi n'obw'ebitabo

Catherine ye yali muwala omu yekka mu maka ag'Abamesodisiti Abawesuleeyaani, era yekka ku baganda be abasajja abana eyawangaala okutuuka okuba omusajja omukulu. Maama we yamuyigiriza okuva mu buto okwagala amazima n'okwagala Ebyawandiikibwa, era amasomo gombi ne ganywerera obulamu bwe bwonna. Nga waakaweza emyaka ena yeebaka nga akaaba mu kitanda kye okutuusa lwe yatuula obulimba obutono; nga wa myaka etaano yasoma emboozi za Bayibuli mu ddoboozi ery'omu ku lubaddu lwa maama we. Yali w'ekisa okutuuka ku kubonaabona olw'obukambwe, era omulundi gumu yadduka okuva awaka nga takyayinza kwogera olw'ennaku bwe yalaba omusajja ng'akuba endiga ye.

Teyabeeranga wa maanyi. Obulwadde obukakali obw'omugongo bwamuzza awaka okuva mu ssomero ery'ebbaadi okumala emyaka esatu emiwanvu, ekitundu ekinene ng'agalamidde bugalamivu, era mu kiseera kimu n'amala emyezi musanvu ng'agalamidde bugazza. Yakyusa okusibibwa okwo okufuuka essomero ery'emmeeme, ng'asoma Ebyawandiikibwa, ebya teoloji, n'obulamu bw'abo abaabeerera Katonda, oluvannyuma n'aggalawo buli kitabo n'awandiika ekyo kye yali ayinza okujjukira. Oluvannyuma yalaba ekisa ekyakwekebwa mu kino. "Okubeera nzekka nnyo mu buto bwange," bwe yagamba, "n'okusuulibwa bwe ntyo ku birowoozo byange n'ebyo ebyayogerwa mu bitabo, kinnyambye nnyo."

Okukyuka n'obukakafu bwe yali tayinza kubeera nabwo

Newankubadde Omwoyo gwa Katonda gwali gulwana naye okuva mu buto, Catherine yatuuka ku kizibu nga wa myaka nga kkumi n'etaano. Teyayinza kuwummula nga talina kutegeera kwa nkakafu nti alokoddwa, ng'agaana endowooza ey'okwewombeka nti obulamu bwonna obw'okugezaako okukola obulungi bwali bumala. "Mukama, nteekwa okukyuka," bwe yasaba. "Siyinza kuwummula okutuusa lw'onookyusa obuntu bwange bwonna; nkolere ekyo ky'okolera ababbi n'abatamiivu." Okumala wiiki mukaaga eggulu lyalabika nga likakkanidde. Yatambulatambula mu kisenge kye okutuusa essaawa munaana ez'ekiro, Bayibuli ye n'akatabo ke ak'ennyimba nga biri wansi w'omutto gwe, ng'asaba obukakafu.

Enkeera emu yazuukuka n'aggulawo akatabo ke ak'ennyimba ku bigambo bya Charles Wesley, nti "Katonda wange, ndi wuwo, / Kubudaabuda ki okuva mu Katonda, / Mukisa ki okumanya nti Yesu wange wange." Yali abisomye emirundi mingi, naye kaakano ne bituuka ku mutima gwe n'amaanyi amaggya. "Bya mazima! Nkkiriza! Ebibi byange bisonyiyiddwa. Ndi wuwo!" Emmeeme ye yonna n'ejjula ekitangaala. Nga tannaba kwambala yadduka okugenda mu kisenge kya maama we ng'akaaba, "Maama, maama, ndi mwana wa Katonda!" Wiiki omukaaga ez'okulwana ne zifuna okuddibwamu kwazo.

Obukakafu obwo tebwalwa kugezesebwa. Mutabani wa kojja we omugezi yamunyigiriza okwogeza naye, era newankubadde yamwagala yamanya nti oyo yali teyeewaayo eri Katonda. Ng'asaba ekitangaala, yasoma nti, "Temwesibanga wamu n'abatakkiriza ekyokolimu ekitasaana," era n'awuliramu eddoboozi lya Katonda. Yawandiika ebbaluwa okumenya okwogeza, era teyaddamu kubikkula nsonga eyo. Emyaka egiddirira yalaba nti singa yagoberera enneewulira ye mu kifo ky'okugondera, "obulamu bwe bwonna bwandikyuse." Obuwulize, ne bwe bwandibadde obw'ekisaale, bwali bufuuka enkola ey'okukkiriza kwe.

Obufumbo, n'okukolebwa kw'omubuulizi

Mu 1852 yasisinkana William Booth, omuweereza omuvubuka Omumesodisiti ow'obunyiikivu obw'amaanyi obw'okubuulira enjiri; baafumbiriganwa mu 1855. Obufumbo bwabwe bwali kukolagana kwa nzikiriza kwa mazima, wadde nga ku nsonga emu baawukana okumala ebbanga eddene. Catherine yali atuuse okukkiriza, okuva mu Byawandiikibwa byennyini, nti abakazi baalina ddembe lye limu ng'abasajja okwogerera Katonda. Empaka ez'omu lupapula lw'amawulire bwe zaayimuka ku nsonga eyo, yaziddamu mu kuwandiika ng'ayita mu katabo ke akakwata ku buweereza bw'abakazi, ng'awakanya nti oba Omwoyo Omutukuvu yali awa omukisa mu ngeri eyeeyoleka bwe ntyo emirimu gy'abakazi abeewaayo, kale okuziyiza kwali kuteekwa okuba okusoma okukyamu, kubanga "ekigambo n'Omwoyo tebiyinza kwewakanya." Yali alwanirira okuyitibwa kw'abakazi abalala ng'akyaziyiza okuyitibwa kwe yennyini.

Ekiseera eky'okusalirwa kyatuuka ku lunaku lwa Pentekooti, mu 1860, mu kkanisa e Gateshead. Ng'atudde mu kkuŋŋaaniro n'omwana we omukulu, ng'ennaku emukutte era nga talina kye asuubira, yawulira Omwoyo ng'amunyigiriza obuwulize bwennyini bwe yali akubirizza abalala. Buli nsonga ne yeeyimusa; oluvannyuma ekigambo eky'enkomerero eky'omukemi ne kisalawo ensonga. Yakiwandiika yennyini.

"Aaa!" ne ŋŋamba, "wano we waali ekinsigadde. Sinnaba kuba mwetegefu kubeera musiru ku lwa Kristo. Kaakano nnaabeera omu!" Awatali kuyimirira dda, ne nsituka okuva mu ntebe yange ne ntambula mu kkubo ery'omu kkanisa.

— Catherine Booth

Balo eyeewuunya teyayinza wabula okulangirira nti, "Mukyala wange omwagalwa ayagala okwogera," n'atuula. Yayimirira, bwe yagamba, ng'awanirira ku "mukono gw'Oyo Ayinza Byonna," era n'ayatula bugatuzi obujeemu bwe obw'ebbanga eddene. Waaliwo okukaaba okusingawo mu kkanisa ku lunaku olwo okusinga ku mulundi gwonna ogwaluggya. "Okwatula okwo okw'amazima," bwe yalowooza, "kwakola ekyo emyaka amakumi abiri egy'okubuulira gye gyandirembye okutuukiriza." Teyaddayo nnyuma nate.

Obuweereza n'ebibala byabwo

Okuyitibwa kwayiwa, kubanga buli we yagenda emmeeme zaalokolebwa. Yafuuka omu ku babuulizi abeeyoleka ennyo ab'omulembe ogwo, ng'ajjuza ebizimbe ebisinga obunene mu Bungereza n'abawuliriza ab'ekika kyonna. Naye tewali kyajja mangu. Ng'alina abaana abato bana, ssente ntono, era nga talina biseera bya kuwummula, yeeteekerateekera nga bw'ayonsa omwana we, ng'awandiika ebirowoozo n'ekkalaamu. Yagaana buli kwewaana; "obukakanyavu," bwe yagamba, bwe bwali ekiziyiza ekinene ky'amazima aga Katonda. Era teyandimatidde na kintu kyonna wabula okukyuka. Ku kkuŋŋaana erimu ery'abasajja eryali lijjudde yawandiika nti, "Nawulira ng'okuba ekintu ekitono okufiira awo n'awo, singa ekyo kyandibaleese eri Yesu."

Katonda yakufuula avunaanyizibwa, si olw'okutuusa amazima, wabula olw'OKUGATEEKA MUNDA—okugatuusa awaka, okuganyweza mu muntima ng'ekyuma ekyokya, ng'omusumaali, butereevu okuva ku ntebe ye ey'obwakabaka.

— Catherine Booth

Mu 1865 William yatandika okubuulira abaavu abasingira ddala mu buvanjuba bwa London, era ekibuuzo eky'engeri gye baali bagenda okubeeramu ku biweebwayo okuva mu baavu ne kinyiga nnyo. Okuddamu kwa Catherine ne kufuuka enkolongo ey'ebyafaayo. Okuva mu mulimu ogwo ogw'e Whitechapel ne kukula Omulimu gw'Abakristaayo (Christian Mission), era okuva mu Mulimu ogwo, mu 1878, ne kuzaalibwa Eggye ery'Obulokozi. Yayamba okukola enjigiriza yaalyo, yawandiika ekitundu ekinene ky'okuyigiriza kwalyo, yawaayo abaana be omu ku omu eri obuweereza bwalyo, era yafuula okwenkanankana kw'abakazi mu buweereza okuba etteeka erinywevu ery'ekibiina ekyo. Yali, mu buli ngeri, maama waakyo.

Obulamu obwawaniriddwa okusaba

Ekyama ky'okubuulira kwe tekyali kirabo kye eky'oku ttaala wabula ekisenge kye eky'omu kyama. Yapima ebivaamu bye n'okwegayiririra abalala kwe, era teyandiyimiridde mu maaso g'ekkuŋŋaaniro okutuusa ng'ayimiridde mu maaso ga Katonda.

Nga enkuŋŋaana ze tezinnatuuka Catherine yeggalirangamu wala, emirundi mingi mu bifo eby'obwannamunigina, era "ng'alwana era ng'eegayirira Katonda okumala essaawa nnyingi, mu maziga n'okubonaabona," oluvannyuma n'ayolekera ekibiina ng'ajjudde okwagala n'okukkiriza. "Bulijjo nsanga engerageranya entuufu mu bivaamu ng'egeraageranyizibwa n'omwoyo ogw'okwegayiririra gwe mbadde nagwo edda," bwe yawandiika. "Kyenva njagala okubeera nzekka." Bwe yategeera ekiragiro ky'omusawo eky'okufa, ekikolwa kye ekisooka, ng'ali yekka mu mmotoka, kyali kufukamira n'awaayo omubiri gwe, amaka ge, n'omulimu gwe eri Katonda.

William, ng'apima oba baali bayinza okuwangaala mu by'ensimbi wakati w'abaavu ab'omu buvanjuba bwa London, bwe yakubakuba, okuddamu kwe kwatwala obwesige bwe bumu: "Twesize Mukama omulundi gumu olw'okuliisibwa kwaffe, era tuyinza okumwesiga nate." Eggye eryakula okuva ku lunaku olwo olw'okukkiriza olumu lwaligenda okubuna ensi yonna.

Okukemebwa okw'enkomerero okw'omuliro

Mu Febbwaali 1888 Catherine yagenda yekka eri omukugu, ng'abuuza, nga bulijjo, amazima. Kyali kookolo, obulwadde bwe bumu bwe yali ayitiddemu maama we bwe yamujjanjaba, nga waliwo emyaka ebiri oba esatu egy'okubonaabona mu maaso. Yeebaza omusawo, n'avuga okudda awaka, era n'afukamira mu mmotoka okusaba. Ekirowoozo kye ekisooka, bwe yagamba William, kyali nnaku nti teyandibeeddewo okumujjanjaba ku nkomerero ye. Ekigambo kye eky'okubonaabona okuwanvu kyali bigambo bisatu: "Olutalo luteekwa okugenda mu maaso." Enkuŋŋaana zaategekebwa okuva mu kisenge kye eky'obulwadde, era ekitundu ekinene ky'enteekateeka ya William eya Mu Bungereza Omusinga Enzikiza ku lw'abo abaasuulibwa kyalowoozebwa ku lubaddu lw'ekitanda kye.

Okumala emyezi kkumi n'ebiri egy'enkomerero yali musibe mu kisenge kye mu bulumi obw'olubeerera, ng'azzibwamu amaanyi buli lipoota ey'obuwanguzi ku lwa Kristo. Ku lunaku olw'4 Okitobba 1890, e Clacton-on-Sea, yatuuka oluvannyuma ku mugga. Yalaga n'olugalo ekyawandiikibwa ekyali kiwaniddwa ku kisenge, nti "Ekisa kyange kikumala," era n'asaba kiteekebwe ku bigere by'ekitanda kye. " Owange Emma, leka ŋŋende, muganzi," yawuunyisa mu ddoboozi ery'omuwaawa, oluvannyuma nti, "Kaakano! Yee, Mukama, jjangu, Owange, jjangu!" Yali wa myaka nkaaga mu gumu.

Ku ntaana ye William Booth yakuŋŋaanya emyaka amakumi ana egy'obufumbo mu sentensi emu: yali kitangaala ky'amaaso ge era ensibuko y'okusikiriza kw'emmeeme ye, era ekyo abasajja abalala kye baafiirwa mu bantu bangi ye yali akifiiridde mu muntu omu. Yaleka ekibiina ekiragiro eky'okufa kye yali abeereddemu: "Okubudaabuda okukulu eri omuserikale w'Eggye ery'Obulokozi ng'ali ku kitanda kye eky'okufa kwe kuwulira nti abadde muwuzi wa mmeeme." Obusika bwe si nnyo bakadde ba bantu Eggye ery'Obulokozi lye liweerezza okuva olwo, wadde nga kwe kuli. Bwe nzikiriza enywevu, eyafunibwa okuyita mu ntiisa n'akookolo n'obuwulize obuwanvu, nti Katonda akyayita gw'ayagala, era akyaddamu okusaba kw'abo abeetegefu okuba abasiru ku lwa Kristo.
