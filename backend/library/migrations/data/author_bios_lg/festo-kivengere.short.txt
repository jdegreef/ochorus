Festo Kivengere (1919–1988) yali mulabirizi Munnayuganda Omwanglikani era omubuulizi w'enjiri ow'Okuzuukuka kw'Afirika ey'Obuvanjuba, gwe baatera okuyita “Billy Graham wa Afirika.” Yakyuka okuva mu kukkiriza okw'oku mimwa mu kuzuukuka okwa 1941, n'afuuka Omulabirizi wa Kigezi mu 1972 era omukulembeze wa African Evangelistic Enterprise. Oluvannyuma lw'okuttibwa kwa Ssaabalabirizi Janani Luwum mu 1977, yadduka Uganda ya Amin ng'ayita ku nsozi okuyingira Rwanda, n'oluvannyuma n'awandiika ekitabo ekyatumbulwa I Love Idi Amin, obujulizi ku kusonyiwa omuyinza omukambwe eyali amugobye mu buwaŋŋanguse.
