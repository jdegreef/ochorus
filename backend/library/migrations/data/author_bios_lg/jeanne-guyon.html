Jeanne-Marie Bouvier de la Motte Guyon yazaalibwa nga 13 Apuli, 1648, mu kibuga Montargis, mu bukiikaddyo bwa Paris, mu maka ag'obugagga era ag'okutya Katonda. Okuva mu myaka gye egy'obuto yasikirizibwa okudda eri Katonda, naye era, ng'ekyennyini bwe yakyeyogerako mu bwesimbu, okudda eri obutaliimu n'okusiimibwa okuwuubaawuuba okw'ensi eyawagira obulungi bwe n'amagezi ge. Ng'ayigirizibwa ng'oluwalo na bannaddiini ab'Ursuline n'ab'Abenedikito, yali muwala ow'omutima omunafu era ow'okwegomba okutali kutereera, oluusi ng'anoonya obutukuvu ate oluusi ng'aserengeta okudda mu malala g'obulamu obwangu. Oluvannyuma yandirabye emyaka gino awatali kwerimba, ng'ayogera obutaliimu bwe ye mu bwesimbu nga bw'ayogera okutya Katonda kwe.

Ng'alina emyaka kkumi n'etaano, mu 1664, maama we yamugabula mu bufumbo eri Jacques Guyon, omusajja omugagga eyamusinga emyaka amakumi abiri mu ebiri. Tebwali bugatta bwa ssanyu. Amaka gaali gafugibwa nnyazaala eyayagala okufuga, ne Jeanne, omuto era ow'ennyiiko, yalaba awaka we ng'ekifo ky'okubonaabona okwa buli lunaku. Mu baana be be yazaala, bangi baafa nga bakyali bato; ennaku yafuuka munne ow'amangu era ow'okumanyibwa. Naye kyali kwennyini mu nnyumba eno enfunda, ey'ennaku, Katonda mwe yatandikira omulimu gwe ogw'omunda ennyo mu ye, ng'amuyigiriza olw'okumalibwa amaanyi okunoonya essanyu ensi ly'etasobola kuwa.

Ekigambo Ekyaggula Omutima Gwe

Okumala emyaka mingi Jeanne yali afubye mu kusaba nga mu mulimu, ng'afuba okuweereza naye ng'afuna kukalu kwokka. Enkyukakyuka yajja mu 1668, omusajja Omufransikaani, eyennyini eyali akyakomyewo okuva mu kwesuula okuwanvu, bwe yamuwa akayogero kamu ak'okubudaabuda ke yandisitudde obulamu bwe bwonna. Okufuba kwe kwali kugudde, yamugamba, kubanga yali anoonya ebweru wuwe kye yali alina dda munda; ka ayige okunoonya Katonda munda mu mutima gwe ye, era anaamusanga eyo. Okuva mu kaseera ako, yawandiika, omutima gwe gwakyuka kyonna. Okusaba kwe yali agoberedde awatali bibala okumala ebbanga eddene kwaweebwa amangwago — si nga ekituukiddwako, naye nga okuzuula ekyali eyo ebbanga lyonna, nga kirindirira okumanyibwa.

Oluvannyuma lw'okufa kwa bba, Guyon yeewaayo eri ekyo kye yayita okusaba kw'omutima — okukyusa okw'omwoyo okwangu, okusirikira, era okw'olubeerera okudda eri Katonda, kwe yakola si mu ssaawa ezaateekebwawo zokka naye ne mu mirimu egya bulijjo egya buli lunaku. Yayigiriza nti okusaba kwali mulimu gwa mutima okusinga mulimu gwa magezi, era nti ekibala kyakwo kyali kukkiriza okunywevu era okuteefu. Yali mpisa eno ey'okukuŋŋaanya omutima munda, eyakuzibwa mu bubombeefu ebbanga eddene nga tannawandiika kigambo kyonna eri abantu, eyafuuka ensulo y'ekintu kyonna kye yaleka oluvannyuma eri ekkanisa.

Okufa kwa bba mu 1676 kwaleka Jeanne nnamwandu ow'emyaka amakumi abiri mu munaana, omugagga era ow'eddembe. Yandisobodde okulonda obwangu. Mu kifo ky'ekyo yakyuka mu bunywevu okudda eri Katonda, n'okudda eri obulamu obwandimuwaliriza kumpi buli kimu. Wansi w'obukulembeze bw'omusaseerdooti Omubarnabito François La Combe, yaleka ensi ye ey'obuteefu n'atambula okuyita mu Savoy ne Switzerland — okutuuka e Geneva, Annecy, Grenoble, Turin — ng'ayigiriza enkola ye ey'okusaba okw'omunda eri ebifo by'abaddiini n'eri abantu aba bulijjo. Yayiwa obugagga bwe mu kugaba n'amaanyi ge mu buweereza, ng'akakasa nti omwoyo ogusinga obwangu, ogutayize era omwavu, gwandisobodde okusaba mu mazima ng'omuyize yenna.

Ekkubo lyokka erituusa mu Ggulu kwe kusaba; okusaba okw'omutima, buli omu kw'asobola, si kw'okuwakana okuli ebibala by'okusoma. Ka abaavu bajje, ka abatategeera n'ab'omubiri bajje; ka abaana abatalina magezi wadde okumanya bajje, era banaafuuka ab'amagezi.

— Jeanne Guyon

Obuweereza Obwabuusibwabuusibwa

Mu 1685 Guyon yafulumya Enkola Empi era Eyangu ey'Okusaba, akatabo akatono akandiwangaddewo okusinga buli mpaka eyamwetooloola. Mu ako yakikakata nti abakkiriza bonna, awatali kusosolako, bayitiddwa era basobozeddwa okusaba — nti Katonda si bwannamunigina bw'ekifo ky'abaddiini naye kye kitundu ekiri okumpi era ekiramu eky'omulimi n'omwana. Mu mwaka gwe gumu yamaliriza okunnyonnyola ku Oluyimba Olusinga. Ebiwandiiko bye byasaasaana amangu, era wamu nabyo obuyinza bwe mu mutima ddala ogwa France. Mu 1688 yasisinkana François Fénelon, omulabirizi omukulu ow'omu maaso ow'e Cambrai, n'omukwano gwabwe ogw'omwoyo ne gufuuka gumu ku egisinga obukulu mu byafaayo by'eddiini y'Abafransa. Okumala ekiseera yasembezebwa mu lubiri, ng'akolebwa mukwano gwa Madame de Maintenon, era ng'aweebwa ekitiibwa nga omukazi ow'okuweereza okutali kwa bulijjo.

Naye okuyigiriza kwe kwatiisa abakuumi b'enjigiriza entuufu. Olulimi lwe olw'okwewaayo, olw'okwagala okulongoofu era okutalina bwekaatiro, olw'okukkiriza Katonda awatali kuziyiza, kwawulikika eri abamunenya nga kuli kumpi mu kabi n'okusirika (Quietism) okwavumirirwa okwa Miguel de Molinos. Omukulu mu bamuwakanya yeeyimusa Jacques-Bénigne Bossuet, Omulabirizi ow'amaanyi ow'e Meaux, eyalaba mu njigiriza ye ekiviirako obugayaavu obw'omwoyo n'obutafaayo obw'empisa. Ekyo ekyali okwagalibwa kyakyuka okufuuka okubuusabuusa. Enkuŋŋaana ez'e Issy mu 1694 ne 1695 zaakebera okuyigiriza kwe, era wadde nga tezaamutuuma linnya, zaggalawo oluggi eri ekitiibwa kye eky'olubwatuka.

Amakomera

Emyaka egyaddirira gyali myaka gya kusibibwa. La Combe yali asiddwa dda mu 1687, awatali kutebekawo, amagezi ge nga gagwa wansi w'okusibibwa okuwanvu. Guyon yennyini, oluvannyuma lw'okubuuzibwa e Meaux, yakwatibwa mu Desemba 1695 n'asibirwa e Vincennes, ne Vaugirard, era ku nkomerero, mu 1698, mu Bastille. Ebbaluwa ez'obulimba zaakolebwa okumuvunaana; yaggyibwako sakramenti, n'akolwa obubi era n'abeera mu bucaafu, era oluusi ng'atya okuweebwa obutwa. Teyatebekebwa okutuusa mu Maaki 1703 — nga emyaka gyonna ana mu kkomera eddene ery'olusiisira, era nga kumpi munaana mu kusibibwa awamu.

Ekyewuunyisa si nti yabonaabona, naye engeri gye yabonaabonamu. Yawandiika nti amayinja gennyini ag'ekkomera lye gaamasamasa mu maaso ge nga masanga, kubanga yali ajjudde okubeerawo kwa Katonda. Yayimba mu kasenge ke ak'ekkomera. Mu nnyiriri ezaavvuunulwa oluvannyuma mu Lungereza omuwandiisi w'ebitontome William Cowper, yeegeraageranya n'akanyonyi akasibiddwa mu kadobo akatuula ne kayimbira Oyo eyakateeka eyo — nga kamativu, singa kwali kwe kwagala kwe, okumalira ekitundu ekisigadde eky'obulamu bwe emabega w'ebisenge biri. Kuno kwe kwali okukungula okw'okusaba okw'omutima okwakuzibwa okumala ebbanga eddene: omwoyo ogwanywerera nnyo eri Katonda ne bisenge bina nga tebisobola kugufunzako.

Mu Bastille, ng'aggyiddwako sakramenti era ng'asuubira ayinza okufiira mu kkomera, Guyon yasaba si olw'okuteebwa naye olw'ekisa eky'okukkiriza kyonna Katonda kye yayagala. Emirembe gye yasaba yagiweebwa mu bujjuvu ne kigira nti yalaga okusibibwa kwe ng'ekifo ky'essanyu — ng'alangirira nti amayinja g'akasenge ke gaamulabikira ng'amasanga ag'omuwendo, era ng'ayimba ennyimba ez'okutendereza mu kifo obuweddemu essuubi we bwandibadde ekya bulijjo. Okununulibwa kwe yaweebwa si kwa mubiri okusooka naye kwa mwoyo: eddembe mu kkomera abakuumi be lye baali batasobola kuwa wadde okuggyawo, era eryawangaala okusinga enjegere ze.

Omuyaga ogw'ekitheoloji gwatuuka ku ntikko yaagwo okwetooloola Fénelon, eyakuuma okwagala okulongoofu mu Ebiragiro by'Abatukuvu bye kwavumirirwa Paapa Innocent XII mu 1699. Fénelon yeewaayo amangwago era mu bwetoowaze. Erinnya lya Guyon yennyini lyafuuka, mu France, kumpi enkyusa y'ekyava ekyabuusibwabuusibwa, era ekitundu ekinene eky'omulimu gwe kyasuulibwa ku Olukalala lw'ebitabo ebyaziyizibwa. Yali afuuse, mu maaso g'ekkanisa lye, akabi — omukazi ono ebyokulwanyisa bye byokka bye byali okusaba n'ekkalaamu.

Okufa n'Obusika

Ng'akomyewo eddembe ku nkomerero, Guyon yamalira emyaka gye egy'enkomerero wansi w'obuziyiza e Blois, okumpi ne muwala we. Yeeyongera okuwandiika n'okuwandiikira ekibiina ekigazi eky'abayigirizwa, bangi ku bo kaakano nga Bapoormingi abaasanga mu ye eddiini eyennyini ey'omunda ennono zaabwe ze zaawagira. Yagumira okuziyizibwa kwe nga bwe yagumira akasenge ke ak'ekkomera — mu kutebenkera, era awatali bukyayi. Yafiira e Blois nga 9 Juuni, 1717, ng'alina emyaka nkaaga mu mwenda, ng'asabye ekkanisa ekisonyiwo bye yali atali agenderedde kunuma era ng'akwasa omwoyo gwe eri Katonda gwe yali anoonyezza munda mu mutima gwe okumala ekitundu ky'ekyasa.

Ndi kanyonyi katono, akasibiddwa okuva mu nnimiro z'empewo; era mu kadobo kange ntuula ne nyimbira Oyo eyanteeka wano.

— Jeanne Guyon

Ng'avumiriddwa mu France ey'Abakatoliki, yasembezebwa n'emikono egyanjuluze awalala. Omuwandiisi Omuholandi Pierre Poiret yakuŋŋaanya n'afulumya emirimu gye; Omupietisti Omudaaki Gerhard Tersteegen yatwala ebitontome bye mu mutima ogw'Abapoormingi ogwa Bulaaya. John Wesley yafunza ebyafaayo bye eby'obulamu, ng'atendereza okuweereza kwe naye ng'alabula ku busukkulumu bwe. Ab'Abakwaka baakozesa ebiwandiiko bye ku bitabo byabwe eby'okusaba okusirika; ebibinja by'Obutukuvu n'Obulamu Obw'Oku Waggulu eby'omu kyasa eky'ekkumi n'omwenda, n'abawandiisi ab'oluvannyuma nga A. W. Tozer n'omuyigiriza Omucayina Watchman Nee, baateeka ebitabo bye ebitono mu mikono gy'emirembe emipya egyalina enjala ey'obuziba bwa Katonda.

Olugero lwe si lwa kwongera bulungi lwa mikwano. Yalina enzikiriza ekkanisa lye lyennyini ze lyalaba nga ez'ensobi, era abasomi abeesigwa bapimye okuyigiriza kwe n'obwegendereza okumala ebbanga. Naye okuyita mu byasa bisatu na buli kisenge ky'oluawula olw'ennono, ensa y'obujulizi bwe eyawangaala — nti Katonda ali okumpi okusinga bwe tumanyi, nti omwoyo ogusinga obwetoowaze gusobola okumunoonya n'okumusanga mu mutima, era nti obulamu obuweereddwayo kyonna eri okusaba busobola okuyimba mu kkomera n'okuyita amayinja gaabwo amasanga. Obujulizi obwo, obwagezesebwa mu muliro ogw'okubonaabona okw'amazima, ye nsonga lwaki ebitabo bye ebitono byawangaala okusinga abasajja abakulu abaamusirisa.
