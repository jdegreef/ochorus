John “Hyde Omusabi” (1865–1912) yali muminsani Omumerika ow’Ekkanisa ya Presbyterian mu Punjab, ajjukirwa okusinga byonna ng’omusajja w’okusaba — omu ku basinga obukulu mu lukuŋŋaana lw’e Sialkot okuva mu mwaka gwalwo ogwasooka era omu ku basinga okuwagira Ekibiina ky’Okusaba eky’e Punjab, ajjukirwa ng’omusajja eyasaba Katonda emmeeme emu buli lunaku. Ekitundu ekinene ku linnya lye erimanyiddwa ennyo kiva mu kitabo eky’okusinza ekya Francis McGaw ekya 1923, “Praying Hyde,” ekyawandiikibwa ng’amaze okufa.
