Muwandiisi Omungereza era omubuulizi Omupuritani, omuddaabiriza w'ensaka ow'e Bedford eyasibibwa mu kkomera okumala emyaka egisukka mu kkumi olw'okubuulira awatali lukusa. Mu kusibibwa okwo mwe yawandiikira ekitabo The Pilgrim's Progress, kimu ku ngero ez'ebifaananyi ezisinga okukubibwa mu kyapa mu lulimi Olungereza.
