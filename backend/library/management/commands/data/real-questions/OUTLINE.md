# Real Questions: series outline

Honest answers for readers aged 13–17 (Ochorus Originals) to the questions teenagers actually ask. The manuscripts in this folder (`real-questions-<n>.md`) are the source of truth; `build_real_questions` converts them and enforces the shape (Introduction, 30 question-titled chapters, Conclusion; every question chapter has "The short answer", "Think about it" and "Go deeper").

## Stance (founder decision, 2026-10-08)

**Mainstream evangelical.** Core doctrine is answered plainly, as most evangelical churches and the library's own authors do: Scripture as God's trustworthy Word; Jesus fully God and fully man, crucified and bodily risen; salvation by grace through faith in Christ alone; hell real; sex within marriage between a man and a woman; humanity made male and female. Always with compassion, never contempt (toward other religions, atheists, LGBTQ people, or other Christians).

Where **faithful Christians genuinely differ**, the main views are set out fairly, the common ground is named, and the book does not pick a side: the age of the earth; eternal security; women in church leadership; end-times timelines; miracles today (continuationist / cessationist); alcohol; and, within the core-doctrine chapters, secondary points such as annihilationism or the fate of those who never hear, which are named as minority or open views.

## Format

- Each question: **The short answer** (two or three sentences); the longer answer (550–750 words, BSB Scripture quoted inline with references); **Think about it**; **Go deeper** (one real chapter of a library classic, summarised truly; no chapter used twice in a volume).
- Sensitive chapters (grief, death, depression, sex, same-sex attraction, gender, dating) carry the trusted-adult and self-harm lines and, where relevant, the body-safety line. Never recommend coercive "therapy".
- Does not repeat Anchored's angle on a topic it already covers; Real Questions answers objections.
