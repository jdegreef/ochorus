## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **God pours out His Spirit on those whom the world overlooks, and He makes them messengers of His word.** Chapter 2, *Your Daughters Will Prophesy* (Joel 2:28)
- **Christ meets those who honestly cry out to Him, even those who once fought against Him.** Chapter 7, *Prayer Is Breathing* (Psalm 16:11)
- **The new birth turns set phrases into the prayer of a child speaking to a Father.** Chapter 12, *Prayer Bands Across Asia* (Acts 3:19-20)

### Learning to Pray

- **The longing for God is itself a gift we may ask God to give.** Chapter 20, *The Pursuit of God* (Psalm 42:1)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **Like a sea creature rising for air, we must keep coming up to God in prayer if we are to live in a drowning world.** Chapter 7, *Prayer Is Breathing* (Psalm 16:11)
- **Learning to pray in quiet days prepares us to pray in dark ones.** Chapter 14, *My Times Are in Your Hand* (Psalm 31:15)
- **The busiest life still needs unhurried time alone with God.** Chapter 20, *The Pursuit of God* (Psalm 42:1)

### Asking Boldly

- **The Spirit comes to people who wait for Him in prayer, and He gives power so that they can bear witness to Jesus.** Chapter 3, *A Stable on Azusa Street* (Acts 1:8)
- **The church’s greatest work is to ask God boldly for what He Himself wants done on earth.** Chapter 13, *The Prayer Ministry of the Church* (Matthew 18:18)

### Persistence

- **Revival is God’s gift in God’s time, so we may keep believing and waiting without quarreling with Him.** Chapter 18, *Two Old Sisters on Lewis* (Isaiah 44:3)

### Faith

- **Every child rescued from danger is a reason to trust God with the next one.** Chapter 8, *Let the Children Come* (Mark 10:14)
- **Faith that moves mountains is not confidence in our own faith but trust in God Himself.** Chapter 9, *Only Believe* (Mark 11:22)
- **God gives His words to those who come to Him feeling they have none.** Chapter 11, *Here Am I, Send Me* (Isaiah 6:8)
- **God’s promise to hold us up is meant to be prayed back to Him on the hardest days.** Chapter 16, *Over the Mountains with a Hundred Children* (Isaiah 41:10)
- **God’s word does not return empty, so we can pray and speak it with confidence.** Chapter 19, *The Praying Men of Charlotte* (Isaiah 55:11)

### Humility and Mercy

- **The Spirit’s filling shows itself in plain obedience: confessing sin, putting away doubtful things and owning Christ in public.** Chapter 1, *"Bend Me"* (Ephesians 5:18)
- **When we pray for revival, we must count ourselves among those who need it.** Chapter 2, *Your Daughters Will Prophesy* (Joel 2:28)
- **God’s presence does not wait for fine buildings, and a poor room can become a house of prayer for every nation.** Chapter 3, *A Stable on Azusa Street* (Acts 1:8)
- **Real repentance goes beyond tears and sets right what it can.** Chapter 4, *Everyone Praying Aloud Together* (1 John 1:9)
- **We should not accuse others before God, but thank Him for the good He is doing in them.** Chapter 6, *Sowing in Tears* (Psalm 126:5-6)
- **God can make a fearless servant out of someone who has no education and no fine words.** Chapter 9, *Only Believe* (Mark 11:22)
- **We may pray boldly about the events of history while leaving the verdict on our prayers to God.** Chapter 10, *Standing in the Gap for a World at War* (Ezekiel 22:30)
- **Before God sends a messenger, He cleanses the messenger, even of small sins.** Chapter 11, *Here Am I, Send Me* (Isaiah 6:8)
- **A church can be busy and still be asleep, and only the light of Christ can wake it.** Chapter 17, *Sleeper, Wake Up* (Ephesians 5:14)
- **A heart thirsty for God Himself is the first sign of revival, before any crowd or program.** Chapter 20, *The Pursuit of God* (Psalm 42:1)

### Forgiveness

- **Hidden hatred blocks the prayers of a church, and confessing it can open the way for God to bless.** Chapter 4, *Everyone Praying Aloud Together* (1 John 1:9)
- **Repentance becomes real when we name our sins one by one and turn from each of them.** Chapter 12, *Prayer Bands Across Asia* (Acts 3:19-20)
- **The love to forgive an enemy is Christ’s gift, given to those who honestly ask Him for it.** Chapter 15, *Give Thanks for the Fleas* (1 Thessalonians 5:18)
- **Walking in the light means putting things right with one another, and it can turn old rivals into a praying family.** Chapter 17, *Sleeper, Wake Up* (Ephesians 5:14)

### Thanksgiving

- **Praise belongs in intercession, because it lifts the heaviness that blocks our prayers.** Chapter 6, *Sowing in Tears* (Psalm 126:5-6)
- **Giving thanks in a dark place is a way of confessing that God has not left it.** Chapter 15, *Give Thanks for the Fleas* (1 Thessalonians 5:18)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **Being filled with the Spirit begins when we ask God to bend our own will.** Chapter 1, *"Bend Me"* (Ephesians 5:18)
- **God can take the longings of our old life and turn them toward Himself.** Chapter 5, *At the Break of Dawn* (Psalm 46:5)
- **It is better to be sent by God than to run ahead of Him, however good our reasons.** Chapter 11, *Here Am I, Send Me* (Isaiah 6:8)
- **Prayer that claims to agree with heaven must be tested by Scripture, not by the strength of our feelings.** Chapter 13, *The Prayer Ministry of the Church* (Matthew 18:18)
- **Our times are safe in God’s hand, even when cruel people hold our days in theirs.** Chapter 14, *My Times Are in Your Hand* (Psalm 31:15)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **When a nation’s night is long, God’s people can keep watch for His morning.** Chapter 5, *At the Break of Dawn* (Psalm 46:5)
- **Joy in God’s presence can go with us on the hardest road.** Chapter 7, *Prayer Is Breathing* (Psalm 16:11)
- **God sometimes takes us out of the battle so that we are free to pray.** Chapter 8, *Let the Children Come* (Mark 10:14)
- **Faith keeps trusting God when the answer is slow or different from what we asked.** Chapter 9, *Only Believe* (Mark 11:22)
- **When we cannot tell who we really are, we can still tell God that we belong to Him.** Chapter 14, *My Times Are in Your Hand* (Psalm 31:15)
- **God may hide His kindness inside the very things we complain about.** Chapter 15, *Give Thanks for the Fleas* (1 Thessalonians 5:18)
- **Courage is not the absence of fear, but fear brought to God one day at a time.** Chapter 16, *Over the Mountains with a Hundred Children* (Isaiah 41:10)

### Praying Together

- **The unity the Spirit gives across race and nation is easily lost, so it must be guarded with humble love.** Chapter 3, *A Stable on Azusa Street* (Acts 1:8)
- **When believers pray aloud together with one heart, their many voices can rise to God as one prayer.** Chapter 4, *Everyone Praying Aloud Together* (1 John 1:9)
- **A church that meets God at daybreak learns to face the day’s troubles together.** Chapter 5, *At the Break of Dawn* (Psalm 46:5)
- **A company that keeps praying night after night can carry a burden too heavy for one person.** Chapter 10, *Standing in the Gap for a World at War* (Ezekiel 22:30)
- **New believers grow strong when they are gathered into small bands that pray and go out together.** Chapter 12, *Prayer Bands Across Asia* (Acts 3:19-20)
- **Believers need one another in prayer, because some answers come only when we pray together.** Chapter 13, *The Prayer Ministry of the Church* (Matthew 18:18)
- **The simple faith of a child in danger can call grown believers back to prayer.** Chapter 16, *Over the Mountains with a Hundred Children* (Isaiah 41:10)
- **Two believers who kneel as equals over God’s word can become the seed of a movement that crosses every line.** Chapter 17, *Sleeper, Wake Up* (Ephesians 5:14)
- **God often answers many hidden prayers together, so no one who prays should claim the revival as their own.** Chapter 18, *Two Old Sisters on Lewis* (Isaiah 44:3)
- **Ordinary believers who pray for the preacher share in the harvest that follows.** Chapter 19, *The Praying Men of Charlotte* (Isaiah 55:11)

### Praying for Others

- **Even those whom God uses greatly are clay pots, and they need care, rest and our prayers.** Chapter 1, *"Bend Me"* (Ephesians 5:18)
- **Praying by name for many people is a work that ordinary believers can share.** Chapter 2, *Your Daughters Will Prophesy* (Joel 2:28)
- **Tears shed in prayer for others are seeds that God will one day turn into a harvest of joy.** Chapter 6, *Sowing in Tears* (Psalm 126:5-6)
- **When we pray for children at risk, we share both the anger and the welcome of Jesus.** Chapter 8, *Let the Children Come* (Mark 10:14)
- **Standing in the gap means letting another’s need become our own burden before God.** Chapter 10, *Standing in the Gap for a World at War* (Ezekiel 22:30)
- **No one is too old, too weak or too hidden to pray for revival.** Chapter 18, *Two Old Sisters on Lewis* (Isaiah 44:3)
- **We can pray for workers God has not yet called, and He may already be preparing them.** Chapter 19, *The Praying Men of Charlotte* (Isaiah 55:11)
