## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **Prayer is a friendship with Jesus, kept alive by frequent and honest conversation.** Chapter 6, *Prayer as Friendship* (John 15:15)
- **We can work carefully without working anxiously, because our Father holds our hand.** Chapter 9, *Prayer for Busy People* (Colossians 3:17)
- **Christ does not throw away bruised people; He bends down to mend them.** Chapter 13, *The Bruised Reed* (Isaiah 42:3)

### Learning to Pray

- **The simple words we learned as children can become a lifelong school of prayer.** Chapter 2, *A Simple Way to Pray* (Psalm 1:2)
- **God wants us to pray with our minds as well as our hearts, in words we understand.** Chapter 4, *Praying with the Mind Also* (1 Corinthians 14:15)
- **In prayer, loving much matters more than thinking much.** Chapter 6, *Prayer as Friendship* (John 15:15)
- **True devotion fits our calling and makes us better at it.** Chapter 9, *Prayer for Busy People* (Colossians 3:17)
- **A life of prayer can be built from God’s own words, gathered one by one.** Chapter 11, *At Midnight I Rise* (Psalm 119:62)
- **Prayer is a way to taste God’s goodness, not only to talk about it.** Chapter 12, *Taste and See* (Psalm 34:8)
- **We learn to pray by praying, so we must be patient with ourselves and with others.** Chapter 13, *The Bruised Reed* (Isaiah 42:3)
- **Meditation turns truths we know into joys we feel.** Chapter 14, *Set Your Mind on Things Above* (Colossians 3:1-2)
- **Prayer is pouring out the heart to God, not putting on a show of fine words.** Chapter 15, *A Heart Without Words* (Psalm 62:8)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **The busier the day ahead, the more it needs to begin with God.** Chapter 1, *Too Busy Not to Pray* (Psalm 46:1)
- **Thinking ahead in prayer about the people we will meet helps us meet them with gentleness.** Chapter 9, *Prayer for Busy People* (Colossians 3:17)
- **We can keep up a quiet conversation with God while our hands are busy.** Chapter 16, *Among the Pots and Pans* (Psalm 16:8)

### Persistence

- **Praying for a nation is the work of a lifetime, not of one dramatic night.** Chapter 5, *Give Me Scotland* (Romans 10:1)
- **Even in despair, the safest place to be is at the feet of Christ in prayer.** Chapter 15, *A Heart Without Words* (Psalm 62:8)
- **In time, remembering God can become easier than forgetting Him.** Chapter 16, *Among the Pots and Pans* (Psalm 16:8)

### Faith

- **God’s promises are the firm ground we stand on when we pray.** Chapter 1, *Too Busy Not to Pray* (Psalm 46:1)
- **God’s command and promise, not our own worth, make our prayers welcome.** Chapter 2, *A Simple Way to Pray* (Psalm 1:2)
- **Prayer is how faith takes hold of the treasures God has promised.** Chapter 3, *The Principal Exercise of Faith* (Hebrews 4:16)
- **Feeling nothing in prayer is not the same as being left by God.** Chapter 7, *The Dark Night* (Isaiah 50:10)
- **The secret place with God is a home to live in, not only a shelter to run to.** Chapter 10, *Prayer in a Secret Place* (Psalm 91:1)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Humble sorrow for our sins and bold confidence in God’s mercy belong together in prayer.** Chapter 3, *The Principal Exercise of Faith* (Hebrews 4:16)
- **Never stop praying because you feel unworthy, for prayer is the road back to God.** Chapter 6, *Prayer as Friendship* (John 15:15)
- **We cannot see our own hearts clearly, so we ask God to search them.** Chapter 8, *Search Me, O God* (Psalm 139:23-24)
- **We may come to Christ’s table as we are, because He bore our blame.** Chapter 12, *Taste and See* (Psalm 34:8)
- **When we forget God, we can simply turn back to Him without long discouragement.** Chapter 16, *Among the Pots and Pans* (Psalm 16:8)
- **Mercy to an enemy in danger can speak louder than any argument.** Chapter 17, *Prayer in the Fire* (Matthew 5:10)

### Forgiveness

- **After a great failure, God can still give a believer courage to stand at the end.** Chapter 4, *Praying with the Mind Also* (1 Corinthians 14:15)
- **Remembering how Christians have wronged one another should lead us to repent and to pray for one another.** Chapter 17, *Prayer in the Fire* (Matthew 5:10)

### Thanksgiving

- **Every truth God teaches us can become thanksgiving, confession and request.** Chapter 2, *A Simple Way to Pray* (Psalm 1:2)
- **Looking back over the day with God begins with thanks, not with blame.** Chapter 8, *Search Me, O God* (Psalm 139:23-24)
- **The night hours, too, can be filled with thanksgiving.** Chapter 11, *At Midnight I Rise* (Psalm 119:62)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **In the dark we are asked to lean on God, not to make our own light.** Chapter 7, *The Dark Night* (Isaiah 50:10)
- **Noticing what draws us toward God or away from Him helps us find His way.** Chapter 8, *Search Me, O God* (Psalm 139:23-24)
- **Our struggles with God, told to Him honestly, can lead us into the freedom of His service.** Chapter 12, *Taste and See* (Psalm 34:8)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **When the danger is greatest, prayer is our safest fortress.** Chapter 1, *Too Busy Not to Pray* (Psalm 46:1)
- **Not every darkness is the same, and wise love helps us tell them apart.** Chapter 7, *The Dark Night* (Isaiah 50:10)
- **God can keep His people praying when every outward support has been taken away.** Chapter 10, *Prayer in a Secret Place* (Psalm 91:1)
- **Remembering the joy ahead gives us courage for the troubles of today.** Chapter 14, *Set Your Mind on Things Above* (Colossians 3:1-2)
- **A prison can take away our freedom, but it cannot shut the door between us and God.** Chapter 15, *A Heart Without Words* (Psalm 62:8)
- **Those who suffer for doing right are blessed, because the kingdom of heaven is already theirs.** Chapter 17, *Prayer in the Fire* (Matthew 5:10)

### Praying Together

- **Praying the same words together makes a scattered people into one praying family.** Chapter 4, *Praying with the Mind Also* (1 Corinthians 14:15)
- **Praying for faithful pastors and true teaching is a gift to the whole church.** Chapter 5, *Give Me Scotland* (Romans 10:1)
- **Ordinary believers can pass on prayer when no pastor is there to lead them.** Chapter 10, *Prayer in a Secret Place* (Psalm 91:1)

### Praying for Others

- **A heart that loves its own people will carry them to God in prayer.** Chapter 5, *Give Me Scotland* (Romans 10:1)
- **We can pray for those who have no one else to pray for them.** Chapter 11, *At Midnight I Rise* (Psalm 119:62)
- **Those who serve people must first plead with God for them.** Chapter 14, *Set Your Mind on Things Above* (Colossians 3:1-2)

### Christ Who Prays for Us

- **Because Jesus is our high priest, the throne we approach is a throne of grace.** Chapter 3, *The Principal Exercise of Faith* (Hebrews 4:16)
- **A weak and confused prayer that comes from a true desire is precious to God.** Chapter 13, *The Bruised Reed* (Isaiah 42:3)
