## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **God is a Father to orphans, and He loves to meet their needs through people who pray.** Chapter 3, *An Orphanage Built on Prayer* (Psalm 68:5)

### Learning to Pray

- **Prayer is the heart turning to God, so even the simplest believer can pray.** Chapter 1, *Be Still and Know* (Psalm 46:10)
- **Children learn to pray when they see prayer kept faithfully at home.** Chapter 6, *The Apron over Her Head* (Psalm 31:20)
- **A true believer needs prayer as a living body needs breath.** Chapter 8, *Oh, That You Would Tear the Heavens* (Isaiah 64:1)
- **Prayer grows wider when we remember the whole church and the whole world, not only our own congregation.** Chapter 16, *The Prayer Call of 1784* (Isaiah 54:2)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **A few words of Scripture, read slowly, can feed the soul more than many words read in a hurry.** Chapter 1, *Be Still and Know* (Psalm 46:10)
- **A crowded, noisy home can still hold a hidden place for meeting God.** Chapter 6, *The Apron over Her Head* (Psalm 31:20)
- **Prayer with others must grow from prayer alone with God.** Chapter 9, *A Concert of Prayer* (Zechariah 8:21)

### Asking Boldly

- **God has chosen to do His work in answer to the prayers of His people.** Chapter 7, *"God Does Nothing but in Answer to Prayer"* (James 4:2)
- **We may boldly ask God to come down in power where people have been forgotten.** Chapter 8, *Oh, That You Would Tear the Heavens* (Isaiah 64:1)
- **Hunger for God’s word, spoken aloud by ordinary believers, is often where a work of God begins.** Chapter 11, *Revive Your Work* (Habakkuk 3:2)
- **Because we come to a generous King, we may bring Him large requests.** Chapter 13, *Ask Large Petitions* (Ephesians 3:20-21)

### Persistence

- **The fire of prayer is kept alive by small, faithful offerings, made day after day.** Chapter 4, *The Fire Shall Never Go Out* (Leviticus 6:13)
- **A plan for prayer, agreed ahead of time, guards us against forgetting to pray.** Chapter 9, *A Concert of Prayer* (Zechariah 8:21)
- **The answer to long prayer may come at a time and place we never expected.** Chapter 10, *Praying in the Snow* (Galatians 4:19)
- **Doing good to those beside us is one of the ways we keep from growing weary.** Chapter 12, *Résister* (Galatians 6:9)

### Faith

- **A small gift placed in God’s hands can be the start of a great work.** Chapter 3, *An Orphanage Built on Prayer* (Psalm 68:5)
- **The harvest comes in God’s time, and some who sow will see it only from heaven.** Chapter 12, *Résister* (Galatians 6:9)
- **Prayer can give the wronged the courage to act, not only the strength to endure.** Chapter 14, *Pulled from Their Knees* (Galatians 3:28)
- **A longing to love God more is a prayer He is glad to answer.** Chapter 15, *All the Families of the Nations* (Psalm 22:27)
- **Great expectation and great effort belong together in the life of prayer.** Chapter 17, *Expect Great Things* (Isaiah 54:3)

### Humility and Mercy

- **A small group stays healthy when it builds others up instead of tearing them down.** Chapter 2, *Little Churches Within the Church* (Malachi 3:16)
- **The truest sign of revival is not loud feeling but changed lives that last.** Chapter 11, *Revive Your Work* (Habakkuk 3:2)
- **Prayer that draws us near to God will in time open our eyes to wrongs we once accepted.** Chapter 13, *Ask Large Petitions* (Ephesians 3:20-21)
- **When God blesses our work, the fitting response is wonder at His grace, not pride in ourselves.** Chapter 17, *Expect Great Things* (Isaiah 54:3)

### Forgiveness

- **God often begins a work of prayer by first teaching His people to love one another.** Chapter 4, *The Fire Shall Never Go Out* (Leviticus 6:13)
- **Knowing that our sins are forgiven turns prayer from an anxious duty into a child’s call to the Father.** Chapter 7, *"God Does Nothing but in Answer to Prayer"* (James 4:2)
- **The largest petitions are for grace: forgiveness, a changed heart and the likeness of Christ.** Chapter 13, *Ask Large Petitions* (Ephesians 3:20-21)
- **Forgiving those who wronged us does not mean pretending the wrong was small.** Chapter 14, *Pulled from Their Knees* (Galatians 3:28)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **God is often found not by straining harder, but by stopping and turning to Him.** Chapter 1, *Be Still and Know* (Psalm 46:10)
- **We ask God to show us His way, and then we go where He sends us.** Chapter 5, *Worthy Is the Lamb* (Revelation 5:9)
- **Giving ourselves wholly to God makes every other prayer honest.** Chapter 7, *"God Does Nothing but in Answer to Prayer"* (James 4:2)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **Those who are at peace with God can sing when the storm breaks.** Chapter 5, *Worthy Is the Lamb* (Revelation 5:9)
- **A believer who struggles with sadness can still be a mighty intercessor.** Chapter 10, *Praying in the Snow* (Galatians 4:19)
- **Holding on in prayer, day after day, is itself a way of resisting evil.** Chapter 12, *Résister* (Galatians 6:9)
- **Those who are let down by God’s people can still trust God and keep serving.** Chapter 15, *All the Families of the Nations* (Psalm 22:27)

### Praying Together

- **When believers who fear God talk together about Him, the Lord listens and remembers.** Chapter 2, *Little Churches Within the Church* (Malachi 3:16)
- **A prayer watch shared by many can keep the fire burning when any one of us grows tired.** Chapter 4, *The Fire Shall Never Go Out* (Leviticus 6:13)
- **When believers agree to pray together for God’s kingdom, they take part in His plan for the world.** Chapter 9, *A Concert of Prayer* (Zechariah 8:21)
- **Revival is usually born in small, faithful prayer meetings long before anyone notices.** Chapter 11, *Revive Your Work* (Habakkuk 3:2)
- **Every believer has the same right to kneel before God, because all are one in Christ Jesus.** Chapter 14, *Pulled from Their Knees* (Galatians 3:28)
- **God’s promise reaches every clan of every nation, so every people may pray to Him in its own tongue.** Chapter 15, *All the Families of the Nations* (Psalm 22:27)
- **Praying together across churches is something every believer can do, whatever else divides us.** Chapter 16, *The Prayer Call of 1784* (Isaiah 54:2)

### Praying for Others

- **Because every believer is a priest, praying for others belongs to the whole church, not only to the pastor.** Chapter 2, *Little Churches Within the Church* (Malachi 3:16)
- **The prayers of the poor are a real gift to God’s work.** Chapter 3, *An Orphanage Built on Prayer* (Psalm 68:5)
- **Prayer for the nations begins with seeing what the Lamb paid for them.** Chapter 5, *Worthy Is the Lamb* (Revelation 5:9)
- **Every person placed in our care is a trust from God, and prayer is the first way we keep that trust.** Chapter 6, *The Apron over Her Head* (Psalm 31:20)
- **Praying for one another keeps love alive, even between friends who disagree.** Chapter 8, *Oh, That You Would Tear the Heavens* (Isaiah 64:1)
- **Longing prayer for others is a kind of birth pain, and God uses it to bring new life.** Chapter 10, *Praying in the Snow* (Galatians 4:19)
- **Those who stay at home hold the ropes for those who go, and prayer is the strongest rope.** Chapter 16, *The Prayer Call of 1784* (Isaiah 54:2)
- **Those who answer God’s call must keep praying for the people it costs.** Chapter 17, *Expect Great Things* (Isaiah 54:3)
