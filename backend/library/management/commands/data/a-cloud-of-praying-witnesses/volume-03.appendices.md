## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **God remembers every sincere prayer.** Chapter 7, *Prayers That Rose Before God* (Acts 10:4)
- **God is never too busy for His children.** Chapter 17, *Never Stop Praying* (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)

### Learning to Pray

- **Scripture gives us words when we are afraid.** Chapter 4, *"Sovereign Master": Praying Under Threat* (Acts 4:31)
- **Not knowing what to pray is not a failure; it is where the Spirit’s help begins.** Chapter 14, *When We Do Not Know What to Pray* (Romans 8:26)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **Prayer was one of the four things the first church would not let go.** Chapter 2, *Devoted to the Prayers* (Acts 2:42)
- **Set times of prayer help us keep praying.** Chapter 3, *The Hour of Prayer* (Acts 3:1)
- **Prayer is real work, and it needs protected time.** Chapter 5, *Holding Fast to Prayer* (Acts 6:4)
- **Good work can crowd out prayer as easily as bad work.** Chapter 5, *Holding Fast to Prayer* (Acts 6:4)
- **Fasting is prayer with the whole person.** Chapter 9, *Worship, Fasting and Sending* (Acts 13:2-3)
- **God loves to meet people at the places where they faithfully pray.** Chapter 10, *The Place of Prayer by the River* (Acts 16:13)
- **Praying without stopping is for ordinary working people.** Chapter 17, *Never Stop Praying* (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)

### Asking Boldly

- **God is able to do far beyond anything we ask or think.** Chapter 13, *Rooted Deep in Love* (Ephesians 3:16-19)
- **The prayers of God’s people move heaven, and heaven moves the earth.** Chapter 20, *Golden Bowls* (Revelation 8:3-4)

### Persistence

- **Waiting on God is not wasted time.** Chapter 1, *Waiting in the Upper Room* (Acts 1:14)
- **A great day of blessing must be followed by daily prayer.** Chapter 2, *Devoted to the Prayers* (Acts 2:42)
- **Prayer for others is often hard, and that is no sign that it is wrong.** Chapter 15, *Always Wrestling in Prayer* (Colossians 4:12)

### Faith

- **We can give others only what we have first received from God.** Chapter 3, *The Hour of Prayer* (Acts 3:1)
- **Begin with who God is before you speak about the danger.** Chapter 4, *"Sovereign Master": Praying Under Threat* (Acts 4:31)
- **God answers even prayers mixed with doubt.** Chapter 8, *Peter in Prison, the Church on Its Knees* (Acts 12:5)
- **Only the Lord can open a heart.** Chapter 10, *The Place of Prayer by the River* (Acts 16:13)
- **The power that raised Jesus from the dead is at work for those who believe.** Chapter 12, *That You May Know Him Better* (Ephesians 1:16-19)
- **The power of prayer comes from God, not from the greatness of the one who prays.** Chapter 19, *The Prayer Made in Faith* (James 5:16)
- **Every prayer leans toward the coming of Jesus.** Chapter 20, *Golden Bowls* (Revelation 8:3-4)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Prayer changes the one who prays.** Chapter 7, *Prayers That Rose Before God* (Acts 10:4)
- **God wants rulers to be saved, even the ones who persecute.** Chapter 18, *For Kings and All in Authority* (1 Timothy 2:1-2)

### Forgiveness

- **Praying for our enemies is the clearest sign of forgiveness.** Chapter 6, *"Do Not Charge Them with This Sin"* (Acts 7:60)

### Thanksgiving

- **Praise can rise from the darkest place.** Chapter 11, *Songs at Midnight* (Acts 16:25)
- **Paul’s prayers begin with thanks for what God has already done.** Chapter 12, *That You May Know Him Better* (Ephesians 1:16-19)
- **Thanksgiving belongs inside every request.** Chapter 16, *Do Not Worry About Anything* (Philippians 4:6-7)
- **Joy, prayer and thanks belong together.** Chapter 17, *Never Stop Praying* (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **The first church placed its first decision in God’s hands.** Chapter 1, *Waiting in the Upper Room* (Acts 1:14)
- **God often answers one person’s prayer through another person’s prayer.** Chapter 7, *Prayers That Rose Before God* (Acts 10:4)
- **God often guides His people while they are worshiping Him.** Chapter 9, *Worship, Fasting and Sending* (Acts 13:2-3)
- **The Spirit always prays in line with what God wants.** Chapter 14, *When We Do Not Know What to Pray* (Romans 8:26)
- **The best prayer for others asks that they will stand firm and grow mature in God’s will.** Chapter 15, *Always Wrestling in Prayer* (Colossians 4:12)
- **God’s peace can guard us before our circumstances change.** Chapter 16, *Do Not Worry About Anything* (Philippians 4:6-7)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **The first church asked for boldness, not for safety.** Chapter 4, *"Sovereign Master": Praying Under Threat* (Acts 4:31)
- **We learn to pray in hard times by watching how Jesus prayed.** Chapter 6, *"Do Not Charge Them with This Sin"* (Acts 7:60)
- **God’s different answers are not a measure of our faith.** Chapter 8, *Peter in Prison, the Church on Its Knees* (Acts 12:5)
- **God’s purpose in our trouble may be bigger than our escape.** Chapter 11, *Songs at Midnight* (Acts 16:25)
- **Paul asks first for strength on the inside, not for an easier life on the outside.** Chapter 13, *Rooted Deep in Love* (Ephesians 3:16-19)
- **Worry is a signal to pray, not a secret to hide.** Chapter 16, *Do Not Worry About Anything* (Philippians 4:6-7)
- **We can pray for a ruler without approving of evil.** Chapter 18, *For Kings and All in Authority* (1 Timothy 2:1-2)
- **When healing does not come, God has not stopped loving us.** Chapter 19, *The Prayer Made in Faith* (James 5:16)

### Praying Together

- **Prayer made one family out of very different people.** Chapter 1, *Waiting in the Upper Room* (Acts 1:14)
- **The first church prayed together, in the temple and at home.** Chapter 2, *Devoted to the Prayers* (Acts 2:42)
- **The church’s first and strongest response to danger is prayer.** Chapter 8, *Peter in Prison, the Church on Its Knees* (Acts 12:5)
- **A small prayer meeting can be the beginning of a great work.** Chapter 10, *The Place of Prayer by the River* (Acts 16:13)
- **It is a gift to have someone to pray with in the dark.** Chapter 11, *Songs at Midnight* (Acts 16:25)
- **We learn the love of Christ together with all God’s holy people.** Chapter 13, *Rooted Deep in Love* (Ephesians 3:16-19)
- **Prayer for the sick is the work of the whole church.** Chapter 19, *The Prayer Made in Faith* (James 5:16)
- **Our prayers are part of the worship of heaven.** Chapter 20, *Golden Bowls* (Revelation 8:3-4)

### Praying for Others

- **Praying people learn to see the people others pass by.** Chapter 3, *The Hour of Prayer* (Acts 3:1)
- **When God’s people share the load, their leaders are free to pray for them.** Chapter 5, *Holding Fast to Prayer* (Acts 6:4)
- **God may answer our prayers long after we are gone.** Chapter 6, *"Do Not Charge Them with This Sin"* (Acts 7:60)
- **Every believer can share in mission by praying and sending.** Chapter 9, *Worship, Fasting and Sending* (Acts 13:2-3)
- **The greatest thing we can ask for anyone is that they would know God better.** Chapter 12, *That You May Know Him Better* (Ephesians 1:16-19)
- **When we cannot be with the people we love, we can still fight for them in prayer.** Chapter 15, *Always Wrestling in Prayer* (Colossians 4:12)
- **Prayer for those in power comes first, not last.** Chapter 18, *For Kings and All in Authority* (1 Timothy 2:1-2)

### Christ Who Prays for Us

- **The Holy Spirit prays for us, and the Son prays for us too.** Chapter 14, *When We Do Not Know What to Pray* (Romans 8:26)
