## Appendix A: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, so you can see at a glance what Jesus teaches about each part of prayer. Each insight points back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **Prayer begins with relationship, not requests.** Chapter 4, *Our Father in Heaven* (Matthew 6:9-10)
- **The secret room is a meeting, not a ritual.** Chapter 6, *The Secret Room* (Matthew 6:6)
- **We do not pray to wear God down; persistence is what trust looks like over time.** Chapter 8, *Always Pray and Never Give Up* (Luke 18:1)
- **The Father Himself loves us; praying in Jesus' name is full of joy.** Chapter 15, *Asking in His Name* (John 16:24)
- **We can live, and die, placing ourselves in the Father's hands.** Chapter 19, *Prayers from the Cross* (Luke 23:34)

### Learning to Pray

- **Prayer can be learned; it is not a gift for a special few.** Chapter 3, *"Lord, Teach Us to Pray"* (Luke 11:1)
- **We learn to pray by watching people who pray.** Chapter 3, *"Lord, Teach Us to Pray"* (Luke 11:1)
- **Jesus is a willing teacher, and still gives His Spirit to all who ask.** Chapter 3, *"Lord, Teach Us to Pray"* (Luke 11:1)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **If Jesus needed to pray, so do we.** Chapter 1, *Very Early in the Morning* (Mark 1:35)
- **Busyness is a reason to pray, not an excuse not to.** Chapter 1, *Very Early in the Morning* (Mark 1:35)
- **God's concerns come first: His name, His kingdom, His will.** Chapter 4, *Our Father in Heaven* (Matthew 6:9-10)
- **Prayer is for an audience of One.** Chapter 6, *The Secret Room* (Matthew 6:6)
- **What happens in secret shapes our public life.** Chapter 6, *The Secret Room* (Matthew 6:6)
- **We need both the secret room and the prayer meeting.** Chapter 11, *Where Two or Three Agree* (Matthew 18:19-20)

### Asking Boldly

- **God cares about our daily needs; we trust Him one day at a time.** Chapter 5, *Bread, Forgiveness, Deliverance* (Matthew 6:11-13)
- **God invites us to ask boldly.** Chapter 7, *Ask, Seek, Knock* (Luke 11:9)
- **We come to God on Jesus' credit, not our own.** Chapter 15, *Asking in His Name* (John 16:24)

### Persistence

- **Ask, search, knock: keep at it.** Chapter 7, *Ask, Seek, Knock* (Luke 11:9)
- **Delay is not denial.** Chapter 8, *Always Pray and Never Give Up* (Luke 18:1)

### Faith

- **Persistent prayer is faith in action.** Chapter 8, *Always Pray and Never Give Up* (Luke 18:1)
- **Faith looks at God, not at the mountain.** Chapter 10, *Faith That Moves Mountains* (Mark 11:24)
- **Jesus prays most of all for our faith, not our escape from testing.** Chapter 16, *"I Have Prayed for You"* (Luke 22:31-32)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Humility is the doorway of prayer.** Chapter 9, *"God, Have Mercy on Me, a Sinner"* (Luke 18:13)
- **Comparing ourselves with others poisons prayer.** Chapter 9, *"God, Have Mercy on Me, a Sinner"* (Luke 18:13)
- **God answers the cry for mercy at once.** Chapter 9, *"God, Have Mercy on Me, a Sinner"* (Luke 18:13)

### Forgiveness

- **Forgiven people forgive.** Chapter 5, *Bread, Forgiveness, Deliverance* (Matthew 6:11-13)
- **Faith and forgiveness go together.** Chapter 10, *Faith That Moves Mountains* (Mark 11:24)
- **Jesus prayed for His enemies, and calls us to do the same.** Chapter 19, *Prayers from the Cross* (Luke 23:34)

### Thanksgiving

- **Thanksgiving is faith speaking before the answer is seen.** Chapter 14, *Thanks Before the Miracle* (John 11:41-42)
- **Remembering past answers builds faith for new ones.** Chapter 14, *Thanks Before the Miracle* (John 11:41-42)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **Prayer gives direction: alone with the Father we are led by God, not driven by the crowd.** Chapter 1, *Very Early in the Morning* (Mark 1:35)
- **Big decisions deserve big prayer.** Chapter 2, *All Night on the Mountain* (Luke 6:12)
- **Faith trusts God's will as well as His power.** Chapter 10, *Faith That Moves Mountains* (Mark 11:24)
- **Praying in His name means praying His will, not using a formula.** Chapter 15, *Asking in His Name* (John 16:24)
- **Surrender is giving over, not giving up.** Chapter 18, *Not My Will but Yours* (Matthew 26:39)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **Praying does not promise an easy road, but it keeps us in the will of God.** Chapter 2, *All Night on the Mountain* (Luke 6:12)
- **We need God's protection every day from testing and the evil one.** Chapter 5, *Bread, Forgiveness, Deliverance* (Matthew 6:11-13)
- **Thanksgiving and tears can go together.** Chapter 14, *Thanks Before the Miracle* (John 11:41-42)
- **We can be honest with God about what we want.** Chapter 18, *Not My Will but Yours* (Matthew 26:39)
- **Prayer prepares us for what we must face.** Chapter 18, *Not My Will but Yours* (Matthew 26:39)
- **An honest cry of "Why?" is still prayer.** Chapter 19, *Prayers from the Cross* (Luke 23:34)

### Praying Together

- **The church was born on a mountain of prayer: its first leaders were chosen after prayer.** Chapter 2, *All Night on the Mountain* (Luke 6:12)
- **We never pray alone: "Our Father" joins us to the whole family of God.** Chapter 4, *Our Father in Heaven* (Matthew 6:9-10)
- **United prayer carries a special promise, and Jesus is present where two or three gather.** Chapter 11, *Where Two or Three Agree* (Matthew 18:19-20)
- **True agreement in prayer begins with reconciliation.** Chapter 11, *Where Two or Three Agree* (Matthew 18:19-20)
- **God's house is first of all a house of prayer.** Chapter 12, *A House of Prayer for All Nations* (Mark 11:17)
- **Jesus clears away what keeps others from God.** Chapter 12, *A House of Prayer for All Nations* (Mark 11:17)
- **Christian unity is a sharing in the life of God.** Chapter 17, *That They May All Be One* (John 17:21)
- **Unity is a witness to the world.** Chapter 17, *That They May All Be One* (John 17:21)

### Praying for Others

- **Much of our asking is for others: we knock at midnight for a hungry friend.** Chapter 7, *Ask, Seek, Knock* (Luke 11:9)
- **God's heart is for all the nations; our prayers should be as wide as His love.** Chapter 12, *A House of Prayer for All Nations* (Mark 11:17)
- **Prayer for the harvest begins with compassion.** Chapter 13, *Ask the Lord of the Harvest* (Matthew 9:37-38)
- **The harvest belongs to the Lord, and He sends workers in answer to prayer.** Chapter 13, *Ask the Lord of the Harvest* (Matthew 9:37-38)
- **Those who pray for workers often become workers.** Chapter 13, *Ask the Lord of the Harvest* (Matthew 9:37-38)

### Christ Who Prays for Us

- **Jesus prays for us by name, even before we fall.** Chapter 16, *"I Have Prayed for You"* (Luke 22:31-32)
- **Restored people strengthen others.** Chapter 16, *"I Have Prayed for You"* (Luke 22:31-32)
- **Jesus prayed for you before you were born.** Chapter 17, *That They May All Be One* (John 17:21)
- **Jesus is still praying for us at the right hand of God.** Chapter 20, *He Always Lives to Pray for Us* (Hebrews 7:25)
- **Our salvation rests on His prayers, not our strength.** Chapter 20, *He Always Lives to Pray for Us* (Hebrews 7:25)
- **Our prayers are joined to His; we never pray alone.** Chapter 20, *He Always Lives to Pray for Us* (Hebrews 7:25)
