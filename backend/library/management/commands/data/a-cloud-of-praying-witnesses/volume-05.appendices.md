## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **Even the best minds must ask God to teach them how to seek Him.** Chapter 6, *Faith Seeking Your Face* (Psalm 27:7-8)
- **Our love for God is always an answer to His love for us.** Chapter 7, *Jesus, the Very Thought of You* (1 John 4:19)
- **God holds all things, even the smallest, because He loves them.** Chapter 13, *"All Shall Be Well"* (Romans 8:28)

### Learning to Pray

- **The prayers of others can give us words when our own words fail.** Chapter 5, *Out of the Depths* (Psalm 130:1-4)
- **Faith and careful thinking are friends, and both can lead us to prayer.** Chapter 6, *Faith Seeking Your Face* (Psalm 27:7-8)
- **We become like the One we look at.** Chapter 9, *Gazing on Christ* (2 Corinthians 3:18)
- **One short word of love from the heart can pierce heaven.** Chapter 11, *A Simple Reaching for God* (Psalm 73:25)
- **Having God’s word is not enough; we need Him to open our eyes to it.** Chapter 15, *Open My Eyes to Your Word* (Psalm 119:18)
- **Prayer is not only speaking to God but also listening for His voice.** Chapter 17, *"Speak, for Your Servant Is Listening"* (1 Samuel 3:10)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **Praise offered seven times a day teaches us that the whole day belongs to God.** Chapter 1, *Ora et Labora: Pray and Work* (Psalm 119:164)
- **Fasting together, as a whole church, teaches us that we need God more than food.** Chapter 2, *Ethiopia Stretches Out Her Hands* (Psalm 68:31)
- **No part of an ordinary day is too small to bring to God in prayer.** Chapter 4, *The Prayers of Ordinary Days* (Psalm 121:8)
- **To meet God we must sometimes shut the door on our cares for a while.** Chapter 6, *Faith Seeking Your Face* (Psalm 27:7-8)
- **A hidden life of prayer is never a wasted life.** Chapter 9, *Gazing on Christ* (2 Corinthians 3:18)
- **Before we study about God, we should speak to God.** Chapter 10, *A Prayer Before Study* (James 1:5)
- **Stillness before God is not empty but full of His presence.** Chapter 12, *Waiting in Stillness* (Lamentations 3:25-26)

### Asking Boldly

- **God gives wisdom gladly to everyone who asks Him in faith.** Chapter 10, *A Prayer Before Study* (James 1:5)
- **We can speak the truth boldly to leaders when we have first prayed for them humbly.** Chapter 14, *Giving God No Rest* (Isaiah 62:6-7)

### Persistence

- **We should keep praying even when prayer feels dry, because God delights in it.** Chapter 13, *"All Shall Be Well"* (Romans 8:28)
- **God invites His people to pray with holy stubbornness, giving Him no rest until He acts.** Chapter 14, *Giving God No Rest* (Isaiah 62:6-7)

### Faith

- **God’s promise reached Africa long before many other lands heard the gospel.** Chapter 2, *Ethiopia Stretches Out Her Hands* (Psalm 68:31)
- **No prayer offered to God is lost, even when the church that prayed it is forgotten.** Chapter 3, *Prayer on the Silk Road* (Malachi 1:11)
- **Love can reach God where our thinking cannot.** Chapter 11, *A Simple Reaching for God* (Psalm 73:25)
- **In prayer we meet the living God Himself.** Chapter 12, *Waiting in Stillness* (Lamentations 3:25-26)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Honest confession is the doorway to God’s mercy, not a wall against it.** Chapter 5, *Out of the Depths* (Psalm 130:1-4)
- **Even the holiest believers need God’s mercy for their mistakes.** Chapter 7, *Jesus, the Very Thought of You* (1 John 4:19)
- **All our learning is straw beside the glory of God Himself.** Chapter 10, *A Prayer Before Study* (James 1:5)
- **Waiting in silence is a confession that only God can save.** Chapter 12, *Waiting in Stillness* (Lamentations 3:25-26)
- **We hear God best when we care more about His word than about who brings it.** Chapter 17, *"Speak, for Your Servant Is Listening"* (1 Samuel 3:10)

### Forgiveness

- **God forgives us so that we will hold Him in awe, not so that we will treat sin as a small thing.** Chapter 5, *Out of the Depths* (Psalm 130:1-4)
- **Praise can become a peacemaker.** Chapter 8, *Praise for Brother Sun* (Psalm 148:3)
- **Blessing our enemies in prayer frees our own hearts from bitterness.** Chapter 16, *Praying for Those Who Burn You* (Matthew 5:44-45)

### Thanksgiving

- **All creation is a choir of praise, and prayer lets us join in.** Chapter 8, *Praise for Brother Sun* (Psalm 148:3)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **Ordinary work, done for God, becomes part of our worship.** Chapter 1, *Ora et Labora: Pray and Work* (Psalm 119:164)
- **God can use a captive to bring His light to a nation.** Chapter 2, *Ethiopia Stretches Out Her Hands* (Psalm 68:31)
- **The Lord who guards our going out and coming in is with us on every road.** Chapter 4, *The Prayers of Ordinary Days* (Psalm 121:8)
- **Having God Himself is enough, even when we cannot understand His ways.** Chapter 11, *A Simple Reaching for God* (Psalm 73:25)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **The church’s true strength is in prayer and love, not in the favor of the powerful.** Chapter 3, *Prayer on the Silk Road* (Malachi 1:11)
- **Troubles that drive us to God can teach us to love Him for Himself.** Chapter 7, *Jesus, the Very Thought of You* (1 John 4:19)
- **We can thank God for His gifts even when we can no longer enjoy them.** Chapter 8, *Praise for Brother Sun* (Psalm 148:3)
- **God does not promise we will never suffer, but He promises we will not be overcome.** Chapter 13, *"All Shall Be Well"* (Romans 8:28)
- **Fire can destroy books and bodies, but it cannot destroy the word of God.** Chapter 15, *Open My Eyes to Your Word* (Psalm 119:18)
- **We can hold firmly to the truth and still pray in love for those who oppose us.** Chapter 16, *Praying for Those Who Burn You* (Matthew 5:44-45)

### Praying Together

- **Praying the Psalms together teaches us to pray with the whole church.** Chapter 1, *Ora et Labora: Pray and Work* (Psalm 119:164)
- **God’s name is great among the nations, and His people pray to Him in every place.** Chapter 3, *Prayer on the Silk Road* (Malachi 1:11)
- **Believers who gather to read and pray together can keep faith alive in the hardest times.** Chapter 15, *Open My Eyes to Your Word* (Psalm 119:18)
- **Kindness to a young believer can shape a whole life of prayer.** Chapter 17, *"Speak, for Your Servant Is Listening"* (1 Samuel 3:10)

### Praying for Others

- **A blessing can be our last gift to those we love.** Chapter 4, *The Prayers of Ordinary Days* (Psalm 121:8)
- **Friends who pray for one another can strengthen each other across great distances.** Chapter 9, *Gazing on Christ* (2 Corinthians 3:18)
- **Real prayer for others always leads us to stand beside them.** Chapter 14, *Giving God No Rest* (Isaiah 62:6-7)
- **Praying for those who hurt us shows that we are children of our Father in heaven.** Chapter 16, *Praying for Those Who Burn You* (Matthew 5:44-45)
