## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **Hearing the Shepherd’s voice begins with belonging to the Shepherd.** Chapter 11, *My Sheep Hear My Voice* (John 10:27)
- **God’s house is meant to be a house of prayer where every people and every outsider is welcome.** Chapter 12, *Fresh Wind, Fresh Fire* (Isaiah 56:7)
- **True prayer holds together awe before a great King and the closeness of a child with a Father.** Chapter 20, *Awe and Intimacy* (Psalm 63:3)

### Learning to Pray

- **A short prayer, repeated slowly, can carry a whole heart to God.** Chapter 4, *Living Together in Unity* (Psalm 133:1)
- **God does not need long or polished prayers, and short, simple prayers from ordinary believers are welcome before Him.** Chapter 8, *What Happens When Women Pray* (Jeremiah 33:3)
- **God’s word cannot be chained, even when His servants are.** Chapter 9, *More Persecution, More Growth* (2 Corinthians 4:8-9)
- **A wise fast fits the body and the season of life that God has given us.** Chapter 10, *The Fast God Chooses* (Isaiah 58:6)
- **What we believe we have heard from God must be tested by His written word and by His people.** Chapter 11, *My Sheep Hear My Voice* (John 10:27)
- **A new season of prayer often begins with the honest confession that we do not pray as we should.** Chapter 15, *The Fire Is Lit Again* (Leviticus 6:13)
- **It is never too late to discover prayer, and hard seasons are often where we find it.** Chapter 20, *Awe and Intimacy* (Psalm 63:3)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **Shared silence before God is a prayer that needs no common language.** Chapter 4, *Living Together in Unity* (Psalm 133:1)
- **Time given to God is never stolen from the needy, because it is where the strength to serve them is found.** Chapter 5, *Prayer Gives a Clean Heart* (Matthew 25:40)
- **Going apart to a quiet place helps us give God our whole attention.** Chapter 6, *I Look Up to the Mountains* (Psalm 121:1-2)
- **Fasting does not earn God’s answer; it humbles us so that we can seek Him with our whole heart.** Chapter 10, *The Fast God Chooses* (Isaiah 58:6)
- **The hours we would give to sleep can become a gift we bring to God.** Chapter 17, *All Night Before God* (Luke 6:12)

### Asking Boldly

- **The simple prayers of children can teach adults to trust.** Chapter 2, *Before They Call, I Will Answer* (Isaiah 65:24)
- **The God who opens blind eyes can also close watching eyes, and we may ask Him to protect His word.** Chapter 3, *Prayer That Opens Closed Doors* (Revelation 3:8)
- **When we are weak and out of answers, calling on God together is not our last resort but our first work.** Chapter 12, *Fresh Wind, Fresh Fire* (Isaiah 56:7)

### Persistence

- **A prayer offered for the nations still counts when a plan for the nations falls short.** Chapter 13, *The 10/40 Window* (Revelation 7:9)
- **When we are pressed to deny Christ, prayer gives us strength to say no again tomorrow.** Chapter 18, *Prayer in a Shipping Container* (Hebrews 13:3)

### Faith

- **God answers prayer for a people in His own time and in His own way, not ours.** Chapter 1, *Ask of Me, and I Will Give You the Nations* (Psalm 2:8)
- **God often begins to answer our prayers before we pray them.** Chapter 2, *Before They Call, I Will Answer* (Isaiah 65:24)
- **The door into God’s presence is always open, even when every other door is shut.** Chapter 3, *Prayer That Opens Closed Doors* (Revelation 3:8)
- **Our help comes from the Lord who made the mountains, not from the mountains themselves.** Chapter 6, *I Look Up to the Mountains* (Psalm 121:1-2)
- **Persecution is never good in itself, but God can turn it to the growth of His church.** Chapter 9, *More Persecution, More Growth* (2 Corinthians 4:8-9)
- **The great crowd before the throne shows us where our prayers for the nations are going.** Chapter 13, *The 10/40 Window* (Revelation 7:9)
- **We do not pray all night to earn God’s answer, but to stay near the One who answers.** Chapter 17, *All Night Before God* (Luke 6:12)
- **Faith trusts that God is able to rescue, and stays faithful even if He does not.** Chapter 19, *Not Bowing to the Image* (Daniel 3:17-18)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Prayer clears the eyes of the heart, so that we can see Christ in the people the world passes by.** Chapter 5, *Prayer Gives a Clean Heart* (Matthew 25:40)
- **Often the first thing God changes when we pray is the one who is praying.** Chapter 8, *What Happens When Women Pray* (Jeremiah 33:3)
- **The fast God chooses always opens our hands to the hungry and the oppressed.** Chapter 10, *The Fast God Chooses* (Isaiah 58:6)
- **We honor God most when we tell what He has done truthfully, without making it larger.** Chapter 14, *Pray for the Peace of the City* (Jeremiah 29:7)
- **The fire of prayer must be kindled from heaven, and its proof is the love it produces.** Chapter 15, *The Fire Is Lit Again* (Leviticus 6:13)
- **Prayer for God’s kingdom to come over a land rightly begins with repentance among His own people.** Chapter 16, *Your Kingdom Come* (Matthew 6:10)

### Forgiveness

- **Bitterness can grow quietly in a wounded heart, so forgiveness must be asked for again and again.** Chapter 7, *Bless Those Who Persecute You* (Romans 12:14)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **Offering our will to God is the heart of prayer, especially in grief.** Chapter 1, *Ask of Me, and I Will Give You the Nations* (Psalm 2:8)
- **Listening to God is meant to lead us into following Him, not only into knowing more.** Chapter 11, *My Sheep Hear My Voice* (John 10:27)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **God’s nearness in suffering is itself an answer to prayer.** Chapter 2, *Before They Call, I Will Answer* (Isaiah 65:24)
- **We can go on praying and loving when God feels far away, and that faithfulness is itself an offering to Him.** Chapter 5, *Prayer Gives a Clean Heart* (Matthew 25:40)
- **God can turn a place of graves and despair into a place of prayer and new life.** Chapter 6, *I Look Up to the Mountains* (Psalm 121:1-2)
- **Blessing those who persecute us does not mean keeping silent about the evil they do.** Chapter 7, *Bless Those Who Persecute You* (Romans 12:14)
- **Pressure on every side does not mean that God has deserted us.** Chapter 9, *More Persecution, More Growth* (2 Corinthians 4:8-9)
- **Praise sung in a dark place can be the prayer that keeps a soul alive.** Chapter 18, *Prayer in a Shipping Container* (Hebrews 13:3)
- **Years stolen by a prison cell can become years given to prayer and to God’s word.** Chapter 19, *Not Bowing to the Image* (Daniel 3:17-18)
- **When life itself is slipping away, God’s loyal love is still worth more than life.** Chapter 20, *Awe and Intimacy* (Psalm 63:3)

### Praying Together

- **Unity among Christians comes down from God, like dew, and we receive it first in prayer.** Chapter 4, *Living Together in Unity* (Psalm 133:1)
- **When believers pray together one subject at a time, they learn to be of one mind with each other and with God.** Chapter 8, *What Happens When Women Pray* (Jeremiah 33:3)
- **The prayer meeting shows the true health of a church more clearly than the Sunday crowd.** Chapter 12, *Fresh Wind, Fresh Fire* (Isaiah 56:7)
- **A city is blessed when its churches stop competing and begin to pray side by side.** Chapter 14, *Pray for the Peace of the City* (Jeremiah 29:7)
- **Stories of past prayer can become kindling for a new generation’s fire.** Chapter 15, *The Fire Is Lit Again* (Leviticus 6:13)
- **The days between Ascension and Pentecost invite the whole church to wait and pray together for the Spirit.** Chapter 16, *Your Kingdom Come* (Matthew 6:10)
- **A long night of prayer is easier to keep together than alone.** Chapter 17, *All Night Before God* (Luke 6:12)

### Praying for Others

- **When we pray for the nations, God may ask us to be part of His answer.** Chapter 1, *Ask of Me, and I Will Give You the Nations* (Psalm 2:8)
- **Small and weak churches are precious to Christ, and He sends others to strengthen them.** Chapter 3, *Prayer That Opens Closed Doors* (Revelation 3:8)
- **To bless a persecutor is to ask God for their greatest good, and the greatest good of all is repentance.** Chapter 7, *Bless Those Who Persecute You* (Romans 12:14)
- **Prayer for the world becomes faithful when it learns the names of the peoples in it.** Chapter 13, *The 10/40 Window* (Revelation 7:9)
- **To pray for a city is to want good for all its people, not only growth for our own church.** Chapter 14, *Pray for the Peace of the City* (Jeremiah 29:7)
- **God’s kingdom comes into the world one heart at a time, so we may pray for it by name.** Chapter 16, *Your Kingdom Come* (Matthew 6:10)
- **Remembering prisoners means praying as if their chains were on our own wrists.** Chapter 18, *Prayer in a Shipping Container* (Hebrews 13:3)
- **Standing with a suffering brother, in prayer and in public, is love that may cost us dearly.** Chapter 19, *Not Bowing to the Image* (Daniel 3:17-18)
