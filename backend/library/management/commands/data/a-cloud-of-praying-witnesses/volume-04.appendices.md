## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **We call God "Father" only because His Spirit gives us the right.** Chapter 8, *Why We Say "Our" Father* (Galatians 4:6)
- **God leads us into quiet places to speak tenderly to us, not to punish us.** Chapter 9, *Into the Desert to Pray* (Hosea 2:14)
- **We pray not to inform God but to become His close friends.** Chapter 14, *Prayer, the Light of the Soul* (Psalm 18:28)
- **We were made for God, and our hearts find rest only in Him.** Chapter 16, *"Our Heart Is Restless"* (Psalm 62:1)

### Learning to Pray

- **The Lord’s Prayer is a pattern to live inside, not a formula to rush through.** Chapter 1, *Three Times a Day* (Psalm 55:17)
- **The heart must be lifted before the hands.** Chapter 7, *Prayer Rising Like Incense* (Psalm 141:2)
- **Prayer is learned from those who walked the road before us.** Chapter 11, *"God, Come and Rescue Me"* (Psalm 70:1)
- **What we sing, we remember, and what we remember shapes what we believe.** Chapter 13, *Prayer in Song* (Colossians 3:16)
- **We do not need a loud voice or a quiet room for God to hear us.** Chapter 14, *Prayer, the Light of the Soul* (Psalm 18:28)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **A simple rhythm of prayer can carry a whole life.** Chapter 1, *Three Times a Day* (Psalm 55:17)
- **A soldier keeps watch at set times, and so should we.** Chapter 6, *Prayer, the Wall of Faith* (Ephesians 6:18)
- **Our whole life can become one long prayer.** Chapter 7, *Prayer Rising Like Incense* (Psalm 141:2)
- **Time alone with God makes us more useful to others, not less.** Chapter 9, *Into the Desert to Pray* (Hosea 2:14)
- **One short verse can carry us through the whole day.** Chapter 11, *"God, Come and Rescue Me"* (Psalm 70:1)
- **Any home can become a house of prayer.** Chapter 12, *A Home Made a House of Prayer* (Psalm 31:5)
- **Calling on the name of Jesus is a prayer for every place and every hour.** Chapter 18, *Lord Jesus Christ, Have Mercy* (Luke 18:38)

### Asking Boldly

- **Even a new believer may come to God with bold confidence.** Chapter 5, *Prayer in a Prison Cell* (Psalm 23:4)

### Persistence

- **A lifetime of prayer prepares us for the hour of testing.** Chapter 4, *"Eighty and Six Years Have I Served Him"* (Revelation 2:10)
- **If prayer feels like a struggle, you are not failing; you are fighting.** Chapter 10, *A Struggle to the Last Breath* (Ephesians 6:12)
- **God stretches our hearts by making us wait.** Chapter 16, *"Our Heart Is Restless"* (Psalm 62:1)
- **Repeating a prayer from the heart is not vain repetition.** Chapter 18, *Lord Jesus Christ, Have Mercy* (Luke 18:38)

### Faith

- **Praise of God’s greatness frees us to pray for the powerful without fear.** Chapter 2, *A Prayer for Rulers Who Persecute* (1 Timothy 2:1-2)
- **Christ’s promise to be with us is the strength of our mission.** Chapter 17, *Christ Be with Me* (Matthew 28:20)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Coming back to God again and again slowly softens a hard heart.** Chapter 10, *A Struggle to the Last Breath* (Ephesians 6:12)
- **We need God’s help in our joys as much as in our troubles.** Chapter 11, *"God, Come and Rescue Me"* (Psalm 70:1)
- **The shortest prayer can hold the whole gospel.** Chapter 18, *Lord Jesus Christ, Have Mercy* (Luke 18:38)

### Thanksgiving

- **Thanksgiving can be our last word, even in suffering.** Chapter 4, *"Eighty and Six Years Have I Served Him"* (Revelation 2:10)
- **Thanksgiving is a medicine for grief.** Chapter 12, *A Home Made a House of Prayer* (Psalm 31:5)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **One word from Scripture, heard with an open heart, can change a whole life.** Chapter 9, *Into the Desert to Pray* (Hosea 2:14)
- **Psalms learned by heart stay with us when our strength is gone.** Chapter 12, *A Home Made a House of Prayer* (Psalm 31:5)
- **God may refuse our request in order to grant our deepest desire.** Chapter 15, *The Child of Those Tears* (Lamentations 2:19)
- **The best prayer learns to want what God wants.** Chapter 16, *"Our Heart Is Restless"* (Psalm 62:1)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **Honest prayer names the suffering before it asks for anything else.** Chapter 2, *A Prayer for Rulers Who Persecute* (1 Timothy 2:1-2)
- **In suffering, the deepest prayer is for faithfulness, not only for escape.** Chapter 3, *Prayer on the Road to the Lions* (Philippians 1:21)
- **God meets us in the darkness before He brings us out of it.** Chapter 5, *Prayer in a Prison Cell* (Psalm 23:4)
- **Prayer is not an extra piece of armor but the strength of all the rest.** Chapter 6, *Prayer, the Wall of Faith* (Ephesians 6:18)
- **God’s grace can be enough for a struggle He does not take away.** Chapter 10, *A Struggle to the Last Breath* (Ephesians 6:12)
- **Prayer brings God’s light into our darkest places.** Chapter 14, *Prayer, the Light of the Soul* (Psalm 18:28)
- **Tears poured out before God are a kind of prayer.** Chapter 15, *The Child of Those Tears* (Lamentations 2:19)
- **The place of our deepest suffering can become the place where we learn to pray.** Chapter 17, *Christ Be with Me* (Matthew 28:20)

### Praying Together

- **Set times of prayer join us to God’s people across the ages.** Chapter 1, *Three Times a Day* (Psalm 55:17)
- **Asking others to pray for us is strength, not weakness.** Chapter 3, *Prayer on the Road to the Lions* (Philippians 1:21)
- **Praying together keeps anyone from being left alone.** Chapter 5, *Prayer in a Prison Cell* (Psalm 23:4)
- **Quarrels among believers weaken prayer, and unity strengthens it.** Chapter 8, *Why We Say "Our" Father* (Galatians 4:6)
- **Singing together is praying together.** Chapter 13, *Prayer in Song* (Colossians 3:16)

### Praying for Others

- **We can ask God to guide the hearts of rulers who are against us.** Chapter 2, *A Prayer for Rulers Who Persecute* (1 Timothy 2:1-2)
- **When we can no longer care for those we love, we can still pray for them.** Chapter 3, *Prayer on the Road to the Lions* (Philippians 1:21)
- **A praying heart remembers everyone, the small and the great.** Chapter 4, *"Eighty and Six Years Have I Served Him"* (Revelation 2:10)
- **Christians answer hatred by praying for those who hate them.** Chapter 6, *Prayer, the Wall of Faith* (Ephesians 6:18)
- **The word "our" makes every prayer a prayer for the whole family.** Chapter 8, *Why We Say "Our" Father* (Galatians 4:6)
- **True praise leads to love for the poor.** Chapter 13, *Prayer in Song* (Colossians 3:16)
- **Prayer for a wandering child is never wasted.** Chapter 15, *The Child of Those Tears* (Lamentations 2:19)

### Christ Who Prays for Us

- **When we pray, Christ prays with us.** Chapter 7, *Prayer Rising Like Incense* (Psalm 141:2)
- **When we do not know how to pray, the Spirit prays within us.** Chapter 17, *Christ Be with Me* (Matthew 28:20)
