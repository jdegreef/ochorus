## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **Thirst for God is not a sign of failure but an invitation to come and drink.** Chapter 4, *My Soul Thirsts for You* (Psalm 63:1)
- **Anyone may come to God as a child comes to a father, without priest, journey or sacrifice.** Chapter 16, *A Bishop on His Knees* (Psalm 72:8)
- **The way into God’s kingdom is not climbing up but being carried, as a child is carried by her father.** Chapter 19, *The Little Way* (Matthew 18:3)

### Learning to Pray

- **Bold asking grows best from deep reading of God’s Word.** Chapter 5, *Open Your Mouth Wide* (Psalm 81:10)
- **Unceasing prayer is learned slowly, with a patient teacher beside us.** Chapter 6, *A Pilgrim Learns to Pray Without Ceasing* (1 Thessalonians 5:17)
- **Prayer is a way to receive the Holy Spirit, not a way to earn God’s favor.** Chapter 7, *A Peaceful Spirit* (Isaiah 26:3)
- **God’s words living in us teach us what to ask, so we should listen before we speak.** Chapter 17, *Abide in Me* (John 15:7)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **The best hours of the day belong to God, not only the hours left over.** Chapter 4, *My Soul Thirsts for You* (Psalm 63:1)
- **A short prayer, prayed again and again, can move from the lips into the heart.** Chapter 6, *A Pilgrim Learns to Pray Without Ceasing* (1 Thessalonians 5:17)
- **Even a crowded working day has room for God when someone opens a door at noon.** Chapter 8, *The Noon Prayer Meeting* (2 Chronicles 7:14)
- **Prayer grows strong through daily practice, as a soldier grows strong through daily drill.** Chapter 14, *Knee Drill* (Isaiah 61:1)
- **The hours before dawn, given to God, can shape a whole life and outlast it.** Chapter 20, *God Looked for an Intercessor* (Isaiah 59:16)

### Asking Boldly

- **We may bring God the plain needs of life and death as members of His family.** Chapter 3, *A Covenant with God* (Jeremiah 50:5)
- **The God who has already rescued us invites us to open our mouths wide.** Chapter 5, *Open Your Mouth Wide* (Psalm 81:10)
- **We may ask God for definite things, because He meets every need from His own great wealth.** Chapter 11, *Move Man Through God* (Philippians 4:19)
- **A wide prayer for the nations can be prayed by a humble heart.** Chapter 16, *A Bishop on His Knees* (Psalm 72:8)
- **The prayers God loves to answer grow out of staying close to Jesus, as fruit grows on a branch.** Chapter 17, *Abide in Me* (John 15:7)

### Persistence

- **A cold heart is a reason to pray, not a reason to stay away from prayer.** Chapter 3, *A Covenant with God* (Jeremiah 50:5)
- **One person willing to wait alone in prayer can open a door for many others.** Chapter 8, *The Noon Prayer Meeting* (2 Chronicles 7:14)

### Faith

- **We do not have to wait for perfect conditions before we pray about God’s work.** Chapter 1, *A Prayer Meeting in the Rain* (Matthew 28:19)
- **God guards with unbroken peace the mind that keeps leaning on Him.** Chapter 7, *A Peaceful Spirit* (Isaiah 26:3)
- **Stories of what God has done can stir us to ask Him to do it again.** Chapter 9, *Four Young Men at Kells* (Psalm 85:6)
- **Trusting God in small things trains us to trust Him in large ones.** Chapter 11, *Move Man Through God* (Philippians 4:19)
- **Specific, believing prayer honors God’s promise that all things are possible for the one who believes.** Chapter 13, *The Bedridden Woman Who Prayed* (Mark 9:23)
- **A life of plain, daily prayer becomes a long record of God’s faithfulness.** Chapter 18, *He Will Not Leave You* (Deuteronomy 31:8)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Strong prayer still needs humility and love when it turns to speak to others.** Chapter 2, *The Man Who Prayed Ahead* (Isaiah 66:8)
- **A heart that prays without stopping grows gentle toward everyone.** Chapter 6, *A Pilgrim Learns to Pray Without Ceasing* (1 Thessalonians 5:17)
- **Humbling ourselves includes turning from the wrongs our whole land has accepted.** Chapter 8, *The Noon Prayer Meeting* (2 Chronicles 7:14)
- **A true leader in God’s church serves first on his knees.** Chapter 12, *Overcomer* (1 John 4:4)
- **We cannot ask others to trust God in the dark if we will not do it ourselves.** Chapter 15, *Trust Without Fear* (Proverbs 3:5-6)
- **A servant who knows he is only God’s instrument can bear being set aside without bitterness.** Chapter 16, *A Bishop on His Knees* (Psalm 72:8)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **The Great Commission is a promise to pray over, as well as a command to obey.** Chapter 1, *A Prayer Meeting in the Rain* (Matthew 28:19)
- **Putting our surrender to God into words can steady us for years to come.** Chapter 3, *A Covenant with God* (Jeremiah 50:5)
- **Seeking God’s will is the beginning of prayer, but not the end of it.** Chapter 5, *Open Your Mouth Wide* (Psalm 81:10)
- **When we give God the weight of a work, we are free to ask Him boldly for what it needs.** Chapter 11, *Move Man Through God* (Philippians 4:19)
- **Trusting God sometimes means admitting the fear we have hidden from ourselves.** Chapter 15, *Trust Without Fear* (Proverbs 3:5-6)
- **When God answers prayer in a way we did not expect, we must be careful not to stand in His way.** Chapter 17, *Abide in Me* (John 15:7)
- **God goes ahead of us into the hard places He sends us, so we never arrive anywhere alone.** Chapter 18, *He Will Not Leave You* (Deuteronomy 31:8)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **The day of trouble is the very day God invites us to call on Him.** Chapter 10, *The Boiler Room* (Psalm 50:15)
- **In our hardest battles we stand in the strength of the One who lives in us, not in our own.** Chapter 12, *Overcomer* (1 John 4:4)
- **God often prepares a great work through people the world never notices.** Chapter 13, *The Bedridden Woman Who Prayed* (Mark 9:23)
- **Prayer is the first answer to fear, whether the danger is a stormy sea, a cruel crowd or a cold glance.** Chapter 15, *Trust Without Fear* (Proverbs 3:5-6)
- **When fear takes away our words, one cry of "Father!" is still a whole prayer.** Chapter 18, *He Will Not Leave You* (Deuteronomy 31:8)
- **We can keep praying in love even when we cannot feel God at all.** Chapter 19, *The Little Way* (Matthew 18:3)

### Praying Together

- **Preaching and praying belong together, and God gives the growth through both.** Chapter 2, *The Man Who Prayed Ahead* (Isaiah 66:8)
- **A shared plan for reading Scripture helps a church agree in what it asks of God.** Chapter 4, *My Soul Thirsts for You* (Psalm 63:1)
- **A prayer meeting does not need a minister, a building or a crowd to be heard in heaven.** Chapter 9, *Four Young Men at Kells* (Psalm 85:6)
- **God listens to the plain prayers of plain believers as gladly as to the prayers of the gifted.** Chapter 10, *The Boiler Room* (Psalm 50:15)
- **Real prayer can reach hearts that sermons do not.** Chapter 14, *Knee Drill* (Isaiah 61:1)

### Praying for Others

- **Prayer turns what we learn about the world into a burden for its people.** Chapter 1, *A Prayer Meeting in the Rain* (Matthew 28:19)
- **Writing down names and praying for them one by one can open the way for people to come to God.** Chapter 2, *The Man Who Prayed Ahead* (Isaiah 66:8)
- **Peace received in prayer flows out to the people around us.** Chapter 7, *A Peaceful Spirit* (Isaiah 26:3)
- **Praying for our own family, and then speaking to them, can carry God’s work from one home to a whole town.** Chapter 9, *Four Young Men at Kells* (Psalm 85:6)
- **The strength of a pulpit is often hidden in the people who pray for it.** Chapter 10, *The Boiler Room* (Psalm 50:15)
- **Those whom God has set free can become His instruments to set others free.** Chapter 12, *Overcomer* (1 John 4:4)
- **A believer who can do nothing else can still pray, and that prayer can reach farther than any journey.** Chapter 13, *The Bedridden Woman Who Prayed* (Mark 9:23)
- **The gospel sets captives free, and prayer comes before every rescue.** Chapter 14, *Knee Drill* (Isaiah 61:1)
- **A child’s prayer can be bold, because it trusts the Father’s mercy more than its own goodness.** Chapter 19, *The Little Way* (Matthew 18:3)
- **God is still looking for people who will stand between Him and a needy world in prayer.** Chapter 20, *God Looked for an Intercessor* (Isaiah 59:16)

### Christ Who Prays for Us

- **We can stand in the gap for others because Jesus has already stood there for us.** Chapter 20, *God Looked for an Intercessor* (Isaiah 59:16)
