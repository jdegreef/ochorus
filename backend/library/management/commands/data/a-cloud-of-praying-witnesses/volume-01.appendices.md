## Appendix 1: Key Insights

Every chapter of this book closes with three key insights. Here they are gathered by theme, each pointing back to the chapter where it is taught and to that chapter's key verse.

### The Father's Love

- **God’s glory is His goodness, and we see it most clearly in Jesus.** Chapter 4, *"Let Me See Your Glory"* (Exodus 33:18)
- **The best thing we can ask of God is God Himself.** Chapter 7, *One Thing I Ask* (Psalm 27:4)
- **No one has fallen too far to come home to God.** Chapter 8, *A Pure Heart* (Psalm 51:10)

### Learning to Pray

- **The best prayer asks for God Himself, not only for His gifts.** Chapter 4, *"Let Me See Your Glory"* (Exodus 33:18)
- **A single desire gives our prayers focus and strength.** Chapter 7, *One Thing I Ask* (Psalm 27:4)
- **Scripture stored in the heart becomes prayer in the dark.** Chapter 13, *A Prayer from the Depths* (Jonah 2:2)
- **A short prayer in the moment is real prayer, and God hears it.** Chapter 15, *Arrow Prayers and Long Fasts* (Nehemiah 2:4-5)

### Making Room for Prayer

- **The more we know God, the more we long to know Him.** Chapter 4, *"Let Me See Your Glory"* (Exodus 33:18)
- **The habit of prayer we build in quiet days carries us through dangerous ones.** Chapter 14, *Windows Open Toward Jerusalem* (Daniel 6:10)

### Asking Boldly

- **We can pray boldly because we pray to a God who is just and good.** Chapter 1, *The Friend Who Pleaded for a City* (Genesis 18:25)
- **The strongest prayers appeal to God’s own character, name and promises.** Chapter 3, *Standing in the Gap* (Exodus 32:11)
- **The best prayers ask God to make Himself known.** Chapter 10, *Fire from Heaven, Rain on the Earth* (1 Kings 18:37)
- **Pray for the answer that will make God known.** Chapter 12, *Opening the Letter Before the Lord* (2 Kings 19:14-15)

### Persistence

- **Humble persistence is welcome in God’s presence.** Chapter 1, *The Friend Who Pleaded for a City* (Genesis 18:25)
- **Sometimes we prevail in prayer through weakness, not strength.** Chapter 2, *"I Will Not Let You Go"* (Genesis 32:26)
- **Keep praying and keep looking until the small cloud appears.** Chapter 10, *Fire from Heaven, Rain on the Earth* (1 Kings 18:37)
- **Long, hidden prayer prepares us for the sudden moment.** Chapter 15, *Arrow Prayers and Long Fasts* (Nehemiah 2:4-5)
- **When God’s answer is hard to understand, we can still wait at the watchpost.** Chapter 17, *Though the Fig Tree Does Not Flower* (Habakkuk 3:17-18)

### Faith

- **We may hold God to His own promises.** Chapter 2, *"I Will Not Let You Go"* (Genesis 32:26)
- **Prayer can give us peace before it changes our circumstances.** Chapter 5, *Pouring Out the Soul* (1 Samuel 1:15)
- **God’s promises are not a reason to stop praying, but the ground we pray on.** Chapter 10, *Fire from Heaven, Rain on the Earth* (1 Kings 18:37)
- **Big prayers begin with a big view of God.** Chapter 12, *Opening the Letter Before the Lord* (2 Kings 19:14-15)
- **We can pray toward a promise even when we are looking at ruins.** Chapter 14, *Windows Open Toward Jerusalem* (Daniel 6:10)
- **Prayer and work belong together.** Chapter 15, *Arrow Prayers and Long Fasts* (Nehemiah 2:4-5)
- **Lament is not unbelief; it is faith that refuses to walk away.** Chapter 16, *"How Long, LORD?" Honest Prayer* (Psalm 13:1)
- **The righteous live by faithfulness, trusting God’s promise before it comes.** Chapter 17, *Though the Fig Tree Does Not Flower* (Habakkuk 3:17-18)

### Humility and Mercy

- **Real confession calls sin by its true name.** Chapter 8, *A Pure Heart* (Psalm 51:10)
- **God’s people are called to humble themselves before they point at others.** Chapter 9, *If My People Will Pray* (2 Chronicles 7:14)
- **Saying "we do not know what to do" is the beginning of real prayer, not the end of it.** Chapter 11, *"Our Eyes Are Turned Toward You"* (2 Chronicles 20:12)
- **God hears the prayers of those who have run from Him.** Chapter 13, *A Prayer from the Depths* (Jonah 2:2)
- **Honest confession comes before bold requests.** Chapter 14, *Windows Open Toward Jerusalem* (Daniel 6:10)

### Forgiveness

- **We can keep praying for people who have hurt or rejected us.** Chapter 6, *The Sin of Not Praying* (1 Samuel 12:23)
- **Only God can make a heart new.** Chapter 8, *A Pure Heart* (Psalm 51:10)
- **God always hears those who turn back to Him.** Chapter 9, *If My People Will Pray* (2 Chronicles 7:14)

### Thanksgiving

- **Praise can go out ahead of the answer.** Chapter 11, *"Our Eyes Are Turned Toward You"* (2 Chronicles 20:12)

### Guidance and Surrender

- **God often answers in a way that serves His bigger purposes.** Chapter 5, *Pouring Out the Soul* (1 Samuel 1:15)

### Prayer in Suffering and Testing

- **God meets us in our fear, not after we have fixed ourselves.** Chapter 2, *"I Will Not Let You Go"* (Genesis 32:26)
- **We can bring our deepest pain to God exactly as it is.** Chapter 5, *Pouring Out the Soul* (1 Samuel 1:15)
- **God’s presence is our shelter in the day of trouble.** Chapter 7, *One Thing I Ask* (Psalm 27:4)
- **Take the thing that frightens you and lay it before God.** Chapter 12, *Opening the Letter Before the Lord* (2 Kings 19:14-15)
- **No place is too deep for prayer to reach God.** Chapter 13, *A Prayer from the Depths* (Jonah 2:2)
- **God invites us to bring Him our real feelings, even our complaints.** Chapter 16, *"How Long, LORD?" Honest Prayer* (Psalm 13:1)
- **We can rejoice in God Himself even when everything else is taken away.** Chapter 17, *Though the Fig Tree Does Not Flower* (Habakkuk 3:17-18)

### Praying Together

- **When God’s people seek Him together, even the children have a place.** Chapter 11, *"Our Eyes Are Turned Toward You"* (2 Chronicles 20:12)

### Praying for Others

- **Friends of God pray for others, not only for themselves.** Chapter 1, *The Friend Who Pleaded for a City* (Genesis 18:25)
- **An intercessor stands between God and people who cannot stand for themselves.** Chapter 3, *Standing in the Gap* (Exodus 32:11)
- **Praying for others is a duty of love, not an extra.** Chapter 6, *The Sin of Not Praying* (1 Samuel 12:23)
- **We pray for our land without demanding a guarantee.** Chapter 9, *If My People Will Pray* (2 Chronicles 7:14)

### Christ Who Prays for Us

- **True intercession costs the one who prays.** Chapter 3, *Standing in the Gap* (Exodus 32:11)
- **Jesus never stops praying for us, so we need never stop praying for others.** Chapter 6, *The Sin of Not Praying* (1 Samuel 12:23)
- **Jesus Himself prayed the laments, so we never pray them alone.** Chapter 16, *"How Long, LORD?" Honest Prayer* (Psalm 13:1)
