# A Hidden Fire — claims a Ugandan reader should check before approval

Simeon Nsibambi died in 1978 and his family is living and prominent; his son
Apolo Nsibambi was Prime Minister of Uganda from 2000 to 2011. That is the
reason this list exists, and the reason the book is deliberately short: the
documentary record is thin, and padding it would have meant inventing him.

The book's design rule is that each chapter states something documented and
then turns to the reader. Where the record is silent it says so rather than
filling the gap. This file records where those lines were drawn.

## NOT asserted in the book, because I could not verify them

These appear in the existing author biography on his Ochorus page and in some
popular accounts. **The book deliberately does not build on any of them**, and
chapter 1 says as much in the text. If a Ugandan reader can confirm or correct
them, both the book and the bio should be revisited.

- That he was educated at Mengo High School and King's College, Budo.
- That he served in the African Native Medical Corps in the First World War,
  and was decorated for distinguished service.
- That his father was a chief.
- That he personally buried Blasio Kigozi on Cathedral Hill. (Kigozi is
  reported to have contracted tick fever while travelling to the synod in
  Kampala; who buried him, I could not establish.)
- The exact wording of his question to Joe Church. The book paraphrases it and
  states openly that the wording varies between tellings.

## Judgement calls a reviewer may want to overturn

- **Chapter 5 quotes the "he's gone mad" complaint in paraphrase, not as a
  verbatim quotation.** The line is widely repeated in histories of the revival
  but the exact wording and the identity of the missionary vary. It is
  presented as remembered speech, not as a citation.
- **The illness is not diagnosed.** Church's word "weakness" is all the book
  claims. It resists the temptation to name a condition.
- **Nothing is put in his mouth or his head.** There is no reconstructed
  dialogue, no interior monologue, and no invented scene anywhere in the seven
  chapters. Chapters 1, 4 and 6 each say explicitly where the record stops.
- **The 37-year figure in chapter 6** is arithmetic from 1941 to 1978, not a
  figure any source states. If either date moves, that number moves with it.

## Relationship to the existing author biography

`simeon-nsibambi` already carries a ~1,650-word biography that narrates the
same life. This book is deliberately NOT a longer version of it: the biography
tells the story, and the book states each anchor briefly and spends its words
on the reader. Chapter 7 was retitled during drafting so that it did not
duplicate a heading in the bio verbatim.

Where the two disagree, the bio is the one carrying unverified claims (see the
second table above), and it is the one that should be corrected.
