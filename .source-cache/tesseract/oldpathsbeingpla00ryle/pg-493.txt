Listen not for a moment to those who tell you that in
this life we can never be sure of our own spiritual state,
and must always be in doubt. The Roman Catholics say
so. The ignorant world says so. The devil says so. But
the Bible says nothing of the kind. There is sucha thing
as strong assurance of our acceptance in Christ, and a
Christian should never rest till he has obtained it. That
aman may be saved without this strong assurance I do
not deny. But that without it he misses a great privilege,
and much comfort, I am quite sure.
