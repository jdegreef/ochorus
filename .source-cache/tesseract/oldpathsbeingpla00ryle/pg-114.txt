I lay down this principle as a starting point, and I ask
my readers to consider it well. I admit most fully that
there are different degrees of grace among true Christians.
T do not forget that there are many in the family of God
whose faith is very weak, and whose hope is very small.
But I believe confidently, that the standard of require-
ment I have set up is not a whit too high. I believe
that the man who has a “good hope” will always be
able to give some account of it.
