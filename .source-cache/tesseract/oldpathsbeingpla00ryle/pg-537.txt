(4) Is it not a historical fact, that in the year 1604, in James the First’s
reign, this doctrine of perseverance was considered at the Hampton Court
Conference. The Puritan party wished the Lambeth Articles to be added
to the Thirty-nine Articles. Their request was not granted : but on what
grounds? Not because the doctrine of perseverance was objected to, but
because King James thought it better ‘not to stuff the book of Articles with
all conclusions theological.” While even Overall, Dean of St. Paul’s,
whose soundness on this point was most suspected, used these remark-
able words : ‘‘ Those who are justitied according to the purpose of God’s
election, though they might fall into grievous sin, and thereby into the
present estate of damnation, yet never totally nor finally from justifica-
tion, but are in time renewed by God’s Spirit unto lively faith and
repentance.” — Fuller's Church History, third volume, page 181.
