(b) Forgiven souls love Christ. This is that one thing
they can say, if they dare say nothing else,—they do love
Christ. His person, His offices, His work, His name, His
cross, His blood, His words, His example, His day, His or-
dinanees,—all, all are precious to forgiven souls. The minis-
try which exalts Him most, is that which they enjoy most.
The books which are most full of Him, are most pleasant to
their minds. The people on earth they feel most drawn
to, are those in whom they see something of Christ. His
name is as ointment poured forth, and eomes with a
pecuhlar sweetness to their ears. (Cant. i. 3.) They
would tell you they cannot help feeling as they do. He
is their Redeemer, their Shepherd, their Physician, their
