not a believer !

(4) He that believes on Christ works godly works. It
is written, that “faith worketh by love.” (Gal. v. 6.) Trne
belief will never make a man idle, or allow him to sit still,
contented with his own relicion. It will stir him to do
acts of love, kindness, and charity, according as he sees
opportunity. It will constrain him to walk in the steps of
his Master, who “went about doing good.” (Acts x. 38.)
In one way or another, it will make him work. The works
that he does may attract no notice from the world. They
may seem trifling and insignificant to many persons. But
they are not forgotten by Him who notices a cup of cold
water given for His sake. Where there is no working
love, there is no faith, A lazy, selfish Christian, has no
right to regard himself as a believer!
