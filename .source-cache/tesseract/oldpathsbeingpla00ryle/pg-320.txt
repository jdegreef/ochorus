evening? I cannot. It comes down softly and gently,
noiseless and imperceptible. But when I go forth in the
morning after a cloudless night, and see every leaf spark-
ling with moisture, and feel every blade of grass damp and
wet, I say at once, “There has been a dew.” Just so it is
with the presence of the Spirit in the soul.
