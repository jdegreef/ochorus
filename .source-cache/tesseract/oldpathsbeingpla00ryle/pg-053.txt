Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter. God
has given us the Bible to be a light to guide us to
everlasting life. Let us not neglect this precious gift.
Let us read it diligently, walk in its light, and we shall
be saved.

The following quotations about inspiration, from the works of four
eminent British theologians, I venture to think deserve attentive
perusal. They cre valuable in themselves on account of the
arguments which they contain. They also supply abundant
proof that the high view of verbal inspiration which I advocate
in this paper is no modern invention, but an “old path,” mm
which many of God's ablest children have walked, and found it
a good way.
