desperate pride in man: he will not bow his heart to
receive the Gospel as a little child. I see desperate sloth
in man: he will not take the trouble to arise and call
upon God. I see desperate worldliness in man: he will
not loose his hold on the poor perishable things of time,
and consider eternity. In short, I see the words of our
Lord continually verified: “Ye will not come unto Me,
that ye might have life” (John v. 40), and therefore I am
driven to the sorrowful conclusion that few are likely to
be saved.
