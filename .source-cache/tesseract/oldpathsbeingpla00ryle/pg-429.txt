kingdom of heaven.” “Except ye repent, ye shall all
likewise perish.” (Matt. xviii. 3; Luke xiii. 3.)

But whence comes the necessity of repentance? Why
is such tremendously strong language used about this
necessity? What are the reasons, what the causcs, why
repentance is so needful ?

the work of the blood of Christ alone.—Contrition makes
