I invite you to look calmly at this sum. I defy you
to disprove its correctness. I ask you, on the contrary,
whether I have not entirely understated your case? I
appeal to you, as an honest person, whether it be not true,
that many an hour, and many a day in your life, you have
sinned incessantly? J ask you confidently, whether the
sum would not be far more correct if the total number of
your sins was multiplied ten-fold ?—Oh, cease from your
self-righteousness! Lay aside this proud affectation of
“not being so very bad,” in which you are trying to wrap
yourself up. Be bold enough to confess the truth. Listen
not to that old liar, the devil. Surely in the face of that
damuing sum which I have just cast up, you will not dare
to deny that “you have many sins.”

I leave this part of my subject here, and pass on. I
sadly fear that many a reader will run his eye over what
I have been saying, and remain unconvinced and unmoved.
I have learned by mournful experience that the last thing
a man finds out and understands, is his own state in the
sight of God. Well saith the Holy Ghost, that we are all
by nature “ blind,” and “ deaf,” and “dumb,” and “asleep,” -
and “beside ourselves,” and “dead!” Nothing, nothing
will ever convince man of sin but the power of the Holy
Ghost. Show him hell, and he will not flee from it; show
him heaven, and he will not seek it; silence him with
warnings, and yet he will not stir; prick his conscience,
