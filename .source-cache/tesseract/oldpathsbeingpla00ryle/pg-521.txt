T entreat you to observe how important it is for Christians

I fear the increasing tendency to regard all doctrinal
questions as matters of opinion, and to look on all “ earnest-
minded” men as right, whatever doctrines they maintain.
I warn you that the sure result of giving way to this
tendency will be a vague, low, misty theology,—a theology
containing no positive hope, no positive motive, and no
positive consolation,—a theology which will fail most, just
when it is most wanted, in the day of affliction, the hour
of sickness, and on the bed of death.
