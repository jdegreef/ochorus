Isay this boldly and confidently. Iam aware of the

But I know well what Tam saying. I am bold to say that

forgiven and cleansed away.

Let us remember there is a God above us. We see
Him not in the city. Hurry and bustle, trade and com-
merce, appear to swallow up men’s minds, We see Him
not in the country. Farming and labouring go on in
regular course, and seed time and harvest never fail, But
all this time there is an eternal Eye looking down from
heaven and seeing all that men do: an eye that never
slumbers, and never slecps. Yes! there is not only a
Queen, and a government, and a landlord, and a master,
and an employer, to be remembered. There is One higher,
far higher than all these, who expects His dues to be paid,
That One is the most high God.
