I. First of all let me explain what it is to be saved.

This is a matter that must be cleared up. ‘Till we

To be saved, is not merely to profess and call ourselves
Christians. We may have all the outward parts of
Christianity, and yet be lost after all. We may be
baptized into Christ’s Church,—go to Christ’s table—
have Christian knowledge,—be reckoned Christian men
and women—and yet be dead souls all our lives,—and at
last, in the judgment day, be found on Christ’s left hand,
among the goats. No: this is not salvation! Salvation
is something far higher and deeper than this. Now what
