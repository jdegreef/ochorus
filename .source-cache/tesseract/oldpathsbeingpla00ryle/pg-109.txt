on as He foretold 1800 years ago. Many are being lost
and few saved.

(f) Are there few saved? Then, if you are one, do
not be afraid of having too much religion. Settle it
down in your inind that you will aim at the highest degrce
of holiness, and spiritual-mindedness, and consecration to
God,—that you wil not be content with any low degree of
sanctification. Resolve that, by the grace of God, you will
make Christianity beautiful in the eyes of the world.
Remember that the children of the world have but few
patterns of true religion before them. Endeavour, as far
as in you lics, to make those few patterns recommend the
service of your Master. Oh, that every true Christian
would recollect that he is set as a lighthouse in the midst
of a dark world, and would labour so to live that every
part of him may reflect light, and no side be dim!
