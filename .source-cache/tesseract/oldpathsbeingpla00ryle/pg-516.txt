I leave these people alone. Iam sorry for them. There
are sadly too many of them in the Churches of Christ
just now. I can only wish them better spiritual health,
and less narrowness of views, and a quicker growth in
spiritual knowledge. The persons I have in my mind’s
eye in this part of my paper are of a different class, and
to them I now address myself.
