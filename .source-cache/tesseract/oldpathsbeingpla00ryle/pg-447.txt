I shall never forget the effect produeed upon my own
mind when I read some years ago of that fearful shipwreek,
the loss of the Central America, a great steamer which
was lost on the voyage from Havannah to New York.
That steamer was bringing home from California three or
four hundred gold-diggers. They had all got their gold,
and were coming home, proposing to spend their latter
days in ease in their own country. But man proposes and
God disposes.
