But, I think it just possible that some reader of this
volume is afraid to repent. You think you are so bad
and unworthy that Christ will not have you. I do beseech
you once more, to cast away such fear for ever. Never,
never be afraid to repent. The Lord Jesus Christ is very,
gracious. He will not break the bruised reed, nor quench
the smoking flax, Fear not to draw near to Him.—There
