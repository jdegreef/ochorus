An error about repentance is an errcr that lies at the very

can it be said of any man, that he repents ?

Repentance is a thorough change of man’s natural heart
upon the subject of sin, We are all born in sin. We
naturally love sin. We take to sin, as soon as we can act
and think, as the bird takes to flying, and the fish takes
to swimming. There never was a child that required
schoolg or education in order to learn deceitfulness,
sensuality, passion, self-will, gluttony, pride, and foolish-
ness. These things are not picked up from bad companions,
or gradually learned by a long course of tedious instruction.
