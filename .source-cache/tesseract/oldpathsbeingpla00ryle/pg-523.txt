The grand characteristic of the Gospel is, that it is
glad tidings. It is a message of peace to a rebellious
world, It is good news from a far conntry, alike unex-
pected and undeserved. It is the glad tidings that there
isa hope for us, lost, ruined, and bankrupt as we are by

that it is built on the atoning death and gracious media-

is an actual living person, Jesus the Son of God; able to

able. It is the glad tidings that the way to pardon and
peace by this Saviour is the simplest possible. It is not a
thing high in heaven, that we cannot reach, or decp in the
