J may not tarry longer on this branch of my subject. I
trust I have said enough to prove that I did not use words
without meaning, when I spoke of the importance attached
in Scripture to the work of the Spirit of God.

Before I pass on let me entreat all who read this paper
to make sure that they hold sound doctrine concerning
the work of the Holy Ghost.* Give Him the honour due
unto His name. Give Him in your religion the place and
the dignity which Scripture assigns to Him. Settle it in
your minds that the work of all three Persons in the
blessed Trinity, is absolutely and equally needful to the
salvation of every saved soul. The election of God the
Father, and the atoning blood of God the Son, are the
foundation stones of our faith. But from them must .
never be separated the applicatory work of God the Holy
Ghost. The Father chooses. The Son mediates, absolves,
justifies, and intercedes. The Holy Ghost applies the
whole work to man’s soul. Always together in Scripture,
