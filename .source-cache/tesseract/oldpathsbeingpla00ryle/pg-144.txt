See, too, what reason ministers have to be anxious about
their congregations. We feel that time is short, and lise
uncertain. We know that death spiritual is the high

strongly and plead with you warmly! We dare not give

I. Let me tell you, in the second place, what every
man needs who would be savel—He must be quickened
and made spiritually alive.

Life is the mightiest of all possessions. From death to
life is the mightiest of all changes. And no change short
of this will ever avail to fit man’s soul for heaven.

Yes! it is not a little mending and alteration,—a little
cleansing and purifying,—a little pamting and patching,—
a little whitewashing and varnishing,—a little turning over
a new leaf and putting on a new out-side that is wanted.
It is the bringing in of something altogether new,—the
planting within us of a new nature,—a new being—a new
