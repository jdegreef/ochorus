3. All who have the Spirit are led by Him to the
Scriptures. This is the instrument by which He specially
works on the soul. The Word is called “the sword of
the Spirit.” Those who are born again are said to be
“born by the Word.” (Eph. vi. 17; 1 Peter i123.) All
Scripture was written under His inspiration: He never
teaches anything which is not therein written. He causes
the man in whom He dwells to “delight in the law of the
Lord.” (Psalm i. 2.) Just as the infant desires the milk
which nature las provided for it, and refuses all other
food, so does the soul which has the Spirit desire the sincere
milk of the Word. Just as the Israelites fed on the
manna in the wilderness, so are the children of God taught
by the Holy Ghost to feed on the contents of the Bible.
