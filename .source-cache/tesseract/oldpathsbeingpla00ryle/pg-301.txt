(2) Where the Holy Ghost is, there will always be lively
faith in Jesus Christ, as the only Saviour. It is His
special office to testify of Christ, to take of the things of
Christ and show them to man, (John xvi. 15.) He leads the
soul which feels its sin, to Jesus and the atonement made
by His blood. He shows the soul that Christ has suffered
for sin, the just for the unjust, to bring us to God. He
points out to the sin-sick soul that we have only to receive
Christ, believe in Christ, commit ourselves to Christ, and
pardon, peace, and life eternal, are at once our own. He
makes us see a beautifui fitness in Christ’s finished work
of redemption to meet our spiritual necessities. He makes
us willing to disclaim all merit of our own and to venture
all on Jesus, looking to nothing, resting on nothing, trust-
ing in nothing but Christ,—Christ—Christ,—“ delivered
for our offences, and raised again for our justification.”
(Rom. iv. 25.) He that knows nothing of all this, and
builds on any other foundation, is dead before God. He
has not the Spirit of Christ.
