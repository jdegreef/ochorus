I make this statement boldly and confidently. Startling
as it sounds to the natural heart, I lay it down as a
piece of undeniable Scriptural truth. In spite of all the
Pharisees, and Roman Catholics, and Socinians, and Deists,
and idolaters of human reason and human power, I unhesi-
tatingly repeat my assertion—Man’s sins are many and
great. It is of the utmost importance that these sins
should be cleansed away. Man’s guilt in the sight of
God, is enormous. Man’s danger of hell, after he dies, is
imminent and tremendous, And yet man cannot cleanse
away his own sins! It is written, and it is true, “ By the
deeds of the law shall no flesh be justified.” (Rom. iii. 20.)
