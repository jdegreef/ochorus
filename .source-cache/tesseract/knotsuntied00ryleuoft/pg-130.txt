IJ. Let me show, in the second place, the necessity there is for
our being regenerate, or born again.

That there is such a necessity is most plain from our Lord
Jesus Christ’s words in the third chapter of St. John’s Gospel.
Nothing can be more clear and positive than His language to \*
Nicodemus: “Except a man be born again, he cannot see the +
kingdom of God.” “ Marvel not that I say unto thee, Ye must
be born again.” (John iii. 3, 7.)
